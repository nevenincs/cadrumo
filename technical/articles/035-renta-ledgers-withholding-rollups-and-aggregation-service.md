## Renta income projections

The income ledger shares a typed classifier but exposes separate quarterly, annual and activity-narrowed entry points. Quarterly income folds active incoming receipts that qualify by an activity marker or business/mixed classification over a cumulative year-to-date window. Modelo 100 folds the annual period, and Modelo 131 accepts only its selected agrarian activity codes and income concepts permitted by its projection. The repository-backed quarterly and annual paths require transaction and invoice read capabilities bound to the same bucket; date-partitioned transaction reads classify only rows inside the filing window and return an out-of-window summary. The Modelo 131 path retains eligible income excluded by the activity selector as a separate measured result, rather than making its target's zero indistinguishable from a genuinely empty quarter. Quarterly repository projection (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/renta_income_ledger.py`) Annual projection (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/renta_income_ledger.py`) Agrarian narrowing and excluded-income measure (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/renta_income_ledger.py`)

An admitted receipt carries both bank gross and, when known, an IVA-exclusive taxable base. A linked sales invoice supplies base, quota and withholding evidence; transaction tax base is the fallback. When neither base is declared, the bank amount still contributes to income, while its explicit CASH_FALLBACK grounding allows the calculate layer to warn that cash is not the same as gross invoice income. Withholding derivation is likewise tagged so a true zero, an inferred amount and a refused inference cannot be conflated. Unusable linked-invoice evidence is summarized in bounded diagnostics with transaction identifiers fitted to the message limit. Income observation construction (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/renta_income_ledger.py`) Invoice-evidence diagnostics (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/renta_income_ledger.py`)

The classifier excludes outgoing rows from this path, keeps operator-reviewed exclusions out, rejects payroll income from activity targets, and emits reason-coded issues for unsupported currency, personal or unclassified rows, and dates outside the selected window. Activity-marked receipts use the selected matcher as the eligibility signal; otherwise a business share is applied consistently to gross, base and derived withholding. The typed observation validates that its grounding marker agrees with the presence of a base and that withholding markers agree with the amount. Income classification and gates (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/renta_income_ledger.py`) Observation invariants (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/renta_income_ledger.py`)

## Annual Renta deductible expenses

The annual first-slice expense pipeline resolves dated category and IVA-ratio policy through a pinned authority operation. Profile facts supply the IVA regime and ordinary residence; the prorrata register supplies the provisional deduction ratio where applicable. If no ratio resolves, the function returns `None` and preserves the documented base-only behavior rather than fabricating a percentage. Residence is fail-closed to the state profile when absent or not parseable. Registry-driven IVA deduction ratio (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/renta_ledger.py`) Repository aggregation and profile inputs (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/renta_ledger.py`)

For each eligible expense, the ledger validates direction and EUR conversion, business share, category taxonomy, year profile, and any territorial override. Annual filing refuses a mere activity-category marker until the business classification has been reviewed, even where the quarterly M130 path accepts that marker. Linked purchase evidence must belong to the active bucket, be a received invoice, link reciprocally to exactly one transaction, and match the transaction's EUR grand total. The invoice's EUR base and IVA then enrich the typed expense fact; when no purchase-invoice evidence identifier is attached, the transaction's own tax substrate remains available to the deductibility evaluator. Outside-period, missing or unknown category, evidence mismatch, and category-profile failures remain explicit issues. Deductibility classifier (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/renta_ledger.py`) Purchase-invoice link constraints (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/renta_ledger.py`) EUR amount reconciliation (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/renta_ledger.py`)

One representational gap merits a scoped follow-up: `Modelo180Type2Row.sign` exposes a reimbursement value for negative taxable bases, but `RetencionObservation` requires both taxable base and withholding amount to be nonnegative. The public observation model therefore cannot supply a reimbursement to the type-2 grouping path. Confirm whether Modelo 180 reimbursements are intentionally unsupported here; otherwise the input and total models need a signed representation. Observation amount constraints (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/retenciones.py`) Modelo 180 sign field and materializer (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/retenciones.py`)

## Withholding observations and service boundary

The withholding primitives serve Modelos 111, 115, 123, 180, 190 and 193. They normalize perceptor tax identity, require a canonical source kind, parse accrued dates, and use strict typed evidence for annual property and pending-capital details. Dated registry facts select each model's permitted schemes; aggregators then produce stable perceptor/scheme rollups and validate row and total reconciliation. Modelo 180 materialization groups property evidence and chooses the latest accrued withholding percentage, refusing conflicting recipient/property details or an ambiguous latest percentage. The repository ports store opaque tax-ID-derived keys and expose separate annual and through-year reads for cross-period materialization. Typed observation contract (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/retenciones.py`) Registry scheme selection (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/retenciones.py`) Modelo 180 annual materialization (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/retenciones.py`)

The central service is an operator-facing aggregation/reporting route, distinct from the calculation source mesh. It identifies retenciones, counterpart or foreign-assets ownership from selected registry revisions, rejects observations belonging to a different provider, and returns a typed result envelope whose model, period and provider must match its payload. Logs contain stable counts and model/provider identifiers rather than observation contents. A separate withholding-row loader preserves the difference between a revision not reading a store (`None`) and reading it with no rows (an absent source family). Provider discovery and supported-model refusal (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/service.py`) Typed command and result contract (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/service.py`) Aggregation dispatch (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/service.py`) Withholding source availability (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/service.py`) Calculation-row loading (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/service.py`)

The service receives a pinned operation for revision/provider selection and passes it into counterpart aggregation. Its retenciones helper delegates through the canonical resolver without an operation argument; trace the resolver's dispatch to verify that a custom or retained authority generation cannot differ between provider selection and scheme aggregation. This is a generation-continuity question, not evidence of a reachable filing error in this chunk. Retenciones delegation (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/service.py`)

## Security and implementation assessment

Typed immutable observations, registry-selected scheme/category policy, reciprocal invoice linking, exact bucket checks, bounded diagnostics, hash-derived storage keys and date-partitioned reads are meaningful controls. The annual property detail can contain addresses and cadastral references, and withholding observations contain recipient identity and amounts; the encrypted storage adapter and caller access controls are outside these files. The ports declare atomic window replacement, but their concrete transaction semantics also live in the bound persistence adapters. No tests are included in this chunk and none were run. Cross-module verification should trace source-mesh callers, invoice-evidence adapters, prorrata/profile repositories, the retenciones dispatcher and concrete capture stores. Authority declarations are product data, not external legal validation.
