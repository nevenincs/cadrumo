This layer turns profile facts, bank transactions, invoices, registers, prior declarations, and operator evidence into typed source observations for a selected Modelo revision. A source resolver receives a bucket, filing period, revision, and caller-held authority operation, and returns values, rows, diagnostics, and provenance through separate channels. ## Transaction and invoice evidence

The IVA classifier admits transactions through dated revision facts, filing-period and direction gates, business share, measured base/quota/rate, category, cash-accounting, and deduction identity. Valid ordinary rows become settlement observations; cash-accounting rows split by payment evidence and retain a later fallback date for unpaid remainder. The repository-backed path checks bucket scope, reads the relevant date partition and named investment acquisition rows, then applies general, special, or sectorized prorrata to deductible input quota while leaving bases and output quota unchanged. Readiness reuses admission gates, though it does not calculate values. Invoice IVA screening converts amounts to EUR, preserves base-only lines, checks category/counterparty and deduction links, and compares invoice-derived output to ledger bindings. A missing or unreadable invoice catalogue is a degraded source, not evidence of no invoices. IVA admission (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/_iva_transaction.py`) Cash split (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/_iva_transaction.py`) Prorrata bindings (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/iva_ledger.py`) Invoice screen (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/_modelo_bindings_invoice_iva.py`) ([031](../articles/031-aggregation-iva-screening-and-fiscal-evidence.md), [033](../articles/033-iva-ledger-filing-drift-and-withholding-arrivals.md))

Renta sources intentionally differ by period and model. Modelo 130 uses a cumulative quarter-to-date window, Modelo 100 an annual one, and Modelo 131 narrows to selected agrarian categories. An admitted bank receipt can still contribute cash when invoice-base evidence fails, with a `CASH_FALLBACK` marker and advisory rather than pretending cash equals gross invoice income. Annual deductible-expense rules require reviewed business classification where quarterly rules may accept an activity marker, and reciprocal purchase-invoice linkage is checked before the invoice's base and IVA enrich the expense. Activity-asset acquisitions and depreciation claims have their own ownership route to avoid ordinary-expense double counting. Modelo 210 requires a durable official income-code classification; impatriate source jurisdiction and category come from governed facts. These are calculation source routes, not official export or submission capabilities. ## Withholding and cross-period state

Withholding capture derives recognition from payment or exigibility evidence, routes supported work, professional, urban-rent, and ordinary movable-capital items to 111/115/123, then stores per-perceptor observations through an append/replace/clear service. Replay requires an idempotency key with the same canonical command; replacements require the current window baseline. Annual 190 and 193 are assembled from periodic allocations, with 193 pending-payment rows using a generic recipient and later settlements carrying an explicit unresolved payment-year authority advisory. The path for ordinary capital into Modelo 123 explicitly refuses a calculable window until its annual rent-count definition is supported. A concrete repository must honor the atomic capture contract; this layer does not prove storage transactions. Recognition (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/withholding_recognition.py`) Mutation (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/withholding_observation_service.py`) Annual source (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/withholding_source.py`) Pending phase (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/m193_phase_materialization.py`) ([033](../articles/033-iva-ledger-filing-drift-and-withholding-arrivals.md), [035](../articles/035-renta-ledgers-withholding-rollups-and-aggregation-service.md), [036](../articles/036-source-resolution-contracts-and-withholding-capture.md))

Prior-filing and relation prefills read only revision-reconfirmed observations from the pinned authority. Missing carries remain visible as missing values. The separate clean-state evaluator determines whether the history is filing grade: it checks member rosters, current filing/revision state, verification, local and AEAT evidence, and tightly scoped no-obligation exceptions. Same-year app filing can relax only certain missing-official-evidence blockers to advisory, not missing values or revision divergence. M303 IVA compensation has a canonical disposition-aware carry envelope, a FIFO history, and an atomic co-commit contract for observation plus history. Wallet reconciliation distinguishes local recurrence, live AEAT evidence, and explicit operator override; its first-period zero depends on caller-supplied evidence status. Annual M390 consumes exact filed M303 history and can leave an unknown opening balance unresolved. ## Assessment

Strong controls are source-specific typed refusals, exact bucket and revision checks, dated authority selection, separately marked missing and empty states, provenance-bearing folds, and later filing-grade gates. Financial and tax-identity evidence still traverses this layer and needs outer encrypted storage and disclosure controls. Several local findings deserve focused verification. The invoice silence guard compares raw foreign-currency withholding quota with EUR ledger values in one branch; a multi-transaction linked-authority helper can ignore incomplete linked entries. The filing snapshot fingerprints omit IVA inputs such as operation date, cash payments, and VAT-identification state, so a changed calculation-relevant fact may escape its drift comparison. M303 transition and withholding source helpers reopen default authority despite a pinned operation; impact depends on reachable historical generations. Row-set reconstruction truncates fractional integer cells and defaults malformed dates to year end unless upstream admission forbids them. Currency branch (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/_modelo_bindings_invoice_iva_refusal.py`) Snapshot fields (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/ledger_filing_snapshot.py`) Transition generation (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/m303_arrivals.py`) Row coercion (`src/cadrumo/application/calculations/row_set_assembly.py`) ([031](../articles/031-aggregation-iva-screening-and-fiscal-evidence.md), [033](../articles/033-iva-ledger-filing-drift-and-withholding-arrivals.md), [036](../articles/036-source-resolution-contracts-and-withholding-capture.md), [043](../articles/043-prorrata-settlement-relation-prefill-and-row-set-reconstruction.md))

These are static code observations and conditional integration risks. No calculation was executed and the bundled authority material was not checked against current law. Future checks should establish producer and adapter invariants, reproduce the named currency and snapshot cases, and verify that every filed value passes the clean-state and verification boundaries rather than relying on a preview alone.
