<!-- preserved:summary -->
The domain holds taxpayer/profile facts, family and business records, invoices, bank transactions, IVA classifications, deadlines, filing revisions and evidence. Pinned registry facts supply dated vocabularies and parameters; strict records preserve provenance, canonical identity and incomplete states. This is the substrate for local calculations and draft construction, not a live tax authority. The [full topic](business-and-taxpayer-domain.md) traces each family ([profile](../articles/153-usage-ratios-and-user-profile-domain.md), [transactions](../articles/151-transaction-ledger-domain-and-classifier-contracts.md), [invoices](../articles/142-filing-drafts-and-invoice-records.md)).

Family rules retain dates, relationship, income, work months and nursery spend rather than reducing eligibility to age. Inventory supports evidence-backed FIFO/weighted-average valuation and an explicit closing decision. IVA classification separates establishment, VAT identification and operation date, and refuses ambiguous rate or missing agricultural code authority. Expense and asset modules keep deduction and depreciation claims tied to declared evidence and selected methods (family (`src/cadrumo/domain/contribuyente/family_profile.py`), inventory (`src/cadrumo/domain/contribuyente/inventory/valuation.py`), IVA (`src/cadrumo/domain/iva/classification.py`), [rates report](../articles/144-iva-rates-settlement-and-statutory-projections.md)).

Security/integrity controls include immutable content digests, typed evidence, pinned authority, checksum-validated account identifiers, distinct local versus confirmed filing records, and optional LLM outputs constrained to classification suggestions. Privacy permission for sending transaction or invoice text to a model is caller-owned. An attachment blob can be written before its manifest; actor strings do not authenticate; the portable profile bundle holds decrypted payload bytes pending archive protection. Portal metadata and submission preflight do not execute or establish AEAT filing (attachment (`src/cadrumo/domain/attachments/service.py`), LLM (`src/cadrumo/domain/transactions/llm.py`), filing event (`src/cadrumo/domain/modelos/filing_record.py`), [preflight report](../articles/150-local-submission-preflight-and-audit-records.md)).

The most consequential scoped quality findings are permissive stored family booleans/incomplete-row hydration, unverified transaction FX multiplication, a missing expense-result identity/year join, and IVA compensation lots that omit unused opening credit. Their end-to-end effect depends on upstream validation and downstream consumers. A parsed justificante carries a CSV and digest but cannot itself prove AEAT confirmation; receipt verification remains separate. No tests or external legal checks were performed (stored family facts (`src/cadrumo/domain/contribuyente/descendant_facts.py`), FX (`src/cadrumo/domain/transactions/models.py`), expense join (`src/cadrumo/domain/renta/ledger_expenses.py`), opening credit (`src/cadrumo/domain/iva_compensation/carry_forward.py`), [receipt report](../articles/145-iva-compensation-carry-forward-and-filing-receipts.md)).

## Taxpayer facts and local records

This domain models the taxpayer, business evidence and local filing lifecycle that feed registry-backed calculations. It is deliberately richer than a flat profile: typed records distinguish raw bank movement from tax classification, invoice from cash transaction, operator-entered family facts from derived deductions, acquired inventory from year-end closing authority, a calculation revision from a filing event, and a parsed receipt from independently confirmed AEAT status. The shared identifiers and periods preserve canonical Modelo codes, filing coordinates and evidence references without claiming that a syntactically valid ID belongs to a supported revision (identifiers (`src/cadrumo/domain/identifiers.py`), period helpers (`src/cadrumo/domain/period.py`), [domain base report](../articles/123-domain-evidence-filing-periods-and-capital-goods-registers.md)).

The profile schema and values layer declares field types, sensitivity, effective dating, provenance and derived-selector ownership, then constructs revisioned live records and immutable filing snapshots under an explicit pinned schema context. Canonical digests detect changed content; snapshot hashes are rechecked on load. Schema 6-to-7 migration clears only legacy false payer facts and leaves true answers intact. A registry-contract walk checks whether model bindings and schedule selectors are grounded in declared profile paths. The portable-export model can carry decrypted secure-object payloads and coverage metadata, so confidentiality depends on the archive workflow rather than this in-memory type. Usage ratios are separate operator choices, with a per-bucket lock and category/year checks; they are not legal IVA prorrata (profile records (`src/cadrumo/domain/user_profile/values.py`), registry grounding (`src/cadrumo/domain/user_profile/registry_contract.py`), usage ratios (`src/cadrumo/domain/usage_ratios/model.py`), [profile report](../articles/153-usage-ratios-and-user-profile-domain.md)).

Family records retain descendant birth/death, relationship, disability, cohabitation/dependency, custody, income, work months and childcare spend, allowing distinct state minimum, maternity and regional-deduction predicates. Their thresholds and vocabularies come from dated governed facts; the record is not itself current-law authority. Canonical month/range parsers and strict CLI inputs preserve detail needed for turning-three and maternity windows. At the stored-fact hydration boundary, however, noncanonical booleans can be interpreted as true and incomplete indexed rows can be dropped without count reconciliation. Those are concrete local robustness concerns whose product reach depends on profile-store admission. The census-certificate projector likewise emits candidate address/activity facts explicitly marked as operator-supplied unverified evidence, not an AEAT census read (family aggregation (`src/cadrumo/domain/contribuyente/family_profile.py`), stored booleans (`src/cadrumo/domain/contribuyente/descendant_facts.py`), certificate projection (`src/cadrumo/domain/censo/certificado.py`), [family report](../articles/139-taxpayer-and-family-domain-records.md), [category/census report](../articles/138-spending-categories-proportionality-and-census-projections.md)).

## Accounting, classification and calculation substrate

Transactions preserve provider provenance, amount, direction, dates, currency, narrative and a separate tax substrate with jurisdiction, IVA, IRPF, withholding, invoice and evidence axes. Gross and cash-accounting validators constrain compatible amounts and eligible dates, while incomplete classification remains distinguishable from zero. Optional LLM classification is a proposal surface: prompts use closed allow-lists and ask for categories rather than euro amounts or statutory rates, but narratives and invoice text may be sent to the chosen provider only under caller policy. The split response parser permits missing category/citation fields and a small proportion residual, so review must close those gaps before financial allocation. The transaction model checks FX field co-presence but does not rederive EUR value from native amount times rate; importer/application checks determine reachability. Pure mutation helpers append classification history and return new catalogues, although whitespace normalization can create no-op history entries and their returned rows retain an old modification timestamp (transaction model (`src/cadrumo/domain/transactions/models.py`), LLM parser (`src/cadrumo/domain/transactions/llm.py`), mutation (`src/cadrumo/domain/transactions/service.py`), [transaction reports](../articles/151-transaction-ledger-domain-and-classifier-contracts.md), [mutation report](../articles/152-transaction-mutations-and-registry-backed-income-predicates.md)).

Invoices are independent strict records of document lines, counterparties, legal mentions, payments and FX provenance. Arithmetic and IVA-rate checks reconcile line and document totals. Decomposition produces either complete euro components or named defects, leaving a captured but incomplete invoice visible for review rather than inventing tax zeroes. Reconciliation suggestions use amount/direction and name overlap; they do not write links. A historical-date issue remains conditional: some string token and simplified-invoice checks consult today's registry rather than the invoice's operation date. The invoice identity hash also excludes its separate series, so the caller's invoice-number convention matters. Filing drafts similarly carry value origins, formula traces, approval basis and status; a validator can report schema, box, formula and optional deadline findings, but the draft's content ID is finally checked at persistence (invoice validation (`src/cadrumo/domain/invoices/models.py`), decomposition (`src/cadrumo/domain/invoices/decomposition.py`), draft validator (`src/cadrumo/domain/filing/validator.py`), [invoice/draft report](../articles/142-filing-drafts-and-invoice-records.md)).

The IVA classifier uses transaction date and one pinned authority to evaluate ordered registry predicates against party establishment, VAT identification, customer status, operation kind and rate. Country and VAT-ID resolution are intentionally separate: unknown country does not become third-country treatment, and Spain needs subnational evidence rather than an automatic mainland inference. Dated rate lookup distinguishes standing and temporary coexisting rates, refuses ambiguous gross splitting, and keeps absent/contradictory legal evidence explicit. The invoice-to-IVA bridge uses issue date for rate lookup, so callers must check whether it should instead pass the operation/devengo date. Deduction evidence, quota flow, prorrata percentage, recargo and OSS/IOSS projections are typed and source-linked, while statutory values remain registry-owned. Agricultural simplified-regime filing rows are explicitly refused until an official two-digit activity-code crosswalk is available (IVA classifier (`src/cadrumo/domain/iva/classification.py`), territory resolver (`src/cadrumo/domain/iva/establishment.py`), rate ambiguity (`src/cadrumo/domain/iva/saturation.py`), agricultural refusal (`src/cadrumo/domain/iva/regimen_simplificado_rows.py`), [IVA reports](../articles/143-iva-classification-and-evidence-substrate.md), [rate report](../articles/144-iva-rates-settlement-and-statutory-projections.md)).

Other business calculations retain their own authority and evidence boundaries. An inventory ledger supports FIFO and weighted-average valuation, refuses LIFO and stock over-consumption, requires acquisition-cost decomposition and evidence, and preserves disagreements between movement-derived and physical year-end closing before Anexo D projection. Renta expense rules select dated category proportionality and route eligible observations to selected Modelo 100 boxes; activity-asset schedules distinguish ownership, business-use allocation and claimed depreciation, with explicit refusals for missing history or unsupported methods. A local observation-builder gap permits a result from another transaction/invoice or year to be paired with a fact when categories match, pending caller checks. Currency normalization reports `MISSING_RATE` alongside numeric zero, so consumers must check status rather than use zero as an exchange result (inventory valuation (`src/cadrumo/domain/contribuyente/inventory/valuation.py`), asset claims (`src/cadrumo/domain/renta/actividad_asset/claims.py`), expense pairing (`src/cadrumo/domain/renta/ledger_expenses.py`), currency normalization (`src/cadrumo/domain/currency/service.py`), [inventory report](../articles/140-inventory-valuation-and-taxpayer-profile-mechanics.md), [Renta report](../articles/149-renta-expense-rules-and-activity-asset-amortization.md)).

## Obligations, evidence and filing status

Deadline scheduling combines registry windows, profile predicates, activity dates, holiday calendars and recargo bands under a pinned authority. It distinguishes ordinary static-profile obligations from result-qualified windows and reports national-only or unverified regional holiday coverage rather than claiming a fully checked deadline. Missing next-year publication can refuse a business-day walk. A wide taxpayer profile includes separate refund and debit IBANs with checksum validation. Malformed stored setup booleans and multiple-payer counts can be coerced to negative/absent values in local projection, so profile-store guarantees and advisory routing matter (schedule (`src/cadrumo/domain/deadlines/engine.py`), holiday coverage (`src/cadrumo/domain/deadlines/festivos.py`), profile projection (`src/cadrumo/domain/deadlines/profiles.py`), [deadline report](../articles/141-deadline-currency-and-evidence-consent-domain.md)).

Evidence custody uses content-addressed attachment manifests and a strict M303 attestation shape; blob persistence occurs before manifest write and needs adapter-level cleanup or atomicity. Bucket events and calculation revisions retain digests, actor labels, provenance and secure-serialization controls, but string actor labels do not authenticate a human and free-form event payloads need producer-side secrecy discipline. A calculation revision can be draft, verified, filed, superseded or discarded; a separate filing record distinguishes local and externally confirmed events. Verification reports and ledger fingerprints explain which facts contributed and whether they changed. The old-snapshot diff helper's `covers_current_fact_set` default can overstate coverage unless a caller corrects it. Participation indexes are rebuildable caches, not the authoritative revision catalogue (attachment ingest (`src/cadrumo/domain/attachments/service.py`), calculation revision (`src/cadrumo/domain/modelos/calculation_revision.py`), filing event (`src/cadrumo/domain/modelos/filing_record.py`), ledger diff (`src/cadrumo/domain/modelos/ledger_filing_snapshot.py`), [lifecycle reports](../articles/146-modelo-calculation-evidence-and-filing-lifecycle.md), [evidence report](../articles/147-ledger-evidence-detail-rows-and-modelo-verification.md)).

IVA compensation carries filed-period observations into FIFO credit lots and reconciles local recurrence, an AEAT wallet observation and a documented override. The model keeps missing, stale and conflicting evidence separate. Its carry-forward projection does not include unused opening credit in returned lots/balance, a material local undercount if a caller treats that result as the entire wallet; upstream consumers and any independent opening-balance display need tracing. A parsed justificante stores printed CSV, target, amount, PDF digest and URL, but does not verify the CSV or URL against AEAT. A portal catalogue supplies HTTPS destinations and auth-method metadata only, while a prorrata register stores taxpayer-entered year/sector evidence and provisional-percentage precedence; neither performs remote filing (credit lots (`src/cadrumo/domain/iva_compensation/carry_forward.py`), receipt boundary (`src/cadrumo/domain/justificante/schema.py`), portal metadata (`src/cadrumo/domain/portals/metadata.py`), prorrata register (`src/cadrumo/domain/prorrata_register/register.py`), [carry report](../articles/145-iva-compensation-carry-forward-and-filing-receipts.md), [portal/register report](../articles/148-portal-catalogue-and-annual-prorrata-register.md)).

Submission-domain preflight checks a locally approved draft, blocking findings, filing window and auth-provider availability, with caller-controlled skips documented for local-only workflows. It does not execute transport; a separate live-write gate and application policy must refuse unauthorized AEAT writes. Historical attempt records can represent imported audit history, but only a parsed record or local mark cannot confirm acceptance: an independently verified AEAT receipt or equivalent external evidence is required. This packet made no live request, test execution or current-law check (preflight (`src/cadrumo/domain/submission/preflight.py`), submission records (`src/cadrumo/domain/submission/models.py`), [submission report](../articles/150-local-submission-preflight-and-audit-records.md)).
