## Capabilities and mechanisms

The package compiles one validated registry snapshot into a renderer-neutral `SheetExportPlan` shared by Google Sheets, offline XLSX, pull, and parity paths. Its deterministic layout maps input and informational casillas to `Entradas`, computed casillas to `Cálculos`, referenced parameters and relations to `Tarifas`, row-producing bindings to `Detalle`, plus provenance, evidence, and guide facets. Workbook metadata binds the Modelo, revision, filing period, engine version, registry digest, and export time. Cell addresses are structured tab/row/column records with their A1 spelling checked at construction; the closed plan rejects duplicate value/formula write coordinates. Layout planner (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/layout.py`), plan builder (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/engine.py`), strict export plan (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/records.py`)

Formula translation walks the registry expression tree and emits A1 formulas for arithmetic, min/max/clamp, comparisons, conditional selection, parameter and bracket lookups, enum/CCAA dispatch, and age-at-year-end. The engine applies each formula’s declared rounding separately, including ceiling-to-integer rather than nearest-integer rounding. Bracket lookup uses `INDEX`/`MATCH` over the filing-date-active, lower-bound-sorted tariff rows; dispatch keys are quoted safely and missing keys retain the runtime’s `#N/A` failure shape. `is_translatable` probes the real compiler rather than maintaining a second capability list. Unsupported expressions refuse with a typed translation error. Formula translation and probe (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/_translator.py`), rounding and registry digest (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/engine.py`), date-anchored layout and active tariff selection (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/layout.py`)

Before layout, the engine repeatedly excludes only `internal_only` computed casillas whose formulas cannot be represented, rebuilding the layout until the exclusions reach a fixpoint. This prevents formulas from retaining cell references to support casillas removed in an earlier pass, while leaving translatable internal values and official casillas intact. The plan then carries input seeds, formula cells, tariff values, legal/source provenance, styling and number formats, anchors, cell constraints, protection ranges, row-set headers, relation provenance, evidence, and guidance. Relation values are caller-resolved or explicitly supplied; registry requirements supply their legal/source grounding. If neither values nor resolver are given, relation cells remain blank for operator completion. Fixpoint exclusion (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/engine.py`), plan assembly (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/engine.py`), relation provenance record (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/records.py`)

The plan distinguishes input, computed, tariff, provenance, evidence, and guide surfaces. It emits protected ranges for calculated results, provenance, tariffs, guide, and evidence; input and repeating `Detalle` areas remain operator-editable. Declarative cell constraints retain sign and numeric bounds plus legal references, while shared styling records and the theme keep both renderers aligned. Workbook-cell helpers convert plan facets into a canonical addressed write stream so the transports do not reimplement address arithmetic. Protection and cell constraints in the engine (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/engine.py`), presentation plan (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/_styling.py`), shared cell stream (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/workbook_cells.py`)

Ledger evidence is projected into casilla-oriented contributor rows only when the caller supplies transaction-to-casilla attribution; missing attribution raises rather than guessing Modelo-specific tax meaning. Manual facts are carried separately. The same export identity stamps and evidence tables feed both renderers. Cross-revision relation stamps preserve value, provenance tier, source coordinates, references, and resolution time, omitting empty fields so absent evidence is not asserted as an empty fact. Evidence attribution gate (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/evidence.py`), shared tables and identity stamps (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/export_tables.py`)

The export service applies an already-built plan through an injected Google adapter, then persists one sync-run record in the active bucket. It records success or a failed write attempt and re-raises adapter failures. Sharing the built plan lets a CLI preview describe exactly what the write would materialize; the export wrapper itself performs the remote side effect and records provenance. Export and sync-run recording (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/export_service.py`)

The parity harness compares three paths against one registry snapshot: local Decimal calculation, the formulas read back from the applied Sheets workbook, and caller-supplied expected AEAT values. It writes a scenario into `Entradas`/`Tarifas`, waits for recalculation, then reads computed cells and returns per-casilla results. Comparisons are intentionally exact on this axis: legal filing tolerances belong to filed-versus-computed reconciliation, not whether Sheets reproduces the engine or whether a preview would overwrite a changed cell. Missing values remain `None`, distinct from mismatches; without any AEAT oracle values, a local/Sheets match is `inconclusive`, not `all_match`. Exact parity comparison (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/_parity_comparison.py`), verdict rules (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/_parity_comparison.py`), write/read parity workflow (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/parity_harness.py`)

## Knowledge, security, and implementation assessment

The export plan’s authoritative inputs are the caller’s pinned `RegistrySnapshot`, optional operator inputs, registry-identified relation requirements, and optional ledger-filing evidence plus explicit attribution. It is not canonical persistence; the package documentation distinguishes operator-facing workbook export from encrypted application storage. Formula provenance and export stamps make the workbook auditable and allow the pull path to reject a registry or engine mismatch. Storage boundary (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/__init__.py`), registry metadata hash (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/engine.py`), cross-transport identity stamps (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/export_tables.py`)

The strongest design choice is the shared plan contract: formula generation, cell layout, provenance, row sets, styling, protection, and evidence are resolved once for multiple transports. Strict frozen records, canonical A1 coordinates, source-attribution refusal, registry-anchored relation metadata, active-date bracket filtering, formula/rounding separation, and explicit unsupported-op errors reduce silent drift. Row-set ingress checks that each submitted binding belongs to its declared grouping and rejects duplicate coordinates or overlapping ownership before delegating to the existing snapshot-bound observation assembler. Row-set ingress checks (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/row_set_assembly.py`), source-grounded relation assembly (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/engine.py`)

The parity outcome has a strict provenance limit: expected figures are supplied by the caller, and this package does not authenticate that they came from AEAT. A partial expected-value map only establishes parity for the casillas it contains; `aeat_oracle_present` means at least one expected value was supplied. Therefore an `all_match` report is evidence for this snapshot and scenario, not whole-model legal validation. Exact comparisons may surface genuine decimal-engine versus Sheets precision differences; the code’s documented remedy is to quantize both sides to the Modelo’s money scale, not to adopt an unrelated legal tolerance. Caller-supplied oracle contract (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/parity_harness.py`), scenario and partial-oracle behavior (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/parity_harness.py`), exactness rationale (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/_parity_comparison.py`)

The export failure path attempts to persist a failed sync record before its bare `raise`; if that persistence call itself fails, its exception would replace the original adapter failure. That co-write failure mode should be checked against the sync-run persistence contract and tested. More broadly, this layer accepts Google credentials and root-folder identity from its caller but does not establish their authorization; composition and outbound adapters own that boundary. The live parity harness also performs remote create/update and cell writes, so it requires an explicitly composed test or user-authorized spreadsheet rather than being safe to invoke as a local unit check. Failure-path persistence (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/export_service.py`), apply capability boundary (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/export_service.py`), parity side effects (`src/cadrumo/application/storage/calc_sheets/parity_harness.py`)

## Dependencies and follow-up

Trace the Google and XLSX materializers to verify they honor the shared cell stream, protection facets, constraints, registry stamps, relation stamps, and evidence table without adding unplanned writes. Follow pull imports through compatibility checks and row-set assembly into observation construction. Prioritize tests for translated versus unsupported registry ops, temporal tariff selection, integer ceiling rounding, exclusion fixpoints, missing contributor attribution, out-of-group row-set cells and overlapping row ownership, failed apply plus failed provenance persistence, and parity scenarios with absent/partial AEAT expectations. No assigned tests were run; the parity harness was inspected but not invoked.
