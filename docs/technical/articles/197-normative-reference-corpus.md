## Scope

This is a static review of the assigned reference-data directory `src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/normatives`: 1,438 files totaling 78,066,788 bytes. The inventory is complete; semantic reading was stratified across the principal formats and legal families within the 24,000-token sample cap. I reviewed the BOE consolidated IVA text and its extraction sidecars, a regional tax article and sidecars, a 2026 BOE derogation resolution, two dated XML amendment records, the 2026 Modelo 390 extraction, and the complete bundled PDF provenance note. I also parsed metadata in all 443 extracted JSON sidecars and checked each declared source digest against its on-disk source. This is not a review of current law, an exhaustive reading of every norm, or a run of the application.

The directory split is 1,326 HTML-directory files (73,526,460 bytes), 16 PDF-directory files (3,556,937 bytes), and 96 XML records (983,391 bytes). By format there are 442 HTML source pages, 443 extracted JSON sidecars, 444 Markdown files, 96 XML files, four PDFs, and nine plain-text form transcriptions. The additional Markdown files are corpus provenance and the PDF extraction sidecar. The HTML source pages have matching JSON/Markdown extractions; filenames identify 271 article-specific excerpts, nine additional provisions named as `da` excerpts, and 162 broader document pages. Those counts describe naming/layout, not legal completeness.

## Product capability and knowledge represented

The corpus supplies documentary reference material across Spanish national tax law, tax administration procedure, regulations, bilateral tax conventions, regional tax provisions and prescribed form layouts. Its filenames are weighted toward ministerial orders, statutes, royal decrees and BOE references: 518 names begin `orden`, 491 `ley`, 163 `rd`, 130 `boe`, 63 `convenio`, 23 `real`, 13 `rdl`, and 12 `rdleg`. Representative full texts include the IVA Law 37/1992, IRPF Law 35/2006, Corporate Tax Law 27/2014, General Tax Law 58/2003, and the IVA and IRPF implementing regulations (Royal Decrees 1624/1992 and 439/2007). Article-level files make selected provisions individually addressable. The sample includes VAT exemptions and exports, the personal minimum in IRPF, and corporate-tax rules on taxable base and related-party transactions. These are source statements, not executed formulas or legal conclusions (Ley 37/1992, art. 20 (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/normatives/html/ley-37-1992-art-20.html.extracted.md`), Ley 37/1992, art. 21 (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/normatives/html/ley-37-1992-art-21.html.extracted.md`), Ley 35/2006, art. 57 (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/normatives/html/ley-35-2006-art-57.html.extracted.md`), Ley 27/2014, arts. 10 and 18 (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/normatives/html/ley-27-2014-art-10.html.extracted.md`)).

The inventory also covers regional differences and treaty text. For example, the Andalucía article excerpt states region-specific personal/family minimum amounts and points back to the common IRPF statute; its extracted Markdown is one of several small, article-scoped records. This supports a potential workflow that needs a region or provision-specific reference, but no consumer code is in this chunk, so the user's actual ability to select region, tax year, or treaty is unverified (Andalucía Law 5/2021, art. 23 bis (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/normatives/html/andalucia-ley-5-2021-art-23-bis.html.extracted.md`)).

HTML originals from BOE preserve publication-page context and navigation, while the parallel `.extracted.json` and `.extracted.md` files provide normalized text units. The JSON records contain schema version, `source_kind`, status, source path and SHA-256, preprocessor name/version, attribution, and the extracted units. All 443 JSON sidecars parsed; all report status `ok`, every source path resolves, and every recorded digest matches the referenced file. The 442 HTML extractions identify `normatives-html` version 1.4; the single PDF extraction identifies `corpus-pdf` version 1.2. The unit counts vary from 1 for a short article to 479 in the sampled consolidated Royal Decree 1175/1990 extraction. These checks establish local extraction-to-source consistency, not the source's legal authority, completeness, or freshness.

The XML branch preserves date-stamped provision/version records rather than only one consolidated view. The sampled December 2025 XML record contains Article 17 of RDL 16/2025 and a provision amending Corporate Tax rules with effects for tax periods beginning in 2025; a separate record identifies a June 2026 version of a model 296 logical-layout annex. A sampled consolidated IVA BOE page identifies itself as informational and says its latest published update is 28 February 2026. Together, these examples show version and publication dates matter: the directory does not have one uniform effective date, and correct downstream selection would need to reconcile document, provision, tax year and effective date. No such selector is visible in this data-only chunk (dated RDL 16/2025 Article 17 (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/normatives/xml/rdl-16-2025-art-17.xml`), dated 2026 model 296 amendment (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/normatives/xml/boe-a-2008-18497-ai-3-redaction-20260624.xml`), consolidated IVA source page (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/normatives/html/ley-37-1992.html`)).

## Forms and provenance

The PDF area is a mixed set of four publisher-served PDF captures, nine transcriptions of form text, one provenance record, and the 2026 Modelo 390 extraction pair. The captures include the 2003 Modelo 185 annex, two Modelo 721 layouts (2023 and an amendment in 2024), and the BOE's January 2026 order updating Modelo 390 and several IVA reporting models. Its extracted text explains the changes to models 303, 322, 353 and 390 and the electronic VAT books; the extraction is paged text from a legal instrument, not a complete interactive filing service or a declaration calculator (Modelo 390 source and extraction (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/normatives/pdf/boe-a-2026-1761-modelo-390-form.pdf.extracted.md`)).

`pdf/PROVENANCE.md` distinguishes the four captures from nine manually typed annex transcriptions. It documents that the transcriptions were produced from scans or PDFs and that seven are ASCII-folded while two retain accents; this variation is deliberate and can affect exact-caption citations. The note says formula declarations cite these transcriptions for `required_text` checks, a relationship that synthesis should verify against the formula and source-catalogue code. It also records that the source image for the Modelo 296 transcription was withdrawn and cannot now be checked at the cited address; this is a statement in the bundled provenance record, not an external liveness check. It describes the 2003 Modelo 185 PDF as a genuine capture despite failing the project's later BOE-identifier classifier, and says that classifier's remit excludes these source files. These notes provide useful provenance qualifications but do not independently verify each transcription (provenance record, captures (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/normatives/pdf/PROVENANCE.md`), transcription method and formula-caption role (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/normatives/pdf/PROVENANCE.md`), withdrawn Modelo 296 image (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/normatives/pdf/PROVENANCE.md`)).

One local metadata inconsistency is confirmed: the 2026 Modelo 390 PDF's extraction JSON says attribution is unavailable because no acquisition-manifest entry matched, while the neighboring provenance record lists the same file as a BOE capture, its retrieval date, and the official BOE PDF address. This may reflect separate provenance systems, but the JSON alone loses source attribution; no consumer impact is established (PDF extraction metadata (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/normatives/pdf/boe-a-2026-1761-modelo-390-form.pdf.extracted.json`), provenance entry (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/normatives/pdf/PROVENANCE.md`)).

## Security, quality and follow-up

The reviewed content is public legal and administrative reference material; I found no customer records or credentials in the samples. The files themselves contain static HTML, extracted text, XML, PDFs and provenance notes. Nothing in this chunk executes filing, changes user data, makes network calls, or enforces authorization. Raw HTML is publisher markup, so any application behavior that renders it needs a separate rendering/sanitization review; this data inspection does not establish that it is rendered. Likewise, inaccurate or stale reference material could affect downstream advice or calculations only if and how the application consumes it. The key trust boundary is therefore the provenance, temporal selection and interpretation of bundled text, not an access-control mechanism implemented here.

A specific context-loss risk is visible in the representation: the raw Ley 37/1992 BOE page carries the explicit warning that its consolidated text is informational and has no legal value, plus its update date. The extracted Markdown begins at provision text and does not retain that page-level notice or date. JSON retains the file hash and attribution, but not an explicit legal-as-of field. If a downstream consumer indexes only the extraction, it must recover legal status and effective date elsewhere; whether it does so is unresolved. Similarly, source hashes prove that sidecars match the bundled bytes, not that the bundled bytes match an official publication or the law applicable to a user's period.

The strong local qualities are broad primary-source coverage, article-level organization, typed extraction metadata, pinned hashes, and explicit provenance for hard-to-read form annexes. The main unresolved quality questions are coverage completeness by tax/topic/year, source freshness, effective-date resolution, reconciliation of extractions with the raw publisher page, and validation of manual form transcriptions. No tests or runtime behavior are supplied by this chunk. Synthesis should trace which modules consume `.html`, `.extracted.json`, `.extracted.md`, `.xml` and transcription assets; whether they preserve source attribution and “informational” caveats; how they select redactions and tax periods; and how `required_text` citations are verified.
