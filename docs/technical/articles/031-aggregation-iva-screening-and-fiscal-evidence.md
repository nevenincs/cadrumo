## Scope

This is static analysis only: no aggregation or filing operation was executed, and no tax rule or bundled legal source was externally validated. ## Outcomes and main flows

The package supplies common aggregation primitives as well as model-specific source projections. The shared helpers bucket observations in input order, keep the first non-empty human-readable name for an identity, reject unsupported modelo keys, and fold ledger rows into sorted casilla totals with sorted transaction provenance. The quarter-to-date window helper refuses non-quarter periods and returns January 1 through quarter end so income and expense sides can use one window. A rollup checker proves declared totals equal the sum of their rollup rows; it deliberately does not prove the domain-specific uniqueness of those rows. Frozen Pydantic envelopes sort/freeze values and cross-check their modelo and period against the embedded fold. Shared grouping, window, fold, and total checks (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/grouping.py`) Strict casilla models (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/models.py`)

The IVA transaction classifier is the transaction-ledger admission path. It resolves dated model and mapping facts from the pinned authority, then gates in order: filing-period treatment, invoice direction, business attribution, foreign-currency substrate, measured base/cuota/rate, date-valid rate tier, category applicability, cash-accounting eligibility, and deduction classification. Failures become typed row issues; the readiness helper intentionally reuses the same admission gates without calculating observation values. For mixed business use, the base remains unreduced while cuota and recargo follow the business share. A deductible input row requires an exact deduction kind and provenance, validated with the domain contract and facts such as investment-asset or rectified-ledger identity. IVA admission and amount rules (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/iva_transaction.py`) Deduction validation and classification (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/iva_transaction.py`) Shared readiness gate (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/iva_transaction.py`)

After admission, ordinary rows produce a settlement observation and may independently produce a prorrata reference or prorrata issue. Cash-accounting rows split base, cuota, and recargo by recorded payment evidence and payment date, preserve the applied rate and deduction identity on each settlement, and can add an informational operation-date observation. Unpaid remainder is assigned a later fallback date; only parts inside the requested filing period are emitted. This separates timing, row admission, and prorrata diagnostics instead of hiding an invalid attachment by dropping the transaction. The classifier reads legal tables and category/component declarations through the pinned authority, but this report does not verify their tax correctness. Projection and cash-accounting split (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/iva_transaction.py`) Settlement parts (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/iva_transaction.py`)

## Invoice IVA cross-check and Renta evidence

The invoice screen supports the ledger-backed IVA binding resolver for M303/M390. It retains invoice lines with either a positive base or cuota, because base-only exempt and intra-community rows can still feed declared boxes. Where an invoice declares a category, the screen uses the dated category and counterparty gate; where input deduction applies, the deduction family comes from the paying movements' agreed classification or, for a received domestic invoice whose every cuota line is below the art. 108 escaso-valor threshold, from the invoice itself as invoice evidence; otherwise the invoice is withheld and reported rather than given a domestic-current default. Line amounts are converted to EUR before ordinary IVA comparison, and an unresolved foreign-currency amount raises a validation error rather than being silently omitted. Recargo is attributed only when the invoice identifies a single rate tier; otherwise the screen emits a disclosure. Other separate findings cover category/counterparty contradiction, received reverse charge without a rate, missing deduction authority, and published-rate recargo divergence. Base-or-cuota admission and category projection (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/modelo_bindings_invoice_iva.py`) EUR conversion and line projection (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/modelo_bindings_invoice_iva.py`) Screen result and findings (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/modelo_bindings_invoice_iva.py`)

The invoice is the IVA source: every in-period invoice contributes through the invoice-line observation and the one art. 75 period predicate, linked to a payment or not, and a movement linked to an invoice contributes nothing of its own unless it carries a bien de inversión acquisition. Links are read from either record. Ledger readiness and calculation run one shared period check that partitions invoices from their paying movements and reports blocking findings for an unresolved deduction family, a category contradicted by the counterparty, a proposed pair that would count the same operation twice, and criterio de caja enrolment, which invoices cannot yet model. Paying movements whose own figures contradict the invoice's EUR totals are a hard refusal. A blocked calculation refuses before anything is written and names the movement to open. Partition, findings and shared check (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/invoice_iva_source.py`) Invoice screen (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/modelo_bindings_invoice_iva.py`) Observation stream (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/iva_ledger.py`)

On the Renta income side, a linked issued invoice contributes fiscal figures only when its bucket and reciprocal one-transaction link match, its net cash equals invoice grand total less retention, and its decomposition is grounded. If any guard refuses invoice figures, the income row remains in aggregation using the cash path; the invoice refusal does not erase received income. Retention inference is bounded: it derives the gross-to-cash difference only when activity, base, and determinable IVA are present, and caps it using the supported governed rate. The advisory then separates declared retention from cash-shortfall inference, and distinguishes unmatched from sectoral-only rates. The optional profile signal changes wording for the sectoral advisory but cannot gate whether it fires. A strong-rate cash gap remains indistinguishable from actual withholding without separate evidence. Sales-invoice evidence checks (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/_renta_income_evidence.py`) Bounded withholding inference (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/_renta_income_evidence.py`) Inference-rate advisory (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/retencion_rate_advisory.py`)

## Profile facts, provenance, and safeguards

The M184 attribution-member resolver is activated only when the selected revision uses that source. It reads profile facts under a supplied or caller-held decode context, groups indexed socio fields, reports incomplete or schema-invalid rows, and projects only complete rows into both binding observations and filing-detail rows. The source identity is normalized through the tax-ID helper; row provenance identifies the profile field path and carries a fingerprint of the profile record. Explicit assigned base is required rather than inferred from share percentage. Resolver checks make malformed numeric facts visible as row diagnostics, although other optional conversions can still raise if stored values violate their expected shape. M184 resolver and profile projection (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/atribucion_member.py`) Row completeness and schema validation (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/atribucion_member.py`)

The workspace-level source-boundary helper refuses invoice or transaction readers that explicitly declare a different bucket; unbound in-memory readers are accepted by design. That is a composition invariant for repository-backed reads, not a general authorization mechanism. These modules carry tax identifiers, invoice references, and profile-backed financial facts through resolver values and diagnostics. A fingerprint establishes content linkage, not authenticity; a bucket check establishes scope alignment, not access control. Errors and diagnostics should therefore be reviewed together with the outer source mesh and persistence boundaries. Bucket-bound catalogue readers (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/catalogue_read_binding.py`)

Other smaller capabilities include one shared business-proportion dispatch and an explicit policy difference: quarterly M130 can accept the activity marker alone, while annual M100 requires business classification review. The undeclared-activity advisory fires only when activity narrowing excluded income, no excluded row declares any activity, the target casilla folded to zero, and the selected revision contains that casilla; its language states that the income is not classified rather than guessing its destination. The shared issue-reason module centralizes five rejection labels. The namespace initializer intentionally exports no facade. These are code-level behaviors, not validation of the filing treatment. Renta eligibility policy (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/renta_business_eligibility.py`) Undeclared-activity advisory conditions (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/undeclared_activity_advisory.py`)

## Assessment and follow-up

The strongest implementation pattern is explicit evidence routing: dated authority facts, typed refusal reasons, immutable observation provenance, clear distinction between a warning and a filing refusal, and a shared gate between readiness and calculation. Strict models protect aggregation output shape, while caller-selected revisions provide binding identifiers and legal references rather than local model-number tables. The chunk contains no tests, and static inspection cannot establish which readers/compositions are reachable in production or whether deployed registry data supports every described branch. Trace the source-mesh callers to determine whether those preconditions are guaranteed elsewhere. Also confirm that degraded invoice storage is surfaced to operators at the resolver boundary and that the profile reader uses the intended active profile for M184. Important dependencies include the IVA domain classifier and registry bindings, InvoiceCatalogueReadPorts, source_mesh, Renta ledger observations, the user-profile repository, and M184 detail-row projection.
