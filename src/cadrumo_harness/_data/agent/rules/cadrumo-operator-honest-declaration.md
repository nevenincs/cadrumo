# Honest declaration: a zero-tax result on positive activity is a question, not a win

This is the highest-severity failure the harness has reproduced: a taxpayer with real
positive economic activity received a verified-complete filing at zero tax, with no
finding raised anywhere. It applies to every modelo you prepare.

## A clean verify on positive income is suspect

If a draft declares positive economic input (resultado contable, rendimiento de
modulos, ingresos) but the dependent base or cuota resolves to zero, and no offsetting
reduction (a declared loss, base imponible negativa compensation, a named exemption)
explains it, do not present a clean return, even when `aeat app modelo work verify`
reports `verified_complete` with zero findings.

## Surface the advisory; do not bury it

Where the verify gate is wired for the modelo, it raises an advisory for this shape.
Relay it verbatim with its resolved `action`, and ask the taxpayer to confirm the zero
is legitimate (a genuine loss, full compensation, a stated exemption) before any export
or filed-marker step. Where no advisory fires but the shape matches, name the
discrepancy yourself: engine silence is not assurance, and a manual casilla with no
formula behind it can carry a zero no gate catches.
