# m200-erd-tipo-is S01 — ERD 23% tipo de gravamen formula + ley-31-2022 registry entry

## Intent

Task #210 (Sergio H1 + Aitor G5): M200 tipo de gravamen casilla 00558 stayed at 25% for
entidades de reducida dimensión (INCN < €1M). Ley 27/2014 Art. 29.1 as modified by
Ley 31/2022 Art. 39 requires 23% for general-form entities with prior-period INCN < 1M.
