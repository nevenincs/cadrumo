## Outcome

Modelo 763 remains applicability-only and non-fileable for every period the
single `2011-y-siguientes` revision selects. BOE confirms an exclusively
electronic and compulsory telematic AEAT filing procedure, but it also records
layout replacements from 2015 and from 4T 2018. AEAT publishes three distinct
record designs: its historical 2012--2014 artifact, its 2015--3T-2018
artifact, and its 4T-2018-and-following artifact. The selector begins earlier
than the first artifact's stated scope, so the shipped registration cannot
claim a single fileable layout across the selection.

The shipped revision has only the two declaration-header casillas, no bindings,
formulae, or export layout. There is no Modelo 763 producer namespace,
producer implementation, semantic map, render profile, generated export tree,
or emitted-byte proof. The generic filing link and renderer remain the single
canonical authority; this Step adds no competing writer or selector.

The reference routes first-period acquisition and period-aware era partitioning
to `W02.P04.S26`, conditional missing value lifecycles to `W02.P04.S27`, and
complete producer, map, canonical-generation, and byte-proof work to
`W02.P04.S28`. The refusal persists until every selected period has exact
source authority and a proven production export.
