# Adjudicate Modelo 194 revision 2019-y-siguientes design-era coverage

## Scope

- `.vault/reference/`

## Description

- Re-fetch the 2019, 2023, and 2024 primary BOE amendments and their exercise
  applicability provisions.
- Re-fetch AEAT's current and historic record-design catalogues, the exact
  2019, 2023, and 2024 design artifacts, and the historical filing surface.
- Compare those primary sources with the loaded revision, source catalogue,
  corpus manifest, producer vocabulary, and derived filing-capability refusal.
- Record the supported, evidence, and fileable boundaries with existing-plan
  owners and reconsideration gates; do not alter the registry or exporter.

## Outcome

Modelo 194 remains applicability-only and non-fileable for every selected
exercise. The official 2019 design is publicly available but is not in the
bundled, hash-pinned source catalogue; the repository has only exact 2023 and
2024 designs. Those eras contain real statutory record changes and cannot
legitimize a 2019--2022 layout.

The reference assigns source-era acquisition and selection constraints to
`W02.P04.S26`, and complete producer, map, canonical-generation, and byte-proof
work to `W02.P04.S28`, with `W02.P04.S27` as the conditional owner of a missing
external source lifecycle. It preserves the existing refusal until every
selected year, non-casilla value, and emitted byte is independently evidenced.
