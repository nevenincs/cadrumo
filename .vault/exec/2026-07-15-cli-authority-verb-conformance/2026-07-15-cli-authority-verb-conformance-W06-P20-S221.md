# Run the required fresh-context campaign-close honesty review with explicit audits for duplicated authority, vacuous adoption tests, ignored accepted parameters, partial transaction commits, and false-green quality infrastructure

## Scope

- `.vault/audit/`

## Description

Run the fresh-context campaign-close honesty review and persist it as a
vault audit document.

Sweep the five operator-facing surfaces the conformance gates do not scan,
after first proving the sweep can tell a removed spelling from a live one.

Prove each cited audit instrument still discriminates before trusting its
result, rather than reading a green run as evidence.

Track every recommendation as a Step with a verification gate.

## Outcome

Eleven findings recorded: four critical, three high, four medium.

The critical findings are a fail-open in the profile-bound write guard where
every invoice mutation had fallen outside the catalogue, a plan whose
completion was overstated at handover with one of 55 Steps closed in the
prior Wave, and a Wave whose own evidence was not produced, with 12 of 13
execution records left as empty scaffolds.

Two instruments were proven vacuous or blind and are recorded as findings
rather than passed over: the only gate over the write-guard catalogue cannot
distinguish a dead entry from a live write verb, because an unknown command
key classifies as not-read-only, and a naive walk of the lazy command tree
yields one leaf while completing without error.

Two instruments were proven to discriminate and are reported as trustworthy
on that basis: the duplication runner refuses to render empty output, garbage
output, a bare zero-clone claim, an absent binary or an exceeded timeout as
green, and the surface sweep correctly rejects a known-removed spelling.

Nine recommendations were tracked as Steps W06.P20.S267 through S275. One of
them names a decision a follow-on ADR must make, namely the criterion by
which a command path is profile-bound, and does not record the decision here.

A fourth critical finding surfaced after the first pass: the layered import
contracts had not been running at all, because import-linter aborts on a stale
ignore entry and two were present. Restoring the run showed three contracts
kept and two broken.

## Notes

The review contradicts its own handover brief on a load-bearing point, and
the contradiction is the reason it was worth running. The brief stated that
Waves W03, W04 and W05 had landed. W05 carries one closed Step of 55.

The campaign is NOT declared structurally complete by this review. Step S222
remains open deliberately.

Both dispatched verification agents scaffolded their execution records without
filling them: 12 of 13 for W06.P18 and 24 of 24 for W06.P19 carry headings and
no evidence. No Step in either phase was closed on that basis. Counted by file
existence alone those 36 records would have read as 36 completed Steps, which
is why the reconciliation asserts a populated body rather than a present file.

Two standing remainders are recorded rather than concealed. The semantic code
index was truncated throughout while reporting itself healthy, so absence was
established by reading and exact search and never by a semantic miss. Custody
cases carrying the keychain marker fail under an agent logon for environmental
reasons, have never been observed green in any lane, and were not verified
here.
