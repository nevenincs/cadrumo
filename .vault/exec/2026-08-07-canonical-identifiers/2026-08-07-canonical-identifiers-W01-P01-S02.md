the consumer sweep did not travel with them. Nine importers were left unbinding and the

So the atomicity rule was violated in the direction that matters most: one Step equals one
symbol equals one commit carrying the move **and** every consumer, and here the consumers
arrived later. No defect survives at HEAD - the census above is what establishes that, not
the existence of the repair commits.

**The diagnosable cause is not carelessness and is worth more than the row.** This lane had
swept its earlier relocations by grep and landed them atomically. Between those and these it
adopted letting the type checker enumerate consumers - a better instrument in one dimension
and blind in precisely the one that matters here, because an unresolved local name is not an
unresolved cross-package import and nothing resolves an `__all__` string at all. Two
relocations broke in a row before the limit was diagnosed.

**The correction is a run, not an analysis.** The last step of a relocation is a tree-wide
collect reading ERROR lines only, taken before the commit, because only a collect or a real
import exercises a cross-package consumer. Its purchase is bounded, and stating the bound
matters more than the rule: a collect catches the unswept-consumer class and does **not**
catch a typing-only import break, which has been live at HEAD while a collect returned zero
errors.
