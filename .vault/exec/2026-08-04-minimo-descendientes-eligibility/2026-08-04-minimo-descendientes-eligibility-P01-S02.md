## Outcome

The Art. 61 norma 2a own-return exclusion ships as a registry money parameter for every
revision 2020-2025, valued 1.800 euros, with its own legal-catalogue entry and corpus
anchor.

The anchor was verified verbatim by the coordinator: the clause "No procederá la aplicación
del mínimo por descendientes, ascendientes o discapacidad, cuando los ascendientes o
descendientes que generen el derecho a los mismos presenten declaración por este Impuesto
con rentas superiores a 1.800 euros" is present in the bundled corpus.

The same two-authority cross-check, the same measured invariance evidence, and the same
in-memory mutation proof apply as for the sibling threshold, and the discriminating-anchor
matrix confirms this clause matches its own article and not the neighbouring one.

Both entries carry an effective date of 1 January 2015 with no end date.
