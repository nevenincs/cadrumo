# Author two manual-oracle fixtures from the AEAT worked examples that carry printed figures, the Asturias two-entitled-filer example whose individual total is exactly half its conjunta total and which needs no CCAA correction because that comunidad exercised no normative competence, and the own-return-exclusion example grounded on its estatal column ONLY because its comunidad did diverge, then keep the cap and the anualidades flag as structural proofs since a zero, a preserved birth-order rank and a boolean flip are properties no printed figure would prove better

## Scope

- `src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/manual_oracles/`
- `src/cadrumo/application/modelo/tests/`

## Description

## Outcome

Two manual-oracle fixtures ship, grounded on AEAT worked examples with printed figures, and
the act of writing them found a defect in the predicate this campaign had already landed.

That is the headline and it belongs first. Probing all four printed scenarios against the
engine BEFORE authoring anything, three matched and one did not: a conjunta return by an
unmarried couple came out 2.550 euros too high. The engine had collapsed every conjunta into
the married case. Art. 82.1 declares two modalities of unidad familiar, and the second --
which applies absent a marriage -- is one parent plus the children, not both. So the other
progenitor remains separately entitled and norma 1a still prorates. The derivation now turns
on marriage rather than on the declaration type alone.

The ruling that was wrong had been adjudicated by the design authority and independently
verified by the coordinator against the corpus. Both were right within the married case they
reasoned about, and neither had reason to probe the unmarried one. The oracle found it in a
single measurement because the manual prints both cases side by side. This is the argument
for external grounding over reasoning, demonstrated rather than asserted, and it is why this
Step required printed figures rather than a hand-derived expectation.

The direction matters: the defect was an over-grant, which under-declares tax, and it was
introduced by the fix for exactly that class of defect.

One scenario remains ungrounded and is recorded rather than guessed at. The second example's
conjunta figure depends on which descendants sit inside the joint return, a per-descendant
unidad-familiar membership the profile does not carry. Rather than invent a fix, the residual
was measured and left: the error moved from a 2.550 over-grant to a 2.000 under-grant, which
is the safe direction and the one the advisory surfaces. It needs its own grounding.

A canonical-home sweep changed the implementation. The first probe returned this Step's own
new file, ranked first because the index had already picked up uncommitted code -- a
contaminated probe the executor recognised and re-ran by meaning. The second probe found an
existing bundled-oracle inventory with a strict payload model, which a locally written JSON
reader had already duplicated. The reader was retired onto the canonical model and that model
promoted to the package facade, promotion being a precondition of the consuming change rather
than a follow-up.

A finding was reported rather than worked around: the canonical inventory exposes casilla ids
but not expected values, and the model was private, so three existing test modules each carry
their own untyped reader. The promotion now makes that avoidable. The cluster was named, not
swept.

The enrolment gate was confirmed green for the right reason rather than by absence: the
inventory reports both fixtures attributed with nothing unattributed.
