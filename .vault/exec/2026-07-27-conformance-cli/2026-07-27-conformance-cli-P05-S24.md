# run the fresh-context campaign-close honesty review and track every surfaced item as a new step or a formally deferred follow-up

## Scope

- `.vault/audit`

## Description

- Dispatch an independent reviewer with a fresh context, briefed to inherit the
  campaign rather than summarise it and to treat the closure narrative as a claim
  to be tested.
- Hand it this campaign's own record of closed-but-not-done work as grounds to
  distrust that narrative.
- Ask it the question the campaign cannot answer about itself: whether the growth
  from twenty-four Steps was honest discovery or scope generation.
- Persist the findings as an audit and open a Step for every item surfaced.

## Outcome

The gate returned REVISION REQUIRED with five high findings, and it was worth the
cost. Three of the five were the coordinator's own: a committed privacy violation
that left the per-push lane red, a gate-triage record falsely claiming this
campaign's half of the tree-wide gates was clear, and a Step closed while the
precondition named in its own row text had never landed. The last of those had a
compounding cause: an audit reference pointed at a Step that was later re-scoped
to different work, and the reference was never updated, so a real
under-declaration-shape gap fell out of tracking entirely.

The reviewer also confirmed what works, which matters as much: every figure in the
measurement audit re-derived independently, all four verbs behaved to the declared
screen-versus-gate posture, and an eight-probe laundering battery against the
stamp writer held. It verified the decision-record amendments against the tree
rather than against the record, which this campaign's history gives specific
reason to do.

Two findings turned out larger than stated once acted on. The tax-corrections
filing gap was not one missing decision but seven prior records touching the
territory, two of which were found stating falsehoods about live behaviour — so
the corrections were not new decisions but amendments that made accepted records'
premises true. The capability duplication was three sites, not two, and resolved
by retiring a package rather than delegating.

## Notes

The reviewer's assessment of the Step growth is recorded in the audit because it
is the part the driving agent cannot see. Roughly fifty-five per cent of the added
Steps are remediation of this campaign's own new code across four review rounds,
which says those verbs landed under-specified and were hardened by iteration
rather than designed. That is honest discovery about the campaign's quality rather
than about the registry, and it is the more useful finding.

One methodological point worth carrying: the correction the coordinator issued to
the decision-record work was itself wrong, and the executing agent overturned it
with evidence. Being corrected by a brief is not a reason to stop checking the
brief.

The review named an item this record must not paper over: it observed a fabricated
governance stamp appear briefly in the shipped registry during the session and
could not attribute it, while correctly identifying the structural cause as the
stamp command having no injectable registry root until that gap was closed
mid-review.
