`2023/0A` and `2024/0A` as `ingress_blocked`. The official BOE message designs,
AEAT SOAP/XML materials, and S97--S99 remain target and output work, not source
evidence. Existing manual inputs and encrypted threshold-continuity
observations remain genuine but distinct direct/history paths. No conclusion is
drawn for 2025 or later.
