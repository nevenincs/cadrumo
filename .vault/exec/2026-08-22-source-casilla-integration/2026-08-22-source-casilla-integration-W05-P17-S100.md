# adjudicate Modelo 182 donor-detail and Article-3 declarant/header source semantics, including type-1 nature `3` and administrator-holder identity, from official evidence

## Scope

- Canonical research record for Modelo 182 row-source grounding.
- Official BOE Article-3 filer population and AEAT exercise-2025 record design.
- Existing donor carrier, deferred source-mesh disposition, source-connectivity census, Censo boundary, and export boundary.

## Description

- Compare Article 3's statutory filer classes with the AEAT type-1/type-2 record split.
- Verify type-1 nature `3`, the type-2 administrator-holder NIF/name fields, and header-conditioned donor-row fields against the hash-pinned 2025 design.
- Inventory the current donor carrier, row fold, synthetic worksheet identity, secure-owner evidence, deferral, and no-export state.
- Record the bounded evidence result without enrolling a resolver or changing registry, source-mesh, export, or census behavior.

## Outcome

BOE Article 3 establishes recipient entities, specified political-party cases, and protected-estate holders or administrators as the Modelo 182 filer population; donors are declared-person facts, not the filer. The 2025 AEAT design preserves that non-substitutability: type 1 owns declarant identity, nature, count, aggregate total, and declaration controls; type 2 repeats the declarant NIF and carries the declared-person record. Nature `3` identifies a protected-estate holder or administrator, and an administrator requires the separate holder NIF and name in type 2.

The existing donor worksheet observation has only partial donor values and a synthetic row-index source id. Its fold loses the official separate-row requirement for different deduction rates and cash/in-kind donations, and it lacks the header, representative, province, key, conditional, revocation, asset, and holder axes. No Modelo 182 secure repository or live resolver owns either complete donor detail or Article-3 header/holder facts. Censo is an active taxpayer self-read, not an authority for representation or third-party/holder identity.

The source remains deferred and the 2025-only applicability/no-export boundary remains unchanged. The canonical research record carries the evidence and reopening prerequisites. S101 is not implemented.
