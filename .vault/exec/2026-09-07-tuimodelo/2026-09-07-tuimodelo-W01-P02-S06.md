## Notes

The gate now reds when a recorded disposition contradicts the observed shape, and the rule is
ARM-RELATIVE rather than one predicate over both delivery arms. That distinction is the whole
design and it was nearly got wrong. Dispatchability is membership of the mutation dispatch table,
so it can never be true for a read; a single conjunction applied to both arms would have made a
delivered read permanently unclaimable and would have red forever on the first row that earned
one, blocking a later wave's own closure condition. Reads therefore fail closed with their own
reason until a read-routing fact is observed, rather than being judged against a fact that does
not describe them.

The intersection clause is satisfied by construction rather than by a scoping predicate, and the
module now says so, because a later reader will meet a loop running over every classified row.
Being wired requires surface dispatchability, which requires dispatch membership, and the row is
live by construction, so the stale-pending arm can only fire inside the intersection. The
false-delivery arm is deliberately NOT confined there: a row claiming delivery while dispatchable
by nothing sits outside the intersection by construction, so scoping it would have excluded from
the rule exactly the input the rule exists to catch.

The rule is carried by a pure helper rather than written inline, and the reason is a test
constraint rather than style. The validator never accepts a live signature from a caller, which
is an invariant its own docstring protects, so no table fixture can reach the wired half of the
rule. Testing the quadrants through a helper keeps that invariant intact instead of opening a
live-side injection seam to make the rule testable.

Five defect proofs accompany the rule, and two of them carry the argument. The negative control
asserts that an available routing posture with no dispatch entry is NOT a contradiction, which is
what makes the conjunction non-arbitrary rather than a definition chosen to keep the gate green.
And a delivered claim on an UNDISPATCHABLE row proves the false-delivery arm reaches outside the
intersection; without that case the scoping decision above would be unproven. Each proof also
asserts the absence of a signature-drift message for the same identity, so it cannot pass on the
wrong rule.

Zero violations at head is the correct outcome and not a weak one: nothing in the tree is
delivered today, one destination is reachable, and the six dispatchable mutations still declare no
routing posture. The rule is armed for the wave that wires them.

Two stale docstrings in the touched files were corrected in the same change: the module's taxonomy
list still enumerated six arms after the taxonomy grew to eight, and the test module's corpus
figure said seventy-eight where the table holds seventy-nine.
