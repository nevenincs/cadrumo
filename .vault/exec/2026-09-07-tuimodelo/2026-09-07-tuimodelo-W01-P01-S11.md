# Record that the capability relocation needs no separate decision because the migration boundary already governs it

## Scope

- `.vault/adr/2026-09-07-tuimodelo-adapter-migration-adr.md`

## Changes

- `M` `.vault/adr/2026-09-07-tuimodelo-adapter-migration-adr.md`

## Notes

The clause was written into the accepted decision in place rather than as a new record, under
the operator's acceptance condition that these decisions are amended as measurement lands. It
adds no new decision: it states that the capability relocation is implementation under the
boundary the decision already defines, and names the ordering obligation that follows from that
boundary, being that the relocation precedes the denominator extension because the denominator
imports the package declaring the capability today.
