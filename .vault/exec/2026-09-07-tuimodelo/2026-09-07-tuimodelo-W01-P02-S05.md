## Notes

The closed taxonomy grew from six arms to eight, gaining a delivered arm for reads and one for
mutations. The arms are placed beside their pending counterparts rather than appended, because
the pairing is what the taxonomy now expresses: a read or a mutation is either still owed a
surface or has one an operator can reach.

The recorded meaning is deliberately narrower than "built". Delivered means reachable and
invocable, so a screen that exists with no route to it, or an action a surface can name but
cannot dispatch, stays pending. That distinction is exactly what the routing posture and
dispatchability fields added by the preceding step exist to keep honest, and without it the new
arms would let a row claim delivery that no operator could exercise.

NO ROW WAS RECLASSIFIED ONTO THE NEW ARMS, and that is deliberate rather than incomplete. This
step adds the vocabulary; deciding that a particular action is delivered is a claim about a
shipped surface, which belongs to the step that ships it. Moving rows here would assert delivery
for surfaces this campaign has not yet built.

The step carries a positive proof rather than only a definition, because an enum arm nothing can
occupy is not an extension. A test constructs a row on each new arm carrying the observed shape
of a wired action -- available routing posture and surface dispatchability together -- and
asserts it validates. The taxonomy previously had no arm such a row could take.

No gate pinned the arm set, checked before extending: the enumeration has no count assertion
anywhere in the tree, so adding arms could not silently break a frozen tally.
