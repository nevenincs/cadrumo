## Notes

The sibling campaign is opened as a grounding record rather than as a decision or a plan, because
nothing about the residual has been scoped or scheduled and inventing either would charter work
nobody has sized. What a future campaign needs from this step is what remains, how the figure is
derived, who owned it before, and which parts have no owner at all.

THE EXACT REMAINDER IS DELIBERATELY NOT ASSERTED, and refusing to assert it is the substance of
this step rather than a gap in it. The lane arithmetic gives 140, being the ledger, configuration
and live lanes. The modelo campaign additionally takes a small number of named correctness rows
that cross into those lanes, and the crossings are identified in the plan by the step that takes
them rather than by inventory row. Mapping a step to a row was attempted and not confirmed, so
subtracting an unconfirmed count would have produced exactly the rounded figure the governing
decision forbids. The campaign already carries a later step whose whole subject is reconciling the
take and the remainder against the 159, and that step is named here as the authority for the final
number.

The ownership finding is the reason this record exists at all. The ledger lane's sixty-two rows
are the largest block and the only one that is unowned rather than merely unscheduled: they were
inherited from a campaign that is now archived, whose gate can never close because the step that
would close it is unchecked inside an archived plan. Those rows have been orphaned once already,
and a remainder with no named owner is indistinguishable from a remainder nobody considered. The
other two lanes are unscheduled rather than orphaned, and the record says so rather than letting
one status stand for both.

Three findings from the modelo slice are carried forward because they generalise: the migration
target already exists in the tree rather than needing invention, a relocation that moves
filing-grade law is blocked on citing its provision rather than on effort, and a write-route
declaration can bypass the root storage guard silently because the guard keys on the declaration
alone.
