## Notes

Twenty-five open rows were inventoried and each was named individually rather than collectively.
Seven already carried a disposition from this wave's earlier adjudications, sixteen were annotated
here, and two could not be annotated safely.

THE HEADLINE IS THAT THIS CAMPAIGN ABSORBS NONE OF THEM. Every row is retained by its source
owner. Three are modelo-subject and overlap this campaign without being covered by it: the proof
that the installed root reaches the modelo workspace only through admitted declarations, the
spreadsheet routing through the operation supervisor, and the reconciliation of every operation's
permitted-frontend claims. In each case a tuimodelo step touches the same area without being a
superset, so claiming absorption would have stranded the uncovered remainder. Two more are
modelo-subject and covered by nothing here at all, searched rather than assumed: the edit
submission handoff and the modelo 390 export tree.

TWO ROWS WERE DELIBERATELY NOT ANNOTATED, and skipping them is the correct outcome rather than a
gap. Their scope clauses are malformed -- the same defect the plan check already reports on three
rows of that plan -- so the owning verb refuses to round-trip them, and forcing an edit would
rewrite another lane's row into something it never said. Their dispositions are recorded here
instead, and the repair belongs to the plan that owns them.

One of those two is the corrupted row, and its disposition is recorded by its TRUE subject rather
than by what it now reads. An earlier cross-lane overwrite replaced its action text with its
neighbour's; the row consequently declares itself blocked on itself, while its trailing paragraph
still discusses the command-path authority decision it actually owns. Annotating it from its
visible text would have propagated the corruption into a second plan under this campaign's name.

An integrity check on the sixteen edits initially reported seven mismatches, which turned out to
be the check's own defect rather than the data's: BOTH PLANS REUSE THE SAME STEP IDENTIFIERS, so
comparing by bare identifier matched each plan's row against the other plan's expected length.
Scoped per plan, every one of the sixteen carries exactly its intended text.
