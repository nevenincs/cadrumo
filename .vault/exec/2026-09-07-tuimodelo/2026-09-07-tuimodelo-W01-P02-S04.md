## Notes

THE BLIND SPOT THIS STEP CLOSES IS LIVE, NOT HYPOTHETICAL, and measuring it first is what made the
change worth making rather than merely scheduled. Across the seventy-nine live candidates the
declared routing posture is seventy-seven unimplemented against two available, while six
identities are both live and dispatchable from a surface today. The two sets are DISJOINT: every
one of the six dispatchable actions declares itself unimplemented, and neither action declaring
itself available is dispatchable at all. Eight identities therefore sit in a state no gate could
observe, which is exactly what this step exists to make visible.

The signature grew from four fields to six, and every row records its observed posture and
dispatchability. The validator returns zero violations, which is the correct outcome for this
step rather than a weak one: recording today's truth ARMS the ratchet, so that wiring a surface
or registering a dispatch entry now changes an observed field and reds the gate. Turning the
contradiction itself into a failure belongs to the next step, which now has a measured population
to red on.

A defect in this step's own work was found by the gate and fixed. Adding two required fields broke
three direct model constructions in the owning test, which had been asserting refusal paths and
instead began failing on missing fields; two of them were asserting a specific refusal message and
would have kept passing for the wrong reason had the match been looser. They now carry the unwired
shape, which is what each fixture is about.

Recorded because a later step will meet it: the table half of the module grew by two lines per row
and now sits about a hundred lines under the default ceiling, at roughly fourteen lines per row.
Six later steps add rows, so the table needs its own baseline entry or a further split before they
all land. The headroom created by the preceding step is real but this step spent most of it.
