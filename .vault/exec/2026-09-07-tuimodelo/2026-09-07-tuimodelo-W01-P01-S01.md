## Notes

The adjudication returns all seven rows to this plan rather than re-homing any of them. Two
arms were used: `HOLD_VOID_ROW_ALREADY_CLOSED` on the two rows already closed, and
`HOLD_VOID_OWNERSHIP_RETURNED` on the five open rows. An earlier four-arm draft carrying a
severed-slice arm and an unowned arm was rejected on review, because the Ledger slice is not
severable: the visual inventory scans the whole full-screen root with no exclusion mechanism and
the Ledger surfaces already sit inside its denominator, so a non-Ledger-only completion would
require narrowing the inventory itself.

Three annotation shapes were found where the brief expected two. Four rows carry a parity-slice
hold with an explicit non-Ledger remainder, two carry a production-slice hold with a
re-admission condition, and one carries an unconditional exclusion that gate closure would never
have lifted. All three are voided on the same ground and the difference is recorded per row.

Scope boundary, recorded because it could otherwise read as resolved: this step adjudicates the
seven annotated rows in this plan only. The archived campaign's own open rows, including its
Ledger product build, are a separate and larger orphaning that this step is not chartered to
adjudicate and has not resolved.

Two collisions were found and are left for their owners. The archived campaign's `W05.P19.S128`
records held-row dispositions and is unrelated to this campaign's `W01.P03.S128`, which charters
adapter-purity residual; the shared number will mislead a reader. Separately, an active
`.vault/exec/2026-09-04-clitui-ledger/` directory still sits alongside the archived copy of the
same feature.
