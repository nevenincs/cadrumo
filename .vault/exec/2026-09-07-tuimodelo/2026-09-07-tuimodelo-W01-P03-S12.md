## Notes

NOTHING WAS PUBLISHED, BECAUSE THE STANDING ARTEFACT ALREADY EXISTS. The classification table is
the coverage baseline: the governing decision states that every family keeps its row there
carrying its disposition, reason and reopening condition, and that this is what makes a deferral
auditable rather than a silence. The validator and the contradiction rule already enforce it.
Publishing a generated file beside it would have rebuilt the denominator snapshot a previous
campaign deleted for being reproducible from its builder, placed it in the one tree where every
artefact has a consuming gate and this one would have none, and taken the shape two standing rules
name as a frozen corpus count.

WHAT WAS GENUINELY MISSING IS ARM COHERENCE OF THE NARRATIVE FIELDS. A row carries three of them
beside its disposition and only the reason was ever validated, so the condition a row is SCHEDULED
BY was free to contradict the arm it is scheduled AS. The reopening-condition vocabulary is now
closed per arm and refused at construction.

THE HOLE WAS OCCUPIED BY A LIVE DEFECT THIS CAMPAIGN INTRODUCED ONE STEP EARLIER, which is the
strongest argument for the rule and is recorded rather than quietly repaired. Reclassifying the
recipient-encryption row from read to mutation updated its disposition and its reason and left its
reopening condition behind, so the row was recorded as a mutation while scheduled behind the read
migration, and would never have been enrolled for the conformance its own arm requires. Counting
arms against conditions is what found it: every other arm carried exactly one condition and that
arm carried two.

The vocabulary was DERIVED FROM THE TABLE RATHER THAN WRITTEN FROM MEMORY, after a first attempt
invented a condition for the bounded-review arm that the table does not use. Three arms are
deliberately left unconstrained because nothing occupies them yet, and inventing conditions for
them would repeat that error at rest.

Five existing fixtures broke on the new rule and were aligned rather than exempted. A fixture free
to construct an incoherent row would mean the rule is a convention rather than a contract, and the
model is the boundary that owns it.
