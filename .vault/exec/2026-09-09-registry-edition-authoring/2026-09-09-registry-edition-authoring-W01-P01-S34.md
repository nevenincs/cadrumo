# [M | opus-medium] Decide the twelve population and ratio floors by asking of each the same question that governs the twelve guards: is the asserted population a defect we intend to repair, or a fact about the corpus we do not control? A frozen corpus count wearing a floor's clothing ratchets against this campaign and will fail the moment the delta lands, which is the worst possible time for a mystery failure. A floor asserting that the corpus exercises a code path at all is a different thing and should survive. Decide by that question, not by whether the assertion is spelled as a floor. Proof: each is kept with its reason, converted to a planted fixture, or deleted with a stated argument.
