## Outcome

BOTH BLOCKERS CLEARED, and the answer is NO DEFECT. The enrolled foreign-asset
resolver writes its row bindings in the exact shape the evidence projection
joins on, and the link the earlier trace asserted is now read rather than
assumed.

The row's two conditions were: the finding model mid-rename with the new shape
existing only in a working tree, and the neighbouring end-to-end module still
asserting the old one. Both have landed at HEAD - the locale-key and facts
shape is in the production module, and the instrument itself is committed
rather than uncommitted. So the row's precondition was met and its warning
against running meanwhile no longer applies.

The instrument was RED on all five cases, and the reason was NOT the question
it exists to ask. It never reached the producer. Two stacked filing-grade
profile readiness refusals stood in front of it: an undeclared tax territory,
and behind that the IVA block that became mandatory after this module was
written. Only once both were declared did the producer run, and then every
case passed.

So the row's own framing was right in a way it could not have known: it warned
against running the instrument against an unsettled model shape and measuring
a state nobody else had. The state that actually needed settling was the
PROFILE the harness builds, not the model.
