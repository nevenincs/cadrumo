## Outcome

WEIGHED AND REFUSED, on the hazard the row itself insisted be answered first.
The row asked to be judged as detection-with-a-precision-problem against
derivation-with-a-guarantee-problem rather than as an obvious win, and judged
that way it loses -- but so does the alternative, and a third shape carries the
candidate's every advantage without its defect.

The hazard is real and unanswerable at acceptable cost. Telling a second
CHARGED IVA tier from a recargo percentage, a retencion percentage or a
discount quoted in prose is the rule-table-over-arbitrary-prose problem the ADR
already names as either trivially incomplete or a model wearing a lookup table.
These findings BLOCK confirmation, so the cost of a false fire is not noise --
it is an operator learning to dismiss the channel that exists to stop them
filing a wrong figure, which is worse than the silence it replaces.

What the candidate had right is the LAYER: deterministic, side-effect free,
already attaching findings, no contract change, no anchor change, no prompt
cost. The adopted shape keeps all of that and drops the transcription. The flat
triple closes over ITSELF -- base times rate equals cuota, the same accounting
identity the per-tier check already applies -- so it reads no prose and has
nothing to misclassify. It asks only whether three figures the reader already
produced agree with each other.

The confounds are answered by the field split rather than by tolerance, which
is why no allowance had to be widened: recargo carries its own rate and amount
and is not inside the cuota, suplidos sit outside the base imponible per LIVA
art. 78.Tres.3 and carry their own field, retencion has its own identity. An
exempt or reverse-charge document states no rate or no cuota, and an identity
with a missing term does not run at all.

No change made on this row's own terms. The identity landed on its sibling.

## Notes

This row did its job precisely by being refused. It was opened to stop the
expensive proposal from being executed unexamined, it forced the comparison,
and the comparison produced a third answer better than either. A candidate that
loses on its own stated hazard is not a wasted row -- writing the hazard down
where the decision would be made is what kept a blocking channel from being
filled with prose-parsing guesses.
