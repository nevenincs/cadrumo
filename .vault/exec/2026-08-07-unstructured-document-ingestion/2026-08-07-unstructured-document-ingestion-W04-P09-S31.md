# Bundle the licence-clean fixture subset with provenance sidecars, including both COM-2026-0005 entries

## Scope

- `src/cadrumo/application/ledger/tests`

## Description

- Copy the two COM-2026-0005 control renderings from the external evidence corpus into the bundled fixture directory, verifying byte identity at read, after copy, and against the committed git blobs.
- Author a provenance sidecar for each, declaring the corpus document id, the manifest content address, the licence, the content address and size of the bundled bytes, and the PDF producer where one exists.
- Extend the corpus gate to settle each sidecar claim against physical evidence rather than accepting the declaration.
- Survey the whole corpus manifest for licence status and record the decline set.

## Outcome

Both renderings of the deliberately-defective control document are bundled: the text-layer PDF and the camera photograph of the same document. One document in two renderings is the point, because a reader that fails on only one is failing on the rendering rather than on the content. The four defects are deliberate and are recorded as such in both sidecars: an invalid control character on the issuer identifier, a non-correlative invoice number, no printed per-rate breakdown, and a printed total that disagrees with the sum of base and cuota. Anything reporting this document clean has a gap, so nothing here may be repaired or normalised. Printed total and grand total are distinct fields throughout the source corpus and agree everywhere except on this document.

Both entries are declared synthetic rather than externally authored. The source manifest attests generated origin and synthetic status for these exact bytes, and the licence is a public-domain dedication.

### The provenance asymmetry, and why the mirror rule is not imposed

The shared fixture vocabulary requires anything declaring synthetic origin to carry the in-tree generator signature in its producer field. These documents were rendered by the external corpus's own tooling under a generic library signature, so that requirement encodes an assumption that does not hold for this class: it assumes every synthetic fixture was generated by this project. Applied here, an honest sidecar would have failed the gate.

Both available escapes were worse. Declaring external origin is precisely the lie the gate exists to catch. A per-fixture exception list re-introduces the honour system the gate exists to remove.

The gate instead binds each claim to the physical evidence appropriate to that fixture's kind. Universally, the declared content address and size must equal the committed bytes. A declared producer must equal the document's own document-information dictionary, which is how an externally rendered synthetic binds its claim. And the honesty direction is kept exactly as the shared vocabulary defines it: no fixture may claim external origin while carrying the in-tree generator signature.

The mirror rule is deliberately not imposed, and the reason is the decisive one: forcing it would push an author toward mis-declaring external origin on synthetic bytes, which is strictly worse than the gap it closes. A gate that makes honesty expensive buys dishonesty.

An anti-vacuity assertion is what keeps the producer check real rather than decorative: if no fixture declares a producer, the gate fails rather than passing over an empty set. Unreadable document information is determined by measurement, since several fixtures are adversarial by design and images and markup carry none at all, so the condition is a property of the bytes and never a list of exempt filenames.

### Licence survey and the decline set

The decline set is thirty-five documents, not the twenty-three initially assumed. Twenty-three carry copyleft or unresolved-chain status: eight strong-copyleft with a network-use clause, three whose repository licence is verified but whose upstream rightsholder publishes no reuse grant, five dual-licensed under a copyleft pair, five share-alike at one version and two at another. A further twelve carry a bare see-repo marker, which is an unresolved marker rather than a licence and therefore falls inside the same instruction to decline anything unresolved. None of the thirty-five is bundled.

The clean generated set is sixty-eight documents under a public-domain dedication. It is deliberately NOT bundled.

### The bundling rule going forward

Bundle a fixture when a gate names it, and not otherwise. The in-repo lane is a gate lane, and a gate needs representative fixtures rather than a corpus; the full document set stays external and read-only, pinned by the manifest content address, where the scoring harness consumes it. Several megabytes of binaries in every clone, permanently, to duplicate a corpus that already exists beside the tree is a bad trade. When a later gate needs a specific document, that document is bundled then, with its sidecar, under the same evidence-binding discipline. This is recorded here so the question is not re-opened.

Byte identity was verified three times per file: on read from the source, after the copy, and against the committed git blob. The third is the one that matters, because the first two only establish what was copied and not what landed.

Three mutation proofs. Each built a temporary copy of the fixture directory with one sidecar mutated and repointed the gate at that copy, so nothing inside the repository was written and a crashed run could leave no residue.

Mis-declaring a content address reds exactly one test, the content-address check.

Mis-declaring a producer reds exactly one test, the document-information cross-check. This is the mis-stamping case the gate exists for.

Flipping a sidecar to claim external origin, with the document's producer rewritten to carry the in-tree generator signature, reds two: the honesty-direction check, and the anti-vacuity assertion, which fired because that same mutation removed the only declared producer in the set. The second red is the vacuity guard demonstrating that it works.

## Notes

A sweep committed the four fixture files and most of the gate body while verification was still running; the remainder landed separately, and the working tree was confirmed identical to the committed content afterwards. The committed blobs were re-hashed and match the source manifest exactly, so byte identity survived the sweep.

The bundled filenames and the corpus document id recorded in each sidecar were relayed to the lane consuming them before it wired its lookup, so either key resolves.

Three import-hygiene failures observed earlier against an unrelated peer test-debt entry were resolved by another lane during this step and are green.

No grounding assertions were written here. Those belong to the step that consumes these fixtures, and authoring them alongside the fixtures would have meant a fixture shaped to match its own assertion.
