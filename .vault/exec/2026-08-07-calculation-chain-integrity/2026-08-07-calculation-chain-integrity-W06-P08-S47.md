# `calculation-chain-integrity` exec W06.P08.S47

## Outcome

Scoped as `2026-08-07-calculation-chain-integrity-m390-annual-under-modelling-research`. Scoping only — no design proposed, because the point is to size the work and record what it is not.

## The measurement

Both of the Step's figures verified from the bundled 2024 diseño rather than restated:

- **375** distinct official box numbers.
- **22** registry casillas on `2010-y-siguientes`, 16 of them bound.

So the annual return is modelled at roughly six per cent of its declared surface. The registry's casillas sit almost entirely in §5 (Operaciones Reg. Gral.) and §7 (Resultado liquidación anual); sections 6 and 8 through 13 are essentially unmodelled.

The Step's other claim holds too: the diseño splits goods from services and splits by rate where the registry carries one combined casilla.

## The correction this scoping carries

`W06.P08.S46` established that the finding which drew attention here — four ledger-IVA categories on the quarterly side with no annual counterpart — is **not** the under-modelling.

Those four are on the annual form in §10 Volumen de operaciones, a turnover disclosure rather than a liquidación. The annual settlement genuinely does not carry per-category export or intra-community devengado lines the way the quarterly return does.

Carrying that into the scoping document is the load-bearing part. Reading the category comparison as the gap leads to adding devengado bindings for boxes the annual liquidación does not have — inventing settlement lines out of a turnover section, which is a worse outcome than the current absence.

## Candidate ordering, offered as a frame

§10 volumen (where the four concepts actually live and the only section with a live consumer today), §12 prorratas (the domain substrate already exists, so it is binding-and-casilla work rather than new computation), §6 Reg. Simplificado (real settlement, carrying its own inversión-del-sujeto-pasivo devengado line at `[1084]`), then §5's rate and goods/services splits.

## Dependencies stated up front

The §5 per-leg split will drop the intracom concept out of the reconciliation parity comparison unless the semantic roles are handled deliberately. `W06.P08.S50` now reddens when that happens rather than letting the intersection shrink silently, so the campaign inherits a guard rather than a trap.

Any new annual casilla bound to ledger IVA joins the parity gate automatically and must match its quarterly counterpart's category set. That is intended behaviour and a design constraint on the campaign, not an obstacle to route around.

consume it, which the close honesty review recorded as `FINDING-3`.
