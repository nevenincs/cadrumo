# Modelo 037 fetch-versus-defer decision

Closed the current-registry Modelo 037 rows as legally descoped.

## Decision

Do not register a current Modelo 037 declaration registry slice, do not
author a current `declaracion_pdf` profile, and do not add a current
round-trip fixture test.

Modelo 037 may be re-opened only as a separate historical pre-2025
slice, with explicit BOE/AEAT source scope and an authorised declaration
fixture. The current declaration extraction rollout should route the
simplified census path through Modelo 036.

## Legal grounding

BOE-A-2025-410 states that Orden HAC/1526/2024 suppresses Modelo 037
and that the order entered into force on 2025-02-03. It also amends
Orden EHA/1274/2007 so the order title and article 1 approve only
Modelo 036, suppresses articles 14, 15, and 16, and suppresses anexo II.

The consolidated BOE text for Orden EHA/1274/2007 reflects the same
state: the model 037 electronic-presentation article and the anexo II
form are marked suppressed from 2025-02-03.

AEAT still has an older Modelo 037 instruction PDF in the public corpus,
but that source is historical. It is not sufficient legal authority for
adding a current Modelo 037 registry/profile in a 2026 worktree.
