# Record in the campaign audit document that the sub-year decision record's finding of one layout-identical Modelo 303 revision pair was overtaken by an occupancy measurement, so the open-ended revision spans five design epochs rather than four and no pair may share a copied export fragment tree, and record beside it that the four-year prescripcion period scoping this plan is grounded on the tree's retention-floor constant rather than on bundled corpus text because Ley 58-2003 articles 66 and 67 are not bundled

## Scope

- `.vault/audit/`

## Description

- Cross-checked the sub-year decision record's revision-pair claim against
  the occupancy measurement the campaign's own authoring rows already
  carried.
- Confirmed Ley 58/2003 articles 66 and 67 are absent from the bundled
  corpus tree and that the per-period voluntary deadlines they are measured
  from, for Modelo 303 and Modelo 390, are present.
- Wrote both findings into the campaign audit document as one entry.

## Outcome

Recorded in the campaign audit document under the finding
`modelo-303-epoch-count-and-prescripcion-grounding-gap`. First: the sub-year
record's one-layout-identical-pair finding is overtaken; the open-ended
Modelo 303 revision spans five design epochs, not four, because the 2023 and
early-2024 designs are themselves not layout-identical (four Regimen
Simplificado employee-count slots real in one, reserved in the other at
unchanged sheet offsets), so no pair among the five may share a copied
export fragment tree. Second: the four-year prescripcion period scoping this
campaign's authoring window is grounded on the tree's own retention-floor
constant rather than on the BOE articles that establish it in law — Ley
58/2003 articles 66 and 67 are not bundled in the corpus, while the
per-period voluntary deadlines the window is measured from (Orden
EHA/3786/2008 art. 7 for Modelo 303, Orden EHA/3111/2009 art. 8 for Modelo
390) are. Recorded as an open honesty item rather than papered over.
