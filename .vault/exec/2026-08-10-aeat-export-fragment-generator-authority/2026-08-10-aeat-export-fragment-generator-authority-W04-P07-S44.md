## Scope

- `.vault/research/`

## Description

- Ground the decision through bounded VaultSpec RAG searches over the decision corpus and production code.
- Read the governing dual-key ADR, proposed simplified-regime ADR, official-form coverage audit, accepted casilla-schema canonical-derivations ADR, related research, reference, and templates in full.
- Amend the accepted dual-key ADR in place with one canonical ownership matrix and exact fixed-slot projection rules.

## Outcome

One accepted ADR now governs all M303 semantic homes and official fixed-slot projections. Calculation casillas, annual-summary endpoints, five activity-prorrata rows, two differentiated-sector rows, simplified activity/module rows, stable profile facts, filing-instance elections, presenter identity, secure accounts, and parser-owned literals each have one declared owner and one projection rule. Applicable incomplete values refuse export. No sibling ADR, duplicate official-box classifier, compatibility bridge, header default, or second aggregation mechanism is authorized.

The proposed simplified-regime ADR is `superseded`; the accepted casilla-schema derivation remains the sole official-box classifier and is not widened into a producer authority.
