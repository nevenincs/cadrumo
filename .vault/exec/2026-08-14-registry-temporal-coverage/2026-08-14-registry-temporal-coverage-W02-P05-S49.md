# Acquire and hash-pin distinct Modelo 721 2023 and 2024 technical contract packages with exact exercise applicability, then split or constrain law-selected revision scope without claiming unbounded technical-layout authority or promoting authority grade.

## Scope

- `src/cadrumo/_data/registry/aeat/modelos/721/`
- `src/cadrumo/_data/registry/aeat/legal/`
- `src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/aeat_official/disenos_registro/modelo_721/`
- `src/cadrumo/domain/calculations/registry/tests/`

## Description

- Verify the two bundled BOE Annex PDFs against their declared hashes and byte counts.
- Bound each form-spec source to its statutory exercise and split Modelo 721 into one law-selected revision per proven package.
- Retain applicability grade and empty export layouts; record that the unavailable historical AEAT SOAP/XML byte set is not claimed as a pair-complete contract.
- Move revision locale keys through the locale authority and update the filing-capability worklist wording.
- Add finite-era, selector-expansion, source-hash, and non-claim regressions.

## Outcome

Modelo 721 now selects the 2023 HFP/886 Annex package only for 2023 and the 2024 HAC/1504 substituted-Annex package only for 2024. Both PDFs remain hash-pinned in the canonical normative corpus. No 2025 selection, positional layout, serializer, AEAT SOAP/XML contract inventory, or authority-grade promotion is asserted.

## Notes

The currently published 2024 AEAT service material was inspected but not enrolled: the former official 2023 description endpoint no longer provides exact bytes. A mixed current-2024 and unavailable-2023 service package would not be an honest historical technical-contract pair.
