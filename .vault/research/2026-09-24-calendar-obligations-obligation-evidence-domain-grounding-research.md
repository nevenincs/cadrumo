# `calendar-obligations` research: `Obligation and filing-evidence domain grounding`

The distinctions a filing calendar and its reconciliation must preserve, as the official AEAT and BOE material frames them (researched 2026-09-21). The selected entity, taxpayer kind and period determine obligations; a view filter must not change legal applicability. Calendar year, return year and period, source-revision year, profile effective dates, filing time and evidence capture time are different coordinates.

## Findings

### Frequency depends on the taxpayer and the tax

AEAT distinguishes annual, quarterly and monthly obligations, and entity kind alone does not settle every regime or exemption.

### There is no universal date-shift rule

Filing and direct-debit cutoffs are distinct. Holiday treatment has specific exceptions, including Modelo 369, and some corporate deadlines are relative to the end of the entity's tax period. Published dates carry their jurisdiction and effective scope, and a date already adjusted by authority must not be shifted again.

### Cessation does not erase obligations

AEAT distinguishes individual cessation, partial cessation and inactive entities that are not legally extinguished; corporate obligations can continue. The annual IVA exemption depends on whether the final-period filing obligation exists (Modelo 390 instructions). Residual obligations due after cessation follow the relevant period's law.

### Payment proof and presentation proof differ

An NRC identifies a tax payment; obtaining it does not complete the declaration's presentation. AEAT's filed-declaration consultation separately provides declaration copies and presentation justificantes. Evidence is matched by its actual kind and coordinates, not by treating any PDF or reference code as a filed return.

### An overdue flag is not a penalty assessment

LGT article 27 covers voluntary late presentation without a prior requirement: the surcharge rises with completed months, and past twelve months different surcharge and interest treatment applies. Eligibility, exceptions and reductions matter. Sanctions, collection-period charges and interest are separate categories, and a zero, refund or informative declaration does not follow the same monetary rule. Display weeks are not statutory completed months.

### Notification access can have legal effects

AEAT distinguishes communications from notifications, their issue, service and read states, and access for represented persons. Opening a notification's content can establish service, and elapsed availability also has legal consequences. Listing metadata, opening content, acknowledging and responding are different permissions. A response deadline is not derived from the issue date alone, and a notification template's "modelo" is not a tax-return Modelo.
