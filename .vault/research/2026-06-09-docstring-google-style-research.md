# `docstring-google-style` research: investigation backing the decision

This research captures the investigation that backed the `docstring-google-style` ADR.

## Findings

The investigation surveyed the existing docstring styles in src/aeat and confirmed Google style as the convention best supported by the Sphinx and autodoc pipeline already in use. It scoped the enforcement gate the ADR adopts.
