# `public-product-modelo-naming` research: `Public product modelo naming`

Make modelo interoperability explicit without posing as the authority.

## Findings

- Use `neve modelos` as the clear product label while clearance remains open.
- Use `Modelario` only as a product-world candidate and not as a protectable master mark.
- Place `Modelo 130`, `Modelo 303`, and `Modelo 390` in the interface and product copy.
- State `Independent software for Spanish tax models` beside the product label.
- State the non-affiliation disclaimer beside every necessary agency reference.
- Reject `Modera`, `Modelia`, `Modario`, `Formera`, and `Pliego` after the live collision screen.
- Treat `Modeluna` as an uncommitted creative seed and not a cleared name.

## Naming frame

- Write `neve · modelos` in the visual identity.
- Write `Modelos tributarios, en local` as the immediate descriptor.
- Write `Para Modelo 130, 303 y 390` as the factual proof line.
