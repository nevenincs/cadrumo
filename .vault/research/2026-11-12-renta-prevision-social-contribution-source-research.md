# `renta-prevision-social-contribution-source` research: `LIRPF arts. 51 to 53 pension-reduction limits, excess carry and order by year 2020 to 2025`

Question: the exact rules for the reduction for contributions to social-welfare systems (arts. 51, 52, DA 16 LIRPF) at devengo 31 December of each year 2020-2025. Conclusion: the 30% limit uses art. 19 net work income (after art. 18, BEFORE art. 20) plus activity net income BEFORE art. 32 reductions, in every year. The absolute limit is 8.000 (2020), 2.000 + 8.000 employer increment (2021), 1.500 + 8.500 (2022), and 1.500 + 8.500 with the Ley 31/2022 coefficient table and a 4.250 autonomo increment inside the 8.500 (2023-2025). A pension-reduction remainder never reduces the savings base. Not investigated: art. 53 (disability systems), DA 11 (deportistas), Reglamento art. 51 and DT 19 read directly (only through the manual), Anexo C row numbers 1284-1315.

Manual paths below abbreviate `X:/code/cadrumo-wt-calculators/src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/manuals/renta/{Y}/part1/source.pdf.extracted.md` as `M{Y}:line`.

## Findings

### Rules common to all six years

- 30% base: "rendimientos netos del trabajo y de actividades economicas percibidos individualmente". Work: art. 19 net, i.e. integro after the art. 18 reduction minus deductible expenses (so before art. 20). Activity: net before art. 32 reductions, including attributed income from entidades en atribucion when the partner actually carries on the activity. M2020:30297-30307, M2021:31889-31899, M2022:34210-34220, M2023:40268-40279, M2025:40355-40366.
- Limits apply individually to each participant in the unidad familiar. M2020:30266, M2025:40330.
- Collective dependency insurance paid by the employer (seguros colectivos de dependencia, SCD): own, independent 5.000 limit, separate from and additional to the general limit. M2020:30263-30265, M2025:40325-40329, 40423-40427. From 2022 the empresario individual's own SCD contributions count as employer contributions (M2022:34248-34250); the 2025 manual adds that the autonomo cannot use the independent 5.000 (M2025:40345-40347).
- Private dependency insurance (art. 51.5): within the general limits, plus a per-beneficiary cap on all payers together: 8.000 (2020, M2020:30313-30319), 2.000 (2021, M2021:31920-31931), 1.500 (2022-2025, M2022:34257-34263, M2025:40444-40450). BOE notes: 51.5 amended by Ley 11/2020 (effect 2021) and Ley 22/2021 (effect 2022).
- Carry (art. 52.2): only amounts not reduced for insufficient base imponible general or for the 30% limit carry to the five following years, on request in the return. Amounts above the annual maximum contribution (art. 51.6 / DA 16 financial limit) never reduce and never carry; they may be withdrawn before 30 June of the next year. M2020:30341-30354; BOE art. 52.2 (the carry "no se aplicara a las aportaciones que superen los limites maximos del apartado 6 del articulo 51").
- Carried excess is applied first, before current-year contributions, under the same limits. M2020:30283-30285, M2021:31957-31959, M2025:40505-40508.
- Order against the bases (art. 50): reductions apply to the base imponible general only, in the order art. 84 (joint), art. 51, art. 53, art. 54, art. 55, DA 11; the base cannot become negative. Only the remainders of the joint-taxation reduction and of pensiones compensatorias (art. 55) reduce the savings base. A pension (art. 51/52) remainder does NOT reduce the base imponible del ahorro; it carries under art. 52.2 instead. M2025:39707-39722, 41619-41621, 41794-41796; BOE art. 50.
- Spouse (art. 51.7): reduces the contributor's base imponible general for contributions to the spouse's systems when the spouse has no net work plus activity income or less than 8.000; outside the taxpayer's own joint limit (M2025:41759-41765); cannot make the base liquidable negative; any unreduced excess is carried by the spouse who is participant, not by the contributor (M2025:40928-40932). Cap 2.500 in 2020 (M2020:30431-30442); 1.000 from 2021 (M2021:766-770 "Se reduce de 2.500 a 1.000"; M2021:32153-32156; M2025:40903-40910). BOE note: 51.7 amended by Ley 11/2020, effect 1 January 2021.

### 2020

- Absolute limit: 8.000 total (art. 52.1.b), plus 5.000 SCD. Annual financial maximum 8.000 under art. 5.3 TRLPFP. M2020:30252-30256, 30308-30309.
- No employer/worker split; section 6.2 names no casilla. Excess window 2015-2019, Anexo C.2. M2020:30355-30364.
- When the 30% or base limit binds, the 2020 worked example distributes the allowed amount proportionally between pension contributions and SCD premiums. M2020:30420-30427.

### 2021

- Absolute limit: 2.000, increased by 8.000 only when the increase comes from employer contributions. The empresario individual's own contributions to plans or mutualidades where he is promoter and participant, or to PPSE/SCD where he is tomador and asegurado, count as employer contributions. Worker contributions to an employment plan fall inside the 2.000. M2021:31836-31861, 31900-31914. BOE: art. 52.1 amended by Ley 11/2020 art. 62.2, effect 1 January 2021.
- Excess 2016-2020 (DT 19 RIRPF): treated as employer contributions up to the employer contributions imputed in those years; the rest is the taxpayer's. The current-year (2021) excess is split in proportion to taxpayer and employer amounts. M2021:31960-31982, 31998-32017. Reported in Anexos C.2 and C.3. M2021:32023-32028.

### 2022

- Absolute limit: 1.500, increased by 8.500 when the increase comes from employer contributions or from worker contributions to the same instrument up to the employer contribution (1:1), per system and per employer individually. Employer amounts arising from the worker's decision count as worker contributions. No separate 4.250 autonomo increment yet: the empresario individual's own contributions count as employer contributions inside the 8.500. M2022:34148-34182, 34221-34245. BOE: Ley 22/2021 art. 59.2, effect 1 January 2022.
- Excess 2017-2021: RD 1039/2022 removed the proportional split. Excess applies under the full increased limit (1.500 + 8.500 = 10.000) regardless of origin; SCD excess under its own 5.000. Oldest year first; within one year, pension contributions before SCD premiums (2022 manual: "se podran", the taxpayer may choose SCD first). The 30% limit or the base is the global cap. M2022:34317-34363. Current-year excess carries under the 10.000 total. M2022:34378-34381. Anexos C.3 and C.4. M2022:34382-34392.
- Casillas named by the manual: 0465 worker contributions (other than 0438/0426); 0438 company amounts from the worker's decision; 0426 worker contributions to an employment plan with employer contributions; 0427 employer contributions except SCD and empresario individual; 0499 empresario individual/professional own contributions; 0466 SCD. M2022:34285-34302.

### 2023, 2024, 2025

- Absolute limit: 1.500 plus up to 8.500 from (first) employer contributions, or worker contributions to the same system up to this table. Worker gross work income from that employer up to 60.000: employer contribution up to 500 gives x 2,5; 500,01 to 1.500 gives 1.250 + 0,25 x (contribution minus 500); over 1.500 gives x 1. Over 60.000: x 1 always. Computed per employer and per system individually. M2023:40150-40196, 40283-40303; M2025:40229-40276, 40370-40395.
- Second increment, 4.250 for autonomos: sectoral plans (art. 67.1.a TRLPFP), simplified plans for autonomos (art. 67.1.c), own contributions of the empresario individual or professional to employment plans he promotes, to mutualidades, to PPSE. Not multipliable across instruments (M2025:40279-40282). The first and second increments together cap at 8.500, so the general maximum is 10.000. M2023:40197-40240, 40309-40326; M2024:44517, 44630; M2025:40277-40324, 40396-40422.
- Third: plus 5.000 SCD. M2025:40423-40427.
- BOE: art. 52.1 amended by Ley 12/2022 DF 1.1 and then by Ley 31/2022 art. 62.1. The AEAT novedades page states the Ley 12/2022 changes apply "con efectos desde el 1 de enero de 2023", with a 2,5 / 2 / 1,5 / 1 coefficient table. The table in force for 2023 is the Ley 31/2022 one shown in M2023. The Ley 31/2022 effect date is confirmed only through the 2023 manual; the BOE note shows no date (unconfirmed on BOE).
- Excess windows: 2018-2022 (M2023:40398-40455), 2019-2023 (M2024:44728 onward), 2020-2024 (M2025:40489-40550). Same RD 1039/2022 ordering as 2022, now worded "se reduciran, en primer lugar, las aportaciones y contribuciones empresariales" before SCD (M2025:40533-40537). Anexo C.3 in 2025 (M2025:40564-40574).
- Casillas named: same six as 2022, with 0499 reworded to "trabajadores por cuenta propia o autonomos, empresarios individuales o profesionales". M2023:40377-40397; M2025:40473-40488.

### Casilla mapping beyond the manual (unconfirmed)

- Section 6.2 of the manual names no casilla for 2020-2021, and never names 0463, 0464, 0467, 0468 or 0469. The registry (`X:/code/cadrumo-wt-calculators/src/cadrumo/_data/registry/aeat/modelos/100/revisions/2020/casillas/0001-declarations.toml:3196-3241`) assigns 0463 excess 2015-2019, 0464 SCD excess, 0465 worker, 0466 SCD, 0467 sum of amounts with right to reduce, 0468 total reduction, 0469 spouse reduction. 0426/0427 enter in 2021 (`.../2021/casillas/0001-declarations.toml:153,162`) and 0438/0499 in 2022 (`.../2022/casillas/0001-declarations.toml:49,56`). These are registry claims, not verified against the official design here.
- 0492/0493 are not pension casillas in the manual: it cites them as the 2015 state and regional tramo of a deduction (M2025:53516-53518). 0488/0489 are the deportista mutualidad excess (M2025:41546-41548).
- Anexo C rows 1284-1315: not found in the manual; unconfirmed.

## Rule x year

| Rule | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Percent limit (art. 19 work + pre-art. 32 activity) | 30% | 30% | 30% | 30% | 30% | 30% |
| General absolute | 8.000 | 2.000 | 1.500 | 1.500 | 1.500 | 1.500 |
| Employer increment | none | +8.000 employer only | +8.500 employer, or worker up to employer | +8.500 coefficient table | same | same |
| Autonomo 4.250 (inside 8.500) | no | no (own = employer) | no (own = employer) | yes | yes | yes |
| Max general total | 8.000 | 10.000 | 10.000 | 10.000 | 10.000 | 10.000 |
| SCD own limit | 5.000 | 5.000 | 5.000 | 5.000 | 5.000 | 5.000 |
| Private dependency per beneficiary | 8.000 | 2.000 | 1.500 | 1.500 | 1.500 | 1.500 |
| Spouse cap / spouse income below | 2.500 / 8.000 | 1.000 / 8.000 | same | same | same | same |
| Carry 5 years (base or 30% only) | yes | yes | yes | yes | yes | yes |
| Excess split rule | proportional | DT 19 + proportional | oldest first, pension before SCD | same | same | same |
| Remainder to savings base | no | no | no | no | no | no |
