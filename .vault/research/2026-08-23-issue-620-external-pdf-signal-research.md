# `issue-620-external-pdf-signal` research: `external PDF evidence boundary`

The question is what parser evidence may honestly be derived from tax-form PDFs downloaded from a private third-party host. The bytes are useful adversarial inputs because they were not generated by this repository, but their hosting chain, branding and mutable PDF metadata cannot establish AEAT authenticity or numeric authority. The useful boundary is therefore physical-byte and layout observation, with source authority left unproved.

## Findings

### The source catalogue is discovery evidence, not authority

FiscalBot describes itself as private and unofficial while offering plain and fillable files for M130, M131, M303, M036 and M349. The page establishes where the bytes were obtained, not who authored or rendered them: https://fiscalbot.es/modelos-tributarios/.

### Physical inspection yields falsifiable facts even when authorship is unknown

SHA-256, byte length, PDF version, page geometry, encryption, AcroForm shape, extracted tokens and identity-pattern scans are reproducible properties of the downloaded bytes. The declaration parser's blank-box guard operates on those token and geometry properties in `src/cadrumo/adapters/inbound/declaracion/_parser.py:580`; an external blank layout can therefore catch a fabricated box-number value without being regulatory authority.

### Metadata is inconsistent across the candidate set

The inspected candidates report multiple producer toolchains, including AEAT-labelled metadata and third-party PDF editors. That variation rules out classifying the catalogue or filename family as one provenance class. Each byte stream needs its own inventory outcome, and a claimed AEAT producer remains an observation rather than authentication.

### Three options remain

Treating every candidate as real AEAT evidence maximizes apparent coverage but makes an unauthenticated claim. Rejecting every candidate preserves authority purity but throws away independent parser-adversarial layouts. Retaining the bytes as unverified external layout candidates preserves the useful signal while keeping populated-value placement and AEAT authenticity explicitly unproved. The evidence favors the third option.

### Uninvestigated boundary

Redistribution rights were not established by the source pages. The implementing work must not infer a licence from public download availability; if repository distribution requires a separate licence ruling, it remains a release or legal review input rather than a provenance fact.
