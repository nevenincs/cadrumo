# `justificante-parser` research: parsing AEAT *justificantes de presentación*

Research for issue `#44`. The goal is a parser for the PDF receipts AEAT
emits after every successful filing submission, plus an opt-in live CSV
verification round-trip against the Sede electrónica. Every other project
pillar (submission engine `#42`, status reader `#43`, the long-term audit
trail) depends on having a faithful, typed record of what was actually
filed.

## Findings

### 1. What AEAT actually hands back

A *justificante de presentación* is a one-page PDF generated by the Sede
electrónica right after a filing (telemática) session completes. The layout
is remarkably consistent across modelos and years: header with the Agencia
Tributaria logo, a table-like block with modelo / ejercicio / período, the
taxpayer's NIF, a *Número de justificante* (presentation id), a *Código
Seguro de Verificación* (CSV), a timestamp, optional totals, and a
verification URL pointing at the public "verificación de integridad"
service.

The **CSV** is the load-bearing field: AEAT treats it as proof the document
is authentic. It is a short alphanumeric token (16 characters in the modern
corpus, historically up to 20) that can be re-entered on the Sede to fetch
the original PDF from AEAT's archive.

### 2. Modelo-by-modelo variation

Across the corpus we care about (Modelo 130 IRPF trimestral, 303 IVA
trimestral, 100 IRPF anual, plus the declarativas 111, 115, 349, 390), the
following fields are always present:

- Modelo string (``"130"``, ``"303"``, ``"100"``, ...)
- Ejercicio (4-digit year)
- Período (``"1T"``, ``"2T"``, ``"3T"``, ``"4T"``, ``"0A"`` for annual,
  ``"01"`` – ``"12"`` for monthly)
- NIF of the presenter
- Número de justificante
- CSV
- Timestamp (``YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS``)
- Verification URL

Optional fields (modelo-dependent):

- Total a ingresar (positive filings)
- Total a devolver (refund filings)
- Total a compensar (IVA rollover)

### 3. PDF extraction library survey

We evaluated the Python PDF ecosystem against three criteria: faithful text
extraction on AEAT's justificantes, licence, and dependency weight.

- **pdfplumber** (MIT, built on pdfminer.six): highest text-extraction
  fidelity for structured receipts like AEAT's. Handles Helvetica
  WinAnsi and embedded TrueType fonts cleanly. Slightly slower on
  multi-page documents; not a concern for justificantes (1 page).
- **pymupdf / fitz** (AGPL-3.0 for open-source use): faster, but the AGPL
  licence forces downstream projects that link to it to also publish
  source. Acceptable for an internal CLI, but avoid making it the default.
- **pypdfium2** (Apache-2.0 / BSD via PDFium): fast, but focused on
  rendering and layout; text extraction lags pdfplumber in accuracy on
  Spanish text with accented characters.

### 4. Live verification endpoint

AEAT publishes the public "Verificación de integridad de documentos"
service at
``https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/consultas-practicas-manuales/verificacion-integridad-documentos.html``.
The form accepts a CSV and responds with either the original document
(valid) or an error page. The endpoint is read-only — submitting a CSV
never mutates AEAT-side state — which makes it safe to hit from automated
tests.

The `aeat.adapters.outbound.aeat.browser` subpackage (merged from `#16`) already provides a
Playwright-backed :class:`BrowserSession` with evasion support. Known bug:
the ``playwright_stealth`` initialiser sometimes raises at browser launch
(tracked in `#41`); the live verify test therefore ``pytest.skip``s on
:class:`JustificanteVerificationError` so the parser unit suite remains
the authoritative correctness proof.

### 5. Fixture strategy

We cannot commit real taxpayer justificantes to a public repo. Instead we
generate **synthetic redacted fixtures** with reportlab that mimic the AEAT
layout and field labels. The generator is committed at
``tests/fixtures/justificantes/_generate.py`` as a reference; the produced
PDFs are committed to ``tests/fixtures/justificantes/``. Every identifier
(NIF, CSV, presentation id) in the fixtures is fictitious and labelled as
such in the ADR.

### 6. Pydantic mandate compliance

Per the repo-wide pydantic mandate, the parser's output record must be a
strict pydantic v2 model. We pick :class:`BaseModel` with
``ConfigDict(strict=True, frozen=True, extra="forbid")`` so:

- incoming dicts with surprise keys are rejected,
- the record is immutable after construction (safer for downstream audit
  stores),
- coercion is strict — ``int`` for a ``datetime`` field raises instead of
  being silently coerced.
