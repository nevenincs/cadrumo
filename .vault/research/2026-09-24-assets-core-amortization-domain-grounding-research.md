# `assets-core` research: `Amortization domain grounding`

The IRPF, IVA and accounting distinctions that asset and amortization work must keep apart, as the official sources frame them (researched 2026-09-21). These are scoping rules and independent-oracle guidance, not a claim that every condition is implemented. Provision, source year, effective date and taxpayer applicability must be preserved when enrolling authority.

## Findings

### Three objects, not one

Statutory depreciation tables, the generated schedule for one asset, and the recorded depreciation history are different things. Professionals and non-commercial businesses must record each asset's annual charge in the investment-goods book. An IVA regularization register does not satisfy that IRPF requirement.

### Tables depend on the regime

Direct simplified estimation uses the specific linear table under RIRPF article 30 and the Order of 27 March 1998: buildings have a 3% maximum coefficient and a 68-year maximum period. The normal table distinguishes commercial, administrative and service buildings and dwellings at 2% over 100 years. Both are legal limits, not two maxima to apply together. Territorial regimes, special conditions and genuine temporal changes need explicit selection; unchanged authority should not be copied per year or region.

### Deductibility is not an expense-or-asset flag

Ordinary depreciation needs the right depreciable base and an effective charge. Separately, new tangible assets with a unit value of at most EUR 300 can use free depreciation (LIS article 12.3), subject to the EUR 25,000 per-period limit and its conditions. Immediate depreciation does not erase the asset or allow a second deduction of the same cost. This threshold is not IVA's and does not apply in every regime.

### Acquisition is not necessarily the depreciation start

The base includes acquisition or production costs and qualifying readiness costs, excludes residual value where applicable, and depreciation starts when the asset is ready for operation. Used assets have their own conditions. Acquisition, business-affectation and in-service dates stay separate where the rules need them.

### A home office needs more than a percentage

IRPF partial affectation concerns a separately usable part of a divisible asset (LIRPF article 29, RIRPF article 22). Home ownership expenses, including depreciation, use the business portion and ownership treatment; common spousal property has special rules. The household-utilities percentage does not apply to depreciation.

### Land and construction differ

Building depreciation excludes the land. RIS article 3 gives a cadastral allocation when land value is unknown, with an evidence-based market-value alternative. Residential rental-income depreciation is a separate treatment, even though both mention 3%.

### IVA is a separate dimension

IVA investment-goods classification concerns durable productive assets and excludes goods below EUR 3,005.06, among other exclusions. That threshold is not an IRPF expensing allowance. IVA deductibility needs its own eligibility and use evidence; a home-office IVA allocation is not automatically the IRPF allocation or the prorrata.

### IVA regularization is not amortization

AEAT describes regularization over four subsequent calendar years, or nine for land and buildings, measured from acquisition or later first use, with a greater-than-ten-point deduction-percentage condition for the annual regularization. Use changes and disposals have their own provisions, and delayed first use needs testing.

### Cost and deductible IVA must reconcile

PGC NRV 12 includes non-deductible input IVA in acquisition cost, but later prorrata or capital-goods regularizations do not change the initial valuation. Recoverable IVA stays out of the depreciable cost, and the schedule's cost is not rewritten each time IVA regularizes.

### Modelo 130 uses cumulative depreciation

The Modelo 130 expense casilla includes deductible depreciation from the start of the year to the quarter end. Annual Modelo 100 needs the annual activity result, not the sum of four cumulative Modelo 130 expense totals, so an oracle must distinguish quarterly increments, year-to-date charges and annual totals.
