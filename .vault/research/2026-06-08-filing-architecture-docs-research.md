# `filing-architecture-docs` research: investigation backing the decision

This research captures the investigation that backed the `filing-architecture-docs` ADR.

## Findings

A discovery and audit sweep inventoried the AEAT text-filing surfaces and their documentation coverage. It found gaps in lifecycle coverage and group-specific identity terminology, and established the generalized-terminology and persona-tutorial approach the ADR adopts.
