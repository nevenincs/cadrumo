# `public-product-naming` research: `Public product naming`

Separate the public brand from the AEAT agency acronym.

## Findings

- Reject `AEAT` as the public brand.
- Keep `aeat-cli` as the technical package name until a migration is approved.
- Use `AEAT` only as a factual compatibility reference.
- State independent status beside every necessary agency reference.
- Avoid agency names, public emblems, official-style marks, and official-looking domains.
- Use a coined word mark and a descriptive support line.
- Keep `software fiscal`, `impuestos`, and `modelos` out of the primary mark.
- Clear Spanish, international, and EU marks before public use or filing.
- Reject `Nifora`, `Tramora`, `Fidara`, and `Lindera` after the live collision screen.

## Creative routes

- Explore an evidence route around traceable records and proof.
- Explore a boundary route around calm, controlled hand-off.
- Explore a workflow route around clear progress through complex work.
- Pair the chosen mark with `Local-first Spanish tax workflows`.

## Provisional recommendation

- Carry `Orriva` to formal clearance first.
- Retain `Cauvia`, `Ceredo`, and `Cifrona` as reserve candidates.
- Treat the collision screen as research and not clearance.

## Guardrail copy

- Use `Independent tax-record software. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for the Agencia Tributaria.`
