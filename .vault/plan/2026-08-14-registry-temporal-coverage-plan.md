# `registry-temporal-coverage` plan

Land the grade-bound coverage contract, remove the three-layer per-modelo schema divergence, place enforcement where it measurably executes, and migrate the corpus with one-shot tooling.

## Description

This L3 plan executes `2026-08-14-registry-temporal-coverage-authority-grade-coverage-adr`, grounded in the measured inventory of `2026-08-14-registry-temporal-coverage-load-topology-reference`. Wave W01 defines the typed grade and coverage contract and removes the per-modelo divergence (the modelo-named snapshot field and authority branch, the uncached annual-orden parse work, the applicability literals, the published raw-loader facade surface). Wave W02 installs enforcement at the surfaces measurement proved execute in both load regimes and unifies the ungoverned coverage ledger onto one derived matrix and one filing-eligibility predicate. Wave W03 migrates the corpus through one-shot `dev/` programs and closes with the owned-tree sweep and the advisory-to-blocking flip.

Two external dependencies gate specific rows and are marked on them: the export-fragment campaign (`2026-08-10-aeat-export-fragment-generator-authority-plan`) owns the `modelos/303/` and `modelos/390/` authoring trees through its S84 and S85 rows, so every row touching those trees is blocked until that campaign closes; and grade promotion is an operator attestation, so the promotion row is blocked on operator application of the derived proposals. No row in this plan writes an operator review stamp or raises a grade mechanically.

Deletion inventory. Every replacement in this plan is delete-not-bridge per the no-legacy rule: the old surface and its tests are removed in the same step that lands the canonical one, never kept as a fallback, alias or compatibility path. What dies, by step: the `m303_annual_orden` field on `RegistrySnapshot` and `RegistryCatalogues` and the `Modelo.M303` branch in `_authority.py` (S04); the applicability rule literals for unowned modelos (S09) and the remaining 303 and 390 literals plus the literal-carrying mechanism itself (S19); the raw loader names from the facade `__all__` (S10); any of the six never-executing validator modules found dead (S21); every regulatory data embed the classifier finds in the modelo-specific modules, with `_m303_orden_constants.py` data (`SUPPORTED_EJERCICIOS`, the seasonal index coefficients, the difficult-justification percentage, the Lorca 2022 reduction) already confirmed as one, replaced by registry declarations and deleted (S22, S23, owned-tree remainder S19); every Python-resident supported-year set, replaced by the registry supported-filing-years catalogue (S24); `_representative_year` and the single-year assessment path (S13); the duplicated filing-eligibility predicate and the by-construction-empty `filing_gaps` surface (S14); the prose grade-marker comments once transcribed (S15); and the one-shot migration programs themselves (S20). What survives deliberately, so executors do not over-delete: modules the S22 classifier records as machinery, with their justification, including whatever of the `_m303_orden_*` parsing modules and the `M303AnnualOrdenAuthority` type remains after their data content is extracted, because migrating the annual-orden content wholesale into authoring-tree TOML is a named open question the ADR leaves to the operator; and the 61 modules that execute in neither load regime but belong to post-load surfaces, out of scope except the six validators S21 classifies. A step that leaves a second implementation, a re-export bridge, a commented-out block or an orphaned test behind is not done, and its executor states in the step record which of these inventory entries the step consumed.
