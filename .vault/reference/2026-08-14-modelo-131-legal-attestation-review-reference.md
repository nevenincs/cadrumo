# `modelo-131-legal-attestation-review` reference: `Modelo 131 legal-reference attestation review packet`

## Summary

This packet covers 25 registry references cited by Modelo 131 across its 2019–2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026
revisions. Each key is the registry/corpus locator. Summaries retain the operative condition, value or threshold
needed by an implementation, effective-year distinction, M131 dependency and historical review stamp.

The broader worklist had 169 unique references across six modelos; 25 belong to M131 and five also appear in
M100. Twenty-four of these 25 references contain numeric law, and 23 carry agent-authored self-verification
claims (22 are both numeric and self-verified). Every one is agent-authored; none is operator-approved. All 25
keys resolve and their required-text checks pass the production normalizer. That is structural integrity only,
not legal content review.

## Use and limits

Choose the law/order version effective for the filing year. The bundled corpus article and registry key below
are the source locators. Condensed notes preserve implementation-relevant rules; consult the actual keyed
