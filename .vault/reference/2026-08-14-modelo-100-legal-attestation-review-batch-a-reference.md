# `modelo-100-legal-attestation-review-batch-a` reference: `Modelo 100 legal-reference attestation review packet, Batch A (restamp-ready tranche)`

## Summary

This packet condenses 49 of the 119 registry reference IDs present across six Modelo 100 revisions (2020–2025).
Each numbered key is the registry/corpus locator. The entries retain the operative interpretation,
effective-year distinction, Modelo 100 dependency, and historical agent review date.

The production normalizer was run against each record: all 49 keys resolve and all declared required-text
phrases pass. This demonstrates structural alignment only. Every reviewed_by/review_status claim in this packet
is agent-authored; no operator independently checked these items against live BOE/AEAT or a manual. The packet
is not legal certification, and a passing text check cannot attest that the interpretation or number is legally
correct.

Thirty-four entries carry numeric claims. Their bundled corpus text is preferred evidence, not infallible
numeric authority. Two higher-risk self-attestations are ley-35-2006:art-63-2015 (state-scale rates/brackets)
and HAC1347-2024 instruction 2.1's agricultural worked example, €18,030 × 0.37 = €6,671.10. Neither received
independent live-source confirmation here.

Five annual form-approval orders are procedural references. Four have no direct Modelo 100 dependency. Orden
HAC-242-2025:art-8 drives the declaration deadline/application link, not a casilla. Nothing in this packet
changes a modelo, operator_reviewed stamp, or registry authority.

the registry remain the primary locators. These condensed statements are orientation for implementation; confirm
