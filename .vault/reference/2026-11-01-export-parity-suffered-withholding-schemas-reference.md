# `export-parity` reference: `suffered withholding schemas`

Grounds the persisted schemas for the suffered-withholding evidence family, the credit-casilla reconciliation finding and the operator resolution record (plan Steps P03.S17, S18, S06, P04.S19, S20 of `2026-11-01-export-parity-plan`) in the code that already does each part. Pinned to worktree `X:\code\cadrumo-renta-withholding`, branch `feature/renta-withholding`, HEAD `712dc10d84`, read 2026-11-01. Official grounding comes from the bundled AEAT Modelo 100 dictionaries and the bundled consolidated LIRPF and RIRPF texts. Code discovery ran without semantic search: an orchestrator context pack, then targeted grep and whole-file reads of each epicenter.

## Summary

### Credit casillas against the official dictionaries

The registry's Modelo 100 casillas live at the 2020 storage baseline (`src/cadrumo/_data/registry/aeat/modelos/100/revisions/2020/casillas/0001-declarations.toml:4099-4210`). 2021 to 2025 inherit them through `casilla_storage_baseline` and per-casilla overrides (`src/cadrumo/_data/registry/aeat/modelos/100/revisions/2022/revision.toml:2276-2322`, `2025/revision.toml:2404-2449`). The official individual-declaration dictionaries are bundled at `src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/aeat_official/disenos_registro/modelo_100/files/`: file 04 is 2020, 05 is 2021, 06 is 2022, 07 is 2023, 08 is 2024 and 01 is 2025. Each dictionary field states its XML path, type, casilla and official description. The 2022 locators are given, and the other years carry the same descriptions at the shifted lines listed.

- 0591, CLDED6 in CuotaAutoliquidacionRes (2022 line 891): retenciones no practicadas que tienen la consideración de deducibles. The registry role `irpf_retenciones_consideradas_practicadas` matches. It is not part of 0609.
- 0592, 0593, 0594 and 0600 are RET5A to RET5D (2022 lines 897-900, with summaries at 478-481): attribution of withholding on capital mobiliario, capital inmobiliario, actividades económicas and ganancias patrimoniales. Each is the sum of a per-entity casilla (1597, 1598, 1599, 1600). The `irpf_retencion_atribuida_*` roles match.
- 0595, CAUTLIQ (2022 line 892): cuota resultante de la autoliquidación, [0587] - [0588] - [0589] - [0590] - [0591], with [0414] inserted in 2025 (2025 line 958). The role `irpf_cuota_resultante_autoliquidacion` is CORRECT. 0595 is not a credit casilla, and 0609 excludes it by design. The suspicion came from treating 0592 to 0606 as one contiguous credit range.
- 0596, RET1 (line 893): por rendimientos del trabajo. `irpf_retencion_trabajo` matches.
- 0597, RET2 (line 894): por rendimientos del capital mobiliario. `irpf_retencion_capital_mobiliario` matches.
- 0598 carries one number on TWO dictionary fields. InmueblesRes/CRETING (2022 line 246, type N102) is the "suma de retenciones e ingresos a cuenta (suma de las casillas [0153])". RetencionesRes/RET3 (line 895, type P102) is the credit "por arrendamientos de inmuebles urbanos". From 2020 to 2024 the registry names the summary occurrence, with role `irpf_suma_retenciones_capital_inmobiliario_aggregate` and section `inmuebles_res` (2020 casillas line 4146-4151). Only the 2025 override corrects the role to `irpf_retencion_arrendamientos_inmuebles_urbanos` and the section to the credit block (`2025/revision.toml:2416-2419`). MISMATCH for 2020 to 2024. The 2025 correction belongs at the post-rectification baseline. The casilla is computed as a copy of 0153 (`2022/formulas/0001-declarations.toml:383-390`), and 0153 is the per-inmueble retención (`2020/casillas/0001-declarations.toml:1154-1158`). The 2024 manual includes urban rentals "constituya o no actividad económica" in this class (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/manuals/renta/2024/part1/source.pdf.extracted.md:58480`, page 1490). The "capital inmobiliario" wording is therefore also narrower than the official class.
- 0599, RET4 (line 896): por rendimientos de actividades económicas. `irpf_retencion_actividades_economicas` matches. The manual excludes urban rentals from it (same page).
- 0601, RET6 (line 901): imputados por AIE y UTE. `irpf_retencion_imputada_aie_ute` matches.
- 0602, RET7 (line 902): ingresos a cuenta del artículo 92.8. `irpf_ingreso_cuenta_art_92_8` matches. LIRPF art. 92 is the image-rights imputation, and 92.8 is the payer's ingreso a cuenta (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/normatives/html/ley-35-2006.html:8151`, block a92). The casilla cites only `ley-35-2006:art-99`.
- 0603, RET8 (line 903): por ganancias patrimoniales, incluidos premios. Role matches.
- 0604, RET9 (line 904): pagos fraccionados ingresados. Role matches. It is the relation channel, not this family.
- 0605, RET10 (line 905): cuotas IRNR (LIRPF art. 99.8). 0606, RET11 (line 906): Directiva 2003/48. Both roles match. Neither is a per-payer income class.
- 0609, PAGOS (line 907): [0592] + [0593] + [0594] + [0596] a [0606]. It matches the 2020 formula operands (`2020/formulas/0001-declarations.toml:2585-2601`).

### Law the record fields rest on

- LIRPF art. 99.1: retenciones, ingresos a cuenta and pagos fraccionados are all pagos a cuenta. Art. 99.5: the perceptor computes the gross consideration and, where withholding was short for a cause attributable only to the payer, deducts what should have been withheld. Art. 99.6: "Cuando exista obligación de ingresar a cuenta, se presumirá que dicho ingreso ha sido efectuado. El contribuyente incluirá en la base imponible la valoración de la retribución en especie ... y el ingreso a cuenta, salvo que le hubiera sido repercutido." Source: `src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/normatives/html/ley-35-2006.html:8720` (block a99; sentences at `:8738`, `:8741`). The repercusión decides only whether the ingreso a cuenta enters the base imponible, through LIRPF art. 43.2 (`ley-35-2006.html:5478`, block a43) and the 2024 manual (`source.pdf.extracted.md:8995-9011`, page 213). The text does not condition the deduction on it. Repercutido is therefore a separate field from ingreso a cuenta, not a subtraction from the credit.
- RIRPF art. 108.3: the payer issues a certificate of the withholding practised or the ingresos a cuenta made, available before the Renta period opens (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/normatives/html/rd-439-2007.html:5978`, sentence at `:6001`). That is the evidence the `payer_certificate` origin denotes. RIRPF art. 108.2.i: the annual summary states "retención practicada o ingreso a cuenta efectuado" per perceptor, which is what datos fiscales later reflect. RIRPF art. 76 lists who withholds (`rd-439-2007.html:4347`). RIRPF art. 75 lists which income (`:4249`). Together they bound the class vocabulary with the manual list (`source.pdf.extracted.md:58476-58492`).

### Encrypted namespace pattern

- A namespace contract is one `SecureObjectNamespaceDefinition`: key, namespace, owner, sensitivity, schema_version, object_key_grammar, scope, custody_disposition and remote mirror policy (`src/cadrumo/adapters/persistence/storage/secure_object_namespaces.py:60-90`). A templated grammar is checked only for traversal (`:144-185`). Ciphertext mirroring requires revision and integrity metadata (`:206-217`). Every namespace is enrolled in one registry tuple that refuses duplicate keys and values (`src/cadrumo/adapters/persistence/storage/namespace_registry.py:103`, `:119`, `:152`; duplicate check at `secure_object_namespaces.py:228-241`). Scope and custody enums are at `src/cadrumo/adapters/persistence/storage/namespace_taxonomy.py:28-50`.
- The closest analogue is the payer-side `WITHHOLDING_OBSERVATIONS_NAMESPACE`, which is FINANCIAL, PROFILE_LOCAL, STRUCTURED_CUSTODY and v1, with key grammar `{modelo}:{filing_year}:{period}:{sha256(perceptor_tax_id)}:{clave}:{subclave}` (`secure_object_namespaces.py:465-476`). The key builder hashes the canonical tax-identity token with sha256 and keeps non-identifying codes plain (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/percepciones_observations_repository.py:29-75`). The natural key is then HMAC-digested into the `object_key` column (`src/cadrumo/adapters/persistence/storage/sql/orm.py:175`), so neither the NIF nor its sha256 is stored in plaintext.
- `SecureBoundRepository` binds a payload type to a namespace. On load it refuses a row whose payload re-derives a different identifier (`src/cadrumo/adapters/persistence/storage/envelope/secure_bound_repository.py:225-262`). Writes accept `expected_revision_id` (`:303`, `:331`). The percepciones adapter shows the shape: envelope payload, `extract_identifier` and a filtered `iter_records` (`src/cadrumo/adapters/persistence/profile/percepciones_observations.py:46-153`).
- The write funnel enforces the registered namespace, classification, schema version and key grammar on every write, inside one atomic batch (`src/cadrumo/adapters/persistence/storage/sql/_secure_object_writes.py:200-260`; policy at `src/cadrumo/adapters/persistence/storage/sql/secure_objects.py:220-250`). Payload AEAD binds the namespace, the HMAC key digest and the schema version (`src/cadrumo/adapters/persistence/storage/crypto/encrypted_columns.py:48-70`). A new namespace therefore needs no AAD of its own. Its row identity is the AAD.
- The other templates are these. The operator-provenance record `LEDGER_CONFIRMATION_RECORD_NAMESPACE` is FINANCIAL because it carries accepted figures (`secure_object_namespaces.py:614-628`). `MODELO_RECONCILIATION_RECORDS_NAMESPACE` uses append-not-overwrite keys bound to an event id (`:509-530`). `LEDGER_CONFIRMED_COUNTERPARTY_FACTS_NAMESPACE` digests a counterparty identifier into the key (`:631-649`).

### Source kind, provider registration and resolver enrolment

- `BindingSourceKind` is the one closed source-kind authority for registry and mesh (`src/cadrumo/core/aggregation.py:228-359`). `WITHHOLDING` and `RETENCIONES_AGGREGATION` are both payer-side perceptor stores, PERCEPTOR_OBSERVATION terminal origin (`src/cadrumo/domain/calculations/registry/binding_provider_registration.py:534-561`). A suffered-side kind must be a distinct member. Reusing either would merge the two sides that `2026-09-21-retenciones-workflow-observation-payment-contract-adr` keeps apart.
- One `BindingProviderRegistration` row per kind carries the provider model, the validator, channels, ops, terminal origins, output, disposition, route and authoring (`binding_provider_registration.py:172-252`). The `_filing_grade` constructor fixes disposition and route (`:254-277`). The table is validated at import for duplicates, union coverage, channel shape and disposition-route agreement (`:750-772`). Per-binding checks read the registration (`:914-971`).
- Terminal origins are a closed enum (`src/cadrumo/domain/calculations/registry/binding_terminal_origin.py`, `TerminalOriginClass`). It has no payer-certificate or AEAT third-party member yet. Plan Step P02.S15 adds them.
- The application route enrols each resolver class once with its stage (`src/cadrumo/application/modelo/calculation_route.py:113-136`), and the route validates complete, unique coverage (`:155-200`). The resolver template is `RetencionesAggregationSourceResolver` (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/modelo_bindings_retenciones.py:67-260`). It carries `resolver_id` and `owned_sources` class vars. `load_calculation_observations` returns `None` when the revision declares no binding of the kind, which differs from an empty window (`:115-145`). A storage failure becomes `storage_degradation_resolution` (`:149-158`). An empty window raises a typed precondition refusal unless an attestation proves absence (`:161-227`). Each contributor gets one provenance node with a terminal origin (`:240-258`). Its `source_ref` embeds the plaintext perceptor NIF (`:250`). The new family must not copy that.
- The calculation report reads through the same loader. `CalculationWithholdingRows.absent_source_families` reports a family read and found empty, and never reports a zero (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/service.py:356-377`).
- The proven-absence analogue is `WithholdingDetailAbsence` (`src/cadrumo/application/modelo/withholding_detail_gate.py:148-186`). Absence is filing grade only when every window is attested and no recorded evidence contradicts the attestation.

### Durable findings and the gate

- A persisted `CalculationSourceIssue` has a closed `reason` Literal, `binding_source`, `casilla_id`, `source_ref` and an optional typed `fact` (`src/cadrumo/domain/modelos/calculation_revision_sources.py:231-310`; fact at `:167-197`). The application mirrors the Literal in `_DurableSourceIssueReason` (`src/cadrumo/application/modelo/calculation_bucket_actions.py:968-1002`). Gate membership is `GATE_REFUSED_REASONS`, `CHECK_REFUSED_REASONS` and `EXPORT_REFUSED_REASONS`, with attention mapping in `CALCULATION_NOTE_ATTENTION` (`src/cadrumo/application/modelo/calculation_notes.py:62-100`). `require_no_blocking_calculation_notes` refuses on the persisted revision alone (`src/cadrumo/application/modelo/calculation_note_gate.py:40-120`). Its callers are verification, export, local filing and the form notes (`verification_actions.py`, `export.py`, `filing_actions.py`, `work_form_notes.py` under `src/cadrumo/application/modelo/`). The gate takes no other store today, so a resolution record kept outside the revision needs a new gate input.
- Adding a reason is additive: stored revisions keep parsing. Removing or renaming a reason, or a `BindingSourceKind` member named in `binding_source`, breaks the replay of every persisted revision that carries it.

### Divergence and resolution precedents

- The censal reconciliation keeps the operator's value and reports each `(path, incoming)` divergence rather than overwriting it. Its identity guard runs first (`src/cadrumo/application/user_profile/censo_sync.py:76-95`, `:179-191`, `:274-376`).
- `FindingResolution` pins one resolution to one named blocker by a required `blocker_id` (Hex16), which an operator can match only against a blocker that was raised (`src/cadrumo/application/ledger/confirmation_gate.py:193-230`). An attestation requires a non-blank note and states no value. The actions are in `src/cadrumo/core/confirmation_gate.py:167-200`. The bounded wire form is restored to the canonical record at `src/cadrumo/application/ledger/invoice_evidence_confirm_operation.py:64-75`.
- The concurrently drafted `2026-11-01-calculation-aggregation-taxonomy-tier-divergence-adr` defers its finding envelope to this schema. It persists the finding as a `CalculationSourceIssue` and refuses unscreened legacy revisions. Its resolution accepts the ladder's value and never selects another.

### Borrador path today

- The borrador snapshot stores `binding_values` keyed by binding id (`src/cadrumo/application/live/borrador_100.py:72-73`). Its content id hashes the capture instant and the URL (`:290-312`). The shared lifecycle supersedes by capture time per axis and never compares values (`src/cadrumo/application/live/snapshot_base.py:318-364`). The tier admits only `aeat_prefilled` bindings (`src/cadrumo/application/modelo/borrador_binding.py:185-195`). The only decimal one is the 0596 `manual_input` binding `renta-certificado-trabajo-retenciones` (`src/cadrumo/_data/registry/aeat/modelos/100/revisions/2022/bindings/0001-declarations.toml:156-166`), bound by the 2022 override (`2022/revision.toml:2281-2283`). ### Where the patterns will not fit

- 0598 is computed from 0153. A family binding on 0598 would collide with the computed-casilla binding refusal (`calculation_bucket_actions.py:955-963`). Filling 0153 per inmueble would need a property identity that the per-payer record does not carry.
- Attribution and imputation classes (0592 to 0594, 0600, 0601) are sums of per-entity detail casillas. A per-payer class total can cross-check them, but cannot be their value source, without an entity-row design.
- The calculation-note gate reads only the revision. Honouring an out-of-revision resolution record means passing a resolution reader to `blocking_calculation_issues`, or folding the matched resolution into the revision at recalculation.
