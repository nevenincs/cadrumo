# `registry-completeness-closure` reference: `modelo 187 design era coverage`

## Summary

Modelo 187 revision `2019-y-siguientes` remains an applicability-grade
obligation only. Cadrumo must not emit it for any year. The sole shipped,
hash-pinned AEAT record-design source is the current catalogue's 2022-updated
design. It cannot substantiate 2019--2021, and the revision has no exporter,
semantic map, type-1/type-2 producer vocabulary, or emitted-byte proof for
the post-2022 source period. This is a refusal of filing, not permission to
derive a layout from four hoja-resumen casillas.

## Official design eras

Modelo 187's record design changed by exercise. BOE-A-2020-17271 adds a
type-2 payment-on-account field at position 241, first applicable to the 2020
declaration. BOE-A-2021-20004 adds key `K` to the type-2 acquisition or
disposal-origin field at position 142, first applicable to 2021. BOE-A-2022-14168
then changes the type-2 operation field at position 104 and adds the company
in liquidation identifier at positions 242--250, first applicable to 2022.
These are record-content changes, not portal presentation changes.

The packaged AEAT PDF is 345403 bytes, SHA-256
`c7a21c1feb9619380bb0da3e73066fa3c58c628f430bf85ed9dbea15b1308eb1`.
Its cover identifies exercise 2022; the type-1 table places the modelo at
positions 2--4, exercise at 5--8, and declarant NIF at 9--17. The source
catalogue selects it from 2022 without an end date, and AEAT's current 100--199
design catalogue still publishes that same 2022-updated artifact. It is
available post-2022 evidence, but no evidence for 2019--2021.

AEAT currently exposes Modelo 187 filing for 2025 and historical routes for
2020--2024. Those routes confirm a live filing surface; they do not allow a
2022 design to be used for the unevidenced historic years.

## Filing boundary

For the selected 2019+ period, Article 2 as redrafted by BOE-A-2018-17997
has two independent filer limbs. The first covers persons obliged to withhold
or pay on account that satisfy the stated IIC or subscription-right income. The
second covers the persons or entities in Article 42 RGAT for IIC operations.
That Article 42 population includes IIC management companies and fund
distributors, financial entities and intermediaries, public notaries, SICAVs
in the stated cases, and other regulated participants; it is not a synonym for
a withholding payer. The existing one-fact applicability selector can confirm
only the first limb and must leave the second incomplete until an accepted
profile-population decision gives it a canonical fact. An ordinary participant
is not thereby a filer.

BOE-A-2014-9225 requires the reported operations to follow Annex II.
BOE-A-2014-9784 corrects both type-1 declarant and type-2 operation records
from 250 to 500 positions. A summary sheet is therefore neither the full
record layout nor evidence that an ordinary participant can file the form.

The registered revision has only manual summary casillas `01` through `04`,
no formulas, and no export layout. The live capability worklist derives
`BLOCKED on design coverage`: `aeat-dr-187-2022` starts in 2022 while the
revision claims from 2019. It also requires a layout and a grounded filing-grade
decision; neither exists. No `m187.` `FilingProducerKey` namespace exists for
the non-casilla type-1/type-2 values. This adjudication does not broaden the
existing applicability selector.

## Adjudication, owner, and reconsideration

**Disposition: retain `2019-y-siguientes` at applicability grade with no
export layout and no filing capability.** The no-layout refusal is correct for
every selected year and must remain visible.

`W02.P04.S26` must enroll the temporal remedy in
`registry-temporal-coverage`: acquire and hash-pin exact official designs or
amendment tables for 2019, 2020, and 2021, then split or constrain selection
so every claimed year has a named source era. The post-2022 source must not be
backdated.

`W02.P04.S28` must enroll the export remedy in
`aeat-export-fragment-generator-authority`: assign all type-1/type-2 values
to live provenance-carrying producers; create the reviewed semantic map and
render profile; generate through the canonical publisher; and prove Cadrumo
bytes at official offsets. No parallel writer, remote submission, compatibility
layout, or authority promotion is authorized here.

Reconsider fileability only after every selected year has immutable official
design evidence; an accepted population decision covers both Article-2 limbs
(the withholding/pay-on-account payer population and the separate Article 42
RGAT person/entity population), including their exclusions and priority rules;
all value owners are approved; each selected design has a complete reviewed
field map; and generated fragments plus production rendering pass emitted-byte
proof. The population decision must extend the existing canonical
applicability authority rather than create a second selector. Only then may the
selected revision be considered for filing grade.
