# `docs-cli-sequences` audit: `docs-cli-sequences codification candidates and follow-up register`

## Scope

This is the S38 record for the `docs-cli-sequences` campaign: it captures the codification candidates for post-cycle rule promotion and the accumulated follow-up register. It is NOT the campaign close honesty review (that is a separate fresh-context pass, gated behind S37 and the landed content wave). Per the vaultspec-codify discipline, no rule is authored yet: a candidate is promoted only after it has held across at least one full execution cycle and after the close honesty review runs. The three primary candidates below are recorded verbatim from the ADR's Codification candidates section and reconciled against what actually landed across W01 through W05; secondary emergent-pattern candidates and the follow-up register follow.

## Findings

### primary-candidate-executed-truth | candidate | On an enrolled docs page every executable aeat invocation is executed, golden-gated truth

Proposed rule slug `docs-sequences-are-executed-truth`. Obligation: on a sequence-enrolled docs page, every executable `aeat` invocation lives inside a `cli-sequence` directive, executes hermetically at build time, and matches its committed golden; goldens are refreshed only via `python -m dev.docs.sequences refresh` and never hand-edited. Origin: ADR ruling D2 (committed light goldens, CLI-owned refresh) and D7 (enrolled-page tier). Landed and gate-backed across W02 (the engine and golden store, `dev/docs/sequences/`), W03.P07.S24 (the enrolled-page no-plain-executable-fence tier in `test_documented_command_conformance.py`), W03.P08 (the build-hook and pytest gate surfaces), and W06.P11.S36 (the two-tier coexistence pin). Promotion-ready when: the first enrolled page has shipped (W05 landed it at `docs/how-to/first-quarterly-filing.md`, not under a tutorials tree — see the w05-landed-placement note), the full docs gate suite is green (S37), and the pattern has held one full cycle with the `refresh` workflow exercised by a real golden-moving change — at which point the "never hand-edit" clause has been tested, not merely stated.

### primary-candidate-result-frame-asserted | candidate | Every sequence ends in exactly one asserted terminal @result frame

Proposed rule slug `sequence-result-is-mandatory-and-asserted`. Obligation: every CLI sequence ends in exactly one terminal `@result` frame carrying at least one `@expect` semantic assertion, narrated user-facing as a singular imperative verification step. Origin: ADR ruling D4 (sequence-result contract) and D1 (frame grammar). Landed and gate-backed in W02.P02.S06 (the parser refuses zero, multiple, or non-terminal `@result` frames, `dev/docs/sequences/_parser.py`) and W02.P04.S14 (`@expect` semantic evaluation, `dev/docs/sequences/_compare.py`), proven by the parser and comparison test suites (S08, S15) and exercised by the first enrolled how-to page's real sequences (W05.P10.S34/S35, landed as `docs/how-to/first-quarterly-filing.md`). Promotion-ready when: the enrolled content has demonstrated the contract holds against real authored sequences across a full cycle, and review discipline for the "inspection/verification verb, not a repeated mutation" author-guidance clause (D4, not machine-classified) has a worked precedent to cite.

### primary-candidate-central-mask | candidate | Sequence golden comparison uses exactly the central mask, no per-sequence extension

Proposed rule slug `sequence-mask-is-the-central-mask`. Obligation: sequence golden comparison uses exactly the central `GOLDEN_MASK_FIELDS` set; per-sequence mask extensions are forbidden, and a new nondeterministic field is enrolled centrally with the anti-tautology proof extended in the same change. Origin: ADR ruling D3 (comparison policy) built on the deterministic-output-replay substrate ADR. Landed and gate-backed in W02.P04.S13 (comparison delegates to the observability primitives with exactly the central mask, refusing per-sequence extension, `dev/docs/sequences/_compare.py`) and W02.P05.S18 (the executor-level anti-tautology proof: double execution, pre-mask differing paths equal the central mask set exactly, `dev/docs/tests/test_sequence_goldens.py`). Promotion-ready when: the anti-tautology proof has survived at least one central-mask extension (a genuinely new nondeterministic field added to `GOLDEN_MASK_FIELDS` with the proof extended in the same commit), proving the "enrol centrally, extend the proof" mechanism in practice rather than only in principle.

### secondary-candidate-emergent-patterns | candidate | Emergent cross-wave patterns worth watching for a second occurrence before promotion

Three durable patterns emerged across W01 through W05 that are NOT yet in the ADR's candidate list and have held for only this one campaign — recorded here so a second occurrence can trigger promotion rather than being missed. (1) One-engine-two-surfaces: the Sphinx `builder-inited` check hook and the `dev/docs/tests` pytest gate both call the same engine check functions and neither re-implements execution (ADR D6; W03.P08.S26/S27; the pull-equals-calculate discipline restated for docs). (2) Golden-store-is-CLI-owned: the `refresh`/`check` split with committed light data and gitignored heavy output (ADR D2; W02.P05.S16/S17) is the same discipline `aeat-docs-scaffolding-cli` and `modelo-locales-cli-authority` already codify for other generated surfaces — a candidate to fold into or sibling those rules rather than a standalone. (3) Static-plus-runtime shared live-refusal taxonomy: a live-AEAT `pull` sequence is refused both statically (unenrollable, stays a documented fence) and at runtime (the sandbox never sets `CADRUMO_LIVE_TESTS_ENABLED`), landed in W03.P07 (commit `b8f0612158`, statically refuse live-AEAT sequences). None is promotion-ready: each has one occurrence; the codify bar is a lesson that has held across at least one full cycle, so these wait for the close honesty review to confirm durability.

### w05-landed-placement | note | W05 landed as a how-to page, not a tutorials tree

W05 landed the first enrolled page at `docs/how-to/first-quarterly-filing.md` (two `cli-sequence` directives) with its committed goldens, not under `docs/tutorials/` — `docs/tutorials/` does not exist at HEAD, so the ADR D7 "tutorials-first" wording resolved to how-to placement. The S34 exec record carries the golden-dir rename mapping should a future `docs/tutorials/` move happen. Because `docs/how-to` IS in the conformance gate's `_TREE_DOC_DIRS` scan scope, the enrolled page is already covered: the shipped-enrolled-page scan and the co-located S27 golden gate are no longer vacuous. W06.P11.S36 added `test_shipped_enrolled_page_scan_is_not_vacuous` (commit `a972006666`) to lock this — an anti-vacuity tripwire asserting at least one shipped page stays enrolled, so the enrolled surface cannot silently collapse back to zero.

### followup-register | tracking | Accumulated deferred and latent follow-ups from W01 through W06

Nine follow-ups accumulated across the campaign, none blocking closure, each recorded here as inventory for a post-cycle pass. (1) DEFERRED — `_TREE_DOC_DIRS` scope expansion (operator decision): verified against `_flat_docs()` at HEAD, the conformance gate scans flat `docs/*.md`, `docs/explanation`, `docs/how-to`, and `README.md`, with `_TREE_DOC_DIRS = ("docs/explanation", "docs/how-to")`. The first enrolled page landed under `docs/how-to`, so enrolled-page coverage holds today (see the w05-landed-placement note). The deferred operator decision is to enroll the `docs/reference/`, `docs/verification/`, and `docs/architecture/` subtrees into `_TREE_DOC_DIRS` — those trees carry roughly six currently-unscanned `aeat` invocations (`docs/reference/commands-and-configuration.md`, `docs/verification/claude-code-install-proof.md`, `docs/verification/claude-desktop-install-proof.md`, `docs/verification/regularizar-atrasos-itinerary-proof.md`, `docs/architecture/index.md`; per the W01 review), so today they receive neither the base verb-path/option-name checks nor the enrolled-page tier. Separately, `docs/tutorials/` does NOT exist at HEAD; IF such a tree is ever created and enrolled pages move there, it MUST be added to the scan before pages ship. Expanding the scan surface is operator-gated because it widens the whole gate's blast radius, not only the enrolled tier. (2) OPEN — orphan-golden-dir sweep: `docs/_sequences/` accumulates per-page golden directories; a page deletion or sequence rename can strand an orphan golden dir with no live sequence, and no sweep verb yet detects them. (3) ACCEPTED-LATENT — multiline-inline-span extraction gap: `_INLINE_CODE_RE` matches single-line inline-backtick spans only (`` `[^`\n]+` ``), so an `aeat` invocation wrapped across a line break inside inline backticks is not extracted; accepted latent because the doc idiom is single-line inline references. (4) LATENT — negative-number token classification: the tokeniser's `_is_option_token` edge on a negative-number value (a bare `-5` read as an option-like token) was recorded as a review LOW in W03.P07.S22 (commit `005f470c94`); latent because no current sequence passes a negative numeric positional. (5) HYGIENE — approximately 26 exec records across the campaign shipped with template annotation blocks not yet stripped; they await a vault hygiene pass (`vaultspec-core vault check annotations --fix`, feature-scoped) once the campaign closes and no agent holds an exec record mid-edit. (6) ENGINE-CANDIDATE — `@expect` cannot assert casilla VALUES: the `@expect <json-path> == <literal>` grammar cannot address numeric casilla keys (e.g. `03`) and the `verify` envelope carries no `casilla_values`, so a sequence can assert the verification STATUS but not an individual casilla's computed value. A json-path grammar extension for numeric/dotted keys (plus surfacing casilla values on the asserted envelope) is the engine enhancement candidate if casilla-value assertions are wanted in future tutorials; deferred because the current result-frame contract (D4) is satisfied by status-level `@expect`. (7) ENGINE-CANDIDATE — seed-recipe grammar refuses comment lines: the frame-line parser treats a leading `#` as an unrecognised line (a parse fault), so a seed recipe file (e.g. `docs/_sequences/seeds/autonomo-irpf-2026.seq`) cannot document its in-file couplings — for instance the `SANDBOX_PROFILE_LABEL` literal the seed's `@setup` frames depend on cannot be annotated in a comment. A small `#`-comment grammar extension in the frame-line parser is a reasonable engine follow-up; deferred because seeds work today without in-file comments (the coupling is documented out-of-band). (8) LIMITATION (live, from honesty-review F2) — executed-truth does NOT cover narrative prose numbers: the sequence engine executes and golden-gates the command frames and the `@result` semantic assertion, but a figure written in the page's PROSE (a stated euro amount, a casilla total in a sentence or table) is not asserted against any golden, so a wrong prose number ships green. Concretely, the golden stores a casilla value as `"01": "1000"` (bare integer-string) while the surrounding prose may render it `1000.00` or `1.000,00`; that specific mismatch is cosmetic, but the class is real — the executed tier proves the commands and the terminal status, not the prose surrounding them. This is a stated live limitation of the feature as shipped, formally deferred (it is the same surface as item (6): assert-able casilla values would let prose figures be cross-checked). (9) CLEANUP (from honesty-review F4) — stale `DEP002` deptry-ignore for `formulas`: `pyproject.toml` lists `formulas` in the `DEP002` ignore array (around line 953) as a pre-existing entry unrelated to this campaign; minor cleanup to drop it if `formulas` is no longer a declared-but-unused dependency, tracked here so the pre-existing lint debt is not lost.

## Recommendations

- Hold all three primary candidates and the three secondary emergent patterns for the campaign close honesty review; promote each only after S37 is green, the content wave has landed a full cycle, and the specific promotion-ready condition named in its finding is met. Do not author any rule in `.vaultspec/rules/` before then (the codify discipline: one full cycle held).
- Route the `_TREE_DOC_DIRS` tutorial-scope expansion to the operator as an explicit decision, flagging that until it lands the enrolled-page live scan does not cover `docs/tutorials`.
- Fold the orphan-golden-dir sweep and the annotation-hygiene pass into the post-close maintenance batch; leave the two latent tokeniser/inline-span gaps as tracked inventory until a real doc triggers them.

### Findings

- **F1 — S37 open, hold close.** The full docs gate suite has not yet been confirmed green in one run; closure is held on it. Not a defect, a sequencing gate: the two build gates and the conformance gate each pass in isolation, but the single-run full-suite green is the close criterion and is owned by the baseline sweep.
- **F2 — executed-truth covers frames and result assertions, NOT narrative prose numbers (formally DEFERRED).** The engine executes and golden-gates the command frames and the terminal `@result`/`@expect`, so a wrong verb, option, output shape, or verification status reds the build. A figure stated in the page's PROSE is not asserted against any golden, so a wrong prose number ships green. Observed instance: the golden stores a casilla value as `"01": "1000"` (bare integer-string) while prose may render it `1000.00`; that mismatch is cosmetic, but the class — unverified prose figures — is real. Formally deferred with the limitation stated plainly; recorded as live register item (8). The durable fix shares the surface of register item (6) (assert-able casilla values).
- **F3 — `SANDBOX_PROFILE_LABEL` duplicated as a bare string in seeds, no binding test (tracked follow-up).** The sandbox profile label literal is repeated as a bare string across the seed recipe and the engine without a binding test pinning them equal. Converted to a tracked follow-up; the fix IS being landed now by the engine owner (out of this reviewer's scope).
- **F4 — stale `DEP002` deptry-ignore for `formulas` (minor, pre-existing).** `pyproject.toml` (around line 953) carries `formulas` in the `DEP002` ignore array, a pre-existing entry unrelated to this campaign. Minor cleanup; recorded as register item (9) so the pre-existing lint debt is not lost.
- **F5 — S18 empty-set-vs-mask-set adjudication (informational, ACCEPT stands).** The executor-level anti-tautology proof (`test_sequence_goldens.py`, S18) was reviewed for whether it pins the empty set or the central mask set; the ACCEPT from the original adjudication stands. Informational only — see the fresh-maintainer surprise below on where the real teeth are.
- **F6 — register verified honest, rename verified complete.** The follow-up register was cross-checked against the landed commits and gates and found honest (no overclaim, no missing landed item); the package/executable rename (`cadrumo` / `aeat`) was verified complete across the surfaces this campaign touched.

### Fresh-maintainer surprises

Three things a fresh maintainer would be surprised by, recorded unsoftened:

1. A constant rename can break the docs build SILENTLY from the maintainer's mental model — the coupling between a renamed CLI constant and the sequence goldens is real but not obvious, so a rename that does not refresh goldens surfaces only at the docs gate, not at the rename site.
2. Prose numbers are UNVERIFIED — a maintainer reasonably assumes "executed docs" means every number on the page is checked; it does not (F2). Only the command frames and the terminal assertion are golden-gated.
3. The anti-tautology proof pins the EMPTY set — the S18 proof's teeth are the mutation mask-bite test (a deliberately-injected nondeterministic field must be caught by the mask), not the empty-set assertion alone; a maintainer reading only the empty-set line would under-estimate what actually guards mask honesty.
