# `source-casilla-integration` audit: `S60 amortization terminal refusal review`

Formal review of the S60 asset-versus-finca amortization dispositions against the accepted amortization research and ADR, the source-connectivity taxonomy, the derived release predicate, and the canonical census/coverage authorities.

## Findings

### initial-classification | medium | Grounding blocked repeated an adjudication already completed

The first implementation classified the asset amortization ledger as `grounding_blocked` and retained a follow-up asking official evidence to decide substitutability. S56 research and the accepted S57-S59 decision already settle the 2025 destinations, exclusive validated-schedule ownership, transaction-ledger collision policy, and finca separation. The initial classification and action were therefore stale.

### corrected-ingress-boundary | pass | Governed source implementation is the remaining gap

The current row is `ingress_blocked`. It records the accepted exclusive 2025 activity-asset schedule for casillas 0208 and 0227, mandatory collision refusal, and the separate finca 0131 contract. Its action now requires the missing typed schedule, canonical source kind and resolver ownership, provenance, encrypted revision/replay, operator review, and supported export proof. This maps directly to S61-S69.

### bounded-accountability | pass | Evidence expiry and implementation ownership remain explicit

The source-connectivity owner is retained, the 2026-11-30 follow-up deadline precedes the 2026-12-31 expiry, and the permanent campaign-close gate will refuse stale evidence. Promotion remains owned by S70 and cannot occur from the scalar ledger alone.

### no-redeclaration | pass | Existing census and authorities are reused

The change adds no source taxonomy, resolver, selector, registry mapping, row carrier, or proof authority. Finca and general asset-register rows remain separate existing census entries.

## Recommendations

- Close S60 only after final re-review confirms the corrected ingress classification and follow-up.
- Keep S61-S71 open until the validated schedule and complete live proof exist; do not infer filing values from the current scalar amortization ledger.
