# schema-hardening r7 m100 batch-4 semantic audit

## Scope

Semantic-correctness review of 64 `semantic_role` groups from Modelo 100 (IRPF)
batch-4.json. Revisions 2020-2025. Structural type-consistency was assumed valid;
this pass checks whether the role name accurately describes the casilla group,
whether all members are coherent under that role, and whether granularity is
appropriate.

Valenciana) and property expense desglose (1395-1403), and (b) medical/health service provider NIFs in Anexo B disability/illness supplement (2085-2099 2025), dependency care provider NIFs (2105-2119), building repair service NIFs (2124-2138 2025), and attachment-sale service NIFs (2186-2200 2025).
