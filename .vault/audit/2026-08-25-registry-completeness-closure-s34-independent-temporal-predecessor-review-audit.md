# `registry-completeness-closure` audit: `S34 independent temporal-predecessor review`

## Scope

Independent current-head review of `W03.P06.S34`, including mixed commits
`0348a26d79` and `68041feaec`. Vaultspec-RAG located the accepted closure
decision, temporal predecessor plan, execution records, current and historical
audits, and the canonical temporal composer; whole-epicentre reads and exact
symbol searches then checked the closure denominator, plan state, and authority
home.

The review is confined to the temporal predecessor limb. It does not claim
cross-authority release eligibility, campaign completion, or ownership of the
source-era implementations carried by their original records.

## Findings

### temporal-predicate-denominator | low | The closure denominator is derived from canonical law-selected coverage

No defect found. `compose_temporal_coverage` expands every registered revision
through `revision_selection_coordinates` and the registry-owned supported-year
horizon. Its `revision_summaries` are the only denominator accepted by
`build_registry_closure_report`. Commit `0348a26d79` removes the stale
`revisions=102` expectation, derives the rendered count from
`load_registry_closure_report(...).rows`, and adds a two-coordinate renderer
bite. It changes test proof only; it does not create a temporal selector,
composer, or registry authority.

### predicate-relevance-and-boundary | low | Only the completed temporal predicate rows are included in the S34 claim

No defect found. The temporal plan and predecessor records show `S13`, `S14`,
`S24`, and `S43` through `S50` checked, with their implementation or corrective
provenance and review evidence. `S13` supplies the full matrix, `S14` retires
the superseded duplicate predicate, and `S24` supplies the supported-year
catalogue gate. The source-era rows retain evidence-bounded coordinates and
refuse unsupported ones. `S11`, `S12`, `S25`, and the wider W01/W03 work remain
unchecked and are expressly excluded; no phase summary or release-eligible
claim is made.

### historical-state-and-provenance | low | Earlier blockers remain auditable history rather than stale current state

No defect found. The earlier S34 audit accurately recorded the then-open
matrix and source-era rows. The final-close audit and execution record explain
the later state without rewriting that history. Commit `68041feaec` is a mixed
documentation commit: its S34 audit, record, plan, and index changes are
reviewed here, while its unrelated profile-custody and source-casilla material
remains separately attributed. The underlying mixed implementation provenance
continues to belong to the individual S13, S24, and S43--S50 records.

### temporal-authority-home | low | No temporal authority redeclaration was introduced

No defect found. Exact symbol searches found one `compose_temporal_coverage`
definition and the existing closure join consumes that composer rather than raw
fragments or a parallel selector. The S34 documents and test amendment add no
runtime authority, legal assertion, or registry data declaration.

## Recommendations

- Accept `W03.P06.S34` only as the independently reviewed temporal predecessor
  closure.
- Make no code or authority correction from this review. Keep `S11`, `S12`,
  `S25`, and the remaining W01/W03 rows open under their existing owners.
