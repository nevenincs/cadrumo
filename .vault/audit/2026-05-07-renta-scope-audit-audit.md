# `renta-scope-audit` audit: `renta scope audit and gaps inventory`

## Scope

This is a rolling audit of the persisted Modelo 100 Renta registry schema,
its legal references, and its mini-model coverage against the actual scope
of a Renta declaration. It is the foundation of a typed-out singular plan
that lists exec steps required for full Renta coverage.

The audit is generated by `scripts/audit_renta_scope.py` which runs against
the live registry on every commit. Re-run produces an up-to-date findings
JSON at `.vault/audit/{date}-renta-scope-audit-findings.json`. This
markdown document captures the prose interpretation, the typed-substrate
onboarding context, and the closed-list of exec steps surfaced by the
audit.

The audit covers four verification layers:

- Layer 1: per-revision casilla classification (input_kind distribution,
  computed vs bound vs manual, never-read casillas).
- Layer 3: mini-model coverage scorecard — group casillas into the AEAT
  mini-model partition (Anexo A, Anexo B per-CCAA, capital inmobiliario
  per-property, ganancias y pérdidas per-transaction, atribución de
  rentas, regímenes especiales) and compute coverage % per group.
- Layer 4: legal grounding inventory — which Ley / RD / Orden articles
  are catalogued, cited, orphan-substrate, or cited-but-uncatalogued.
- Layer 7: scenario archetype coverage — which Renta filing archetypes
  (employee, autónomo direct estimation normal, autónomo simplified,
  autónomo objective estimation, family unit, capital gains transactions,
  CCAA-specific deductions) have at least one synthetic test scenario
  exercising the chain end-to-end.

Layer 2 (per-formula coverage map), Layer 5 (cross-modelo mini-API audit),
and Layer 6 (external-surface mini-API audit) are deferred to follow-up
audit slices; they require deeper formula introspection and corpus byte-
range validation that the current driver does not yet perform.

**Critical gaps surfaced by Layer 3:**

- **Anexo B autonomic deductions (482 casillas, 0% coverage)** — 17
  CCAA × ~30 deductions each, none modelled. Every Renta filer with a
  CCAA-specific deduction (vivienda, donativos, family) hits the gap.
- **Anexo A general deductions (173 casillas, 0% coverage)** — vivienda
  habitual (transitorio régimen), donativos, inversiones culturales,
  inversión empresarial, alquiler vivienda transitorio, eficiencia
  energética. Standard salaried filers with deductions hit the gap.
- **Anexo C Canarias (179 casillas, 0% coverage)** — RIC, ZEC, ventas
  bienes corporales. Canarian-resident filers blocked.
- **Atribución de rentas + transparencia (312 casillas, 0% coverage)** —
  rentas atribuidas por entidades en régimen de atribución, art. 85
  imputación rentas, transparencia internacional. Affects shareholders
  in transparent entities, foundations, civil partnerships.
- **G/P + capital mobiliario base ahorro (219 casillas, 0% coverage)** —
  per-transaction breakdown of capital gains (sale of fund/property/
  rights) and capital mobiliario savings income. Most filers with any
  investment activity hit the gap. The cuota chain consumes
  pre-aggregated 0429/0424 saldos but does not derive them from
  per-transaction inputs.
- **Estimación objetiva (113 casillas, 0% coverage)** — modules-based
  business income (small autónomos, agricultural activities). No formula
  exists; the casilla schema exposes the manual modules but the registry
  cannot compute the rendimiento neto.
- **Regímenes especiales (85 casillas, 0% coverage)** — UTE, AIE,
  régimen especial buques navieras, régimen especial Canarias.
- **Capital inmobiliario per-property (154 casillas, 2.6% coverage)** —
  154 casillas covering per-property real-estate income, gastos
  deducibles, amortización, reducción por arrendamiento de vivienda. The
  per-property logic is implemented in `src/aeat/domain/rental/`
  (Python) but is NOT wired into registry formulas.
- **Retenciones / pagos a cuenta (27 casillas, 11.1% coverage)** —
  cross-modelo relations exist for 2025 only (Modelo 130/131/111/115/
  123/180/190/193) and were NOT backported to prior years. The
  2020-2024 ejercicios still have zero relations and zero bindings.

### Layer 8 — Test honesty inventory

Classifies every Renta test as one of three kinds:

- **Behaviour test** — asserts at least one non-zero numeric output
  derived from at least one non-zero numeric input. The math actually
  has to compute correctly for the test to pass. These tests catch
  formula swaps, dropped operands, and inverted negates.
- **Structural test** — asserts presence/absence of registry elements
  (formulas registered, articles catalogued, no orphans). Catches
  removal/rename regressions but does NOT test computation.
- **Vacuous smoke pattern** — all-zero inputs AND all-zero expected
  outputs. The chain computes `0 = 0 + 0 - 0 + ...` which never
  fails. False coverage signal — looks like a behaviour test, behaves
  like nothing.

| File | Tests | Kind | Zero asserts | Non-zero asserts | Non-zero inputs | Vacuous pattern |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|:---:|
| `test_renta_2025_synthetic_profile.py` | 2 | behaviour | 8 | 31 | 47 | no |
| `test_renta_chain_behaviour.py` | 8 | behaviour | 1 | 16 | 42 | no |
| `test_renta_cuota_chain_contract.py` | 8 | structural | 0 | 0 | 0 | no |
| `test_modelo_100_drift_detection.py` | 8 | structural | 0 | 0 | 0 | no |
| `test_schema_hygiene.py` | 8 | structural | 0 | 0 | 0 | no |

**Total numeric assertions**: 9 zero + 47 non-zero. **Files with
vacuous-smoke pattern**: 0 of 5.

**2026-05-07 cleanup pass:** the audit's first run flagged 7 vacuous
zero-input zero-output tests (one per supported ejercicio in a
parametrized scenario plus one standalone 2025 boundary case). They
asserted that all-zero input produces all-zero output for ~14
casillas × 6 years = 84 zero numeric assertions, none of which tested
anything observable. Removed in the same commit that adds Layer 8.
The test count went 45 → 38 with no loss of behavioural coverage:
chain calculation across years is already exercised by the
parametrized 30k employee scenario which produces non-zero outputs
in each year.

A new schema-hygiene guard at
`test_renta_synthetic_scenarios_do_not_pass_with_pure_zero_inputs_to_zero_outputs`
flags any future Renta scenario whose inputs and expected outputs are
all zero. New vacuous tests will trip this guard at PR time.

## Typed-substrate onboarding (VAT in-flight work)

The VAT domain has been driving a typed-substrate rollout that the Renta
exec plan must integrate from day one. Recent commits in this work:

- `c2fc4e24` Promote `Invoice.iva_category` from `str | None` to
  `VATCategory | None` — replaces a free-form string with the substrate-
  typed enum from `src/aeat/domain/vat/`.
- `c285c81b` Type-harden `Invoice.counterparty` country axis with
  `EUMemberState` bridge.
- `cc4259b8` Add `Invoice.iva_classification_for_line` typed accessor.
- `799d50a3` Track typed counterparty + iva_classification accessor in
  roll-out ledger.
- `688609e5` Legal-basis binding verification + LIVA arts 90/91 corpus
  (introduced the pydantic-typed binding surface that any registry-side
  legal binding now consumes).

The pattern surfaced by this in-flight work that must be inherited by the
Renta exec plan:

- **No free-form strings for substrate axes**. Every Renta domain field
  that asks "what kind of X is this?" must use a strict-frozen pydantic
  v2 enum with closed membership rather than a `str | None` field.
  Examples that need typing:
    - Renta `RentaIncomeType` — closed enum across `trabajo`,
      `capital_mobiliario_general`, `capital_mobiliario_ahorro`,
      `capital_inmobiliario`, `actividades_economicas_directa_normal`,
      `actividades_economicas_directa_simplificada`,
      `actividades_economicas_objetiva`, `ganancias_perdidas_general`,
      `ganancias_perdidas_ahorro`, `imputacion_rentas`, `atribucion_rentas`.
    - Renta `RentaCCAA` — closed enum mirroring `EUMemberState` shape but
      for the 17 Spanish autonomous communities + Ceuta + Melilla. The
      `ZCCAD` casilla currently binds to a free-form string profile key
      and must instead bind to the typed enum.
    - Renta `EstimacionDirectaModalidad` — closed enum {`normal`,
      `simplificada`} replacing the binding selector that maps casilla
      0168 string values "N"/"S".
- **Legal-basis binding verification** — every Renta formula's
  `legal_refs` must resolve through the same pydantic-typed legal binding
  surface that the IVA work introduced (`688609e5`), so the binding
  carries article id, BOE permalink, evidence_tier, and the corpus byte-
  range that anchors the `required_text`.
- **Per-line typed accessors** — the Renta-side equivalent of
  `Invoice.iva_classification_for_line` is a `RentaCasillaClassification`
  accessor on the per-row inputs (per-property real-estate row, per-
  transaction capital gains row, per-employer trabajo row) that returns
  the typed mini-model classification rather than ad-hoc string parsing.

The exec plan downstream of this audit must include explicit slices for
these typed-substrate ports.

## Recommendations / Exec slice inventory

Below is the closed-list of exec slices required for full Renta coverage,
ordered by impact-per-effort. Each slice maps to one or more pending
tasks in the autonomous loop. The single typed-out plan that flows from
this audit lists each as a separate phase with its own substrate
prerequisites and acceptance tests.

### Substrate slices (block downstream formula slices)

1. **Substrate-S1: Renta typed enums** — register `RentaIncomeType`,
   `RentaCCAA`, `EstimacionDirectaModalidad` in `src/aeat/domain/renta/`
   following the IVA `VATCategory` pattern. Bridge them to the existing
   profile bindings.
2. **Substrate-S2: legal article catalogue closure** — fetch and register
   the missing LIRPF articles for the uncovered mini-models: art-31
   (estimación objetiva), art-33-37 (ganancias y pérdidas), art-68
   sub-points (deducciones generales), art-81-bis (maternidad / large
   family), art-82-83 (tributación familiar), art-90-91 (atribución de
   rentas).
3. **Substrate-S3: cross-modelo relations + dependency_classifications +
   constructs backport to 2020-2024** — currently 2025-only (Task #42).
4. **Substrate-S4: escala progresiva parameters per ejercicio** — encode
   the IRPF estatal escala (LIRPF art. 63) and autonomic escala (LIRPF
   art. 74) brackets per year as registry parameters with year-scoped
   valid_from/valid_to (Task #44).

### Mini-model formula slices

5. **MM-1: Anexo B autonomic deductions (482 casillas, 17 CCAA)** —
   the largest single uncovered surface. Each CCAA owns ~30 deductions
   (vivienda, donativos, family, energy efficiency, residence). Register
   per-CCAA constructs with their own legal_refs (each CCAA's Decreto
   Legislativo) and formula sets. Estimated 17 sub-slices, one per CCAA.
6. **MM-2: Anexo A general deductions (173 casillas)** — vivienda
   habitual (transitorio), donativos, inversiones culturales, inversión
   empresarial, alquiler vivienda transitorio, eficiencia energética. 6
   construct groups. Each grounded in LIRPF art-68 sub-points.
7. **MM-3: Capital inmobiliario per-property (154 casillas)** — wire the
   Python rental tier resolver in `src/aeat/domain/rental/` into registry
   formulas, with per-property row inputs. Includes Task #50 (rental
   tier thresholds → registry parameters).
8. **MM-4: G/P per-transaction breakdown (219 casillas)** — per-
   transaction inputs for sale of fund / property / rights / criptomonedas;
   formulas to aggregate into 0418/0419/0422/0423; per-year
   carry-forward compensation chain.
9. **MM-5: Estimación objetiva modules (113 casillas)** — modules-based
   business income for small autónomos and agricultural activities.
   Year-scoped parameter table for module rates.
10. **MM-6: Atribución de rentas + transparencia (312 casillas)** —
    rentas atribuidas por entidades en régimen de atribución, art-85
    imputación, transparencia internacional. Wires Modelo 184 + 232
    cross-modelo relations into Renta.
11. **MM-7: Anexo C Canarias (179 casillas)** — RIC, ZEC, ventas bienes
    corporales. Canarian-specific régimen.
12. **MM-8: Regímenes especiales (85 casillas)** — UTE, AIE, régimen
    especial buques navieras.

### Scenario coverage slices

13. **Scenario-A2: Employee + capital mobiliario savings interest**
    (deferred until MM-1/MM-3 substrate; current scenario is a false
    positive).
14. **Scenario-B1/B2/B3: autónomo scenarios** (B1 needs MM-3, B3 needs
    MM-5).
15. **Scenario-C1/C2: family unit + descendants** (needs MM-2 +
    family-unit binding work).
16. **Scenario-D1: capital gains transactions** (needs MM-4).
17. **Scenario-E1: per-CCAA autonomic deduction** (needs MM-1).

### Hardening slices

18. **Renta typed-binding rollout** — promote every Renta `str | None`
    field that classifies a substrate axis to the corresponding typed
    enum; mirror the `c2fc4e24` IVA pattern.
19. **Per-formula corpus byte-range validation** — Layer 2 audit
    deliverable; verify each formula's `source_citations[].required_text`
    actually appears in the cited source corpus byte-range.
20. **Cross-modelo relation closed-loop validation** — Layer 5 audit
    deliverable; verify each Modelo 100 relation matches an inverse
    declaration on the source modelo side.
21. **External-surface registration** — Layer 6 audit deliverable; verify
    Renta WEB Open / authenticated / borrador / justificante surfaces are
    declared with guard policies and adapter coverage.
22. **Test honesty enforcement** — Layer 8 hardening already landed
    (`test_renta_synthetic_scenarios_do_not_pass_with_pure_zero_inputs_to_zero_outputs`).
    Extension: every behaviour test must derive its expected output
    INDEPENDENTLY of the formula being tested (no `assert
    actual == formula(inputs)` patterns where the test re-implements
    the formula). The audit's Layer 2 driver (deferred) will enforce
    this through formula-trace inspection: for every numeric
    assertion, confirm the expected value is a literal Decimal, not a
    runtime-derived expression.

## Continuous use

This document is regenerated by `scripts/audit_renta_scope.py` and
re-committed when the underlying registry shape changes materially. The
layered findings should monotonically improve over time: 2.6% casilla
coverage, 1/10 archetypes covered, 81 orphan-substrate articles. The
typed-out singular plan that flows from this document tracks each exec
slice with its own substrate prerequisites, acceptance tests, and the
audit metrics it is expected to move.

The audit's value is its honesty. The cuota chain rollout was real and
substantive but it is the spine of a creature whose flesh is the mini-
models above. Without the mini-model slices, the registry computes the
final cuota line of a declaration that the taxpayer or extractor still
has to fill in by hand for ~97% of casillas.
