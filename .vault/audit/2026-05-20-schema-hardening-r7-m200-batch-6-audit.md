# schema-hardening r7 m200 batch-6 semantic review

## Scope

Batch-6 of the M200 (Impuesto sobre Sociedades) semantic-role classification campaign. 94 roles reviewed against casilla labels, section paths, and data-type evidence. Source: `.vault-scratch/r7-m200/batch-6.json`.

## Findings

| `is_balance_activo_importe` | RENAME → `is_balance_activo_linea_importe` | Accurate concept but 82 members span balance_activo_i and balance_activo_ii sections (lines 00101–00701). The name is acceptable; adding `_linea` makes clear these are individual balance-sheet line items, not an aggregate. Acceptable as-is if consistency with `is_balance_patrimonio_neto_pasivo_importe` is preserved. OK if naming siblings are identical in style. |

Recommend split into `is_identificacion_regimen_flag` (entity type / regime markers 00001–00066, 00068–00072, 00078–00089) and `is_identificacion_opcion_flag` (elections: 00037, 00059, 00073, 00090). |
