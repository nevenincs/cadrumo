## Scope

Audit the finite annual matrix boundary and confirm that annual coordinates are enumerated from the law-determined authority without using the portfolio revision denominator or a newest-revision baseline.

## Findings

### S13 finite annual matrix review | low | The annual axis is finite and authority-selected

The matrix contains one exact provisional coordinate: Modelo 100, ejercicio 2025, period `0A`, with law-selected revision `2025`. Its revision identity is read from the validated authority, and the matrix carries an explicit classification census for every supported disposition.

### S13 finite annual matrix review | medium | Annual behavioral coverage remains provisional

The matrix is intentionally one coordinate rather than a claim that all 90 revisions or all 73 modelos have independent annual verification. The remaining coordinates are not silently counted as zero; they remain outside the finite matrix until exact official layout and oracle enrollment decisions are made.

## Recommendations

Keep the annual matrix denominator separate from the portfolio conformance denominator. Expand it only with an exact coordinate, law-selected revision, and grounded layout/oracle contract; preserve `not_yet_measured` and other classifications in the census.
