# schema-hardening r7 m100 batch-9 audit

## Scope

Round 7, Modelo 100 (IRPF), batch 9. 68 roles reviewed spanning revisions 2020–2025. Roles cover capital gains (ganancias patrimoniales) subtotals, real-estate data entry fields, Annexe A/B/C carry-forward and pending amounts, autonomous-community deductions, core tax calculation fields, economic activities (estimación directa/objetiva), attribution regimes, withholding fields, and scattered 2021-2025 new/single-revision roles.
