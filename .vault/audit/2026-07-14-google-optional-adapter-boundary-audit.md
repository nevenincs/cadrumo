## Recommendations

1. Reconcile and close the legacy plan from this row-level ledger; do not execute it as a backlog.
2. Preserve existing authentication, storage-provider, mirror, archive, evidence-acquisition, calculation-Sheets and ledger mutation paths; do not duplicate them.
3. Raise separate domain work only when its own product mandate exists. Rows marked `new-ADR-only` require an accepted ADR before planning or implementation.
4. Treat any future proposal for Google escrow/restore, watched inbound folders, Google-owned taxonomy, reverse merge, or persistent Sheets pull as an architectural change, not unfinished legacy work.
