### official-evidence | low | S01 reviewed and passed

Working checks read only the official observation layer using the caller pin and validate the exact selected registry coordinate. Pending local or operator assertions cannot mask official disagreement. Eleven encrypted adapter cases plus scoped style, type and import checks passed; independent review found no unresolved issue.

### historical-replay | high | Archived work initially hit a mutation-only guard

The first S02 repair required an active work unit even when rechecking an already recorded presentation. Review required a read-only scoped historical lookup before any active-state mutation guard. This was corrected for automatic and explicit targets, with sparse archived replay coverage.

### filing-chain | low | S02 correction verified and passed

Twenty-three encrypted chain cases passed, including receipt-only confirmation followed by contradictory casillas, conflicting register anchors, no-content replay, wrong profile/model/period, missing calculation and archived explicit-target replay. Replays preserve chain and event history, disclose evidence basis and report conflicts as unverifiable. Scope accepts comparison only of casillas actually supplied by AEAT. Independent review closed the historical replay finding. Scoped Ruff, ty, basedpyright, pyrefly and private-import checks passed.

### cross-model-rendering | medium | Shared finding formatter required enrollment

Core S03 review passed with 32 combined working/cross-model cases and scoped checks. Additional operator-path verification found missing fact-kind enrollment for the new incomplete-comparison warning. Formatter correction and CLI/TUI rendering verification are in progress; S03 remains open until those checks pass.

### cross-model-rendering-closed | low | S03 operator contract verified and passed

The shared finding formatter now enrolls counterpart model and both missing-value counts with the correct closed fact kinds. Four-language CLI report JSON roundtrips and TUI wire-finding roundtrips assert displayed amounts and missing counts. Thirteen renderer/producer cases pass in addition to the 32 working/cross-model cases. Scoped style, types and canonical private-import checks pass. Independent review found no remaining S03 finding; the earlier formatter gap is closed.
