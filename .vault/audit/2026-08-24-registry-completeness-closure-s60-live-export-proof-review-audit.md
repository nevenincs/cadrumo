# `registry-completeness-closure` audit: `S60 live export proof review`

## Scope

Fresh-context review of the S60 implementation against the accepted closure ADR,
the S57 high finding, registry authority flow, export-tree ownership, architecture
boundaries, and real-behaviour mutation-gate requirements. The review traced the
application port and refusal taxonomy, the concrete development adapter, canonical
generator reconstruction, production `export_draft` execution, payload and official
offset verification, Modelo 111 refusal, and Modelo 200's current production failure.

## Findings

No critical, high, medium, or low finding remains in the reviewed S60 surface.

The former passive catalogue is absent from the shipped facade. The concrete authority
derives rather than accepts generator locators, revalidates the exact law-selected
layout through current authored semantics and generated bytes, and only constructs the
proof model after production output agrees with the receipt, expected digest, extent,
and positioned literal probes. The composer now separates exact identity conflict from
staleness. Mutation coverage reaches every digest limb and both live capability gaps:
Modelo 111 cannot reach a manifest, while Modelo 200 reaches a valid generator result
and then refuses in the real writer.

## Recommendations

Keep the live acceptance catalogue empty until a revision completes both stages. When
the Modelo 200 year-field defect or another production export path is independently
fixed, add the first successful entry and an end-to-end green proof using externally
reviewed payload digest and offset probes; do not pre-author those expected bytes while
the writer is refusing.
