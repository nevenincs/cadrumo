# `registry-completeness-closure` audit: `S34 temporal predecessor predicate closure reconciliation`

## Scope

This is a current-head, predicate-scoped reconciliation for `W03.P06.S34`.
Vaultspec-RAG first located the accepted closure decision, the temporal plan,
the predecessor records and their reviews, and the one temporal composer;
whole-document reads and exact-symbol searches then tested those results.

The scope is the temporal limb of the registry-completeness predicate only:
every law-selected revision must have validated temporal coverage through the
canonical authority. It does not assert that the cross-authority release
predicate passes, close the wider temporal campaign, or absorb source and
export work owned elsewhere.

## Findings

### predicate-temporal-rows | resolved | All release-predicate temporal rows are complete with traceable records

`W02.P06.S13` supplies the full validated modelo, filing-year, and period
matrix; `S14` removes the superseded coverage-ledger filing predicate in favour
of the snapshot-owned authority; and `S24` supplies the supported-year
catalogue and its validation gate. Each row is checked and has its execution
record. The eight bounded source-era rows `S43` through `S50` are likewise
checked, recorded, and reviewed: M038, M182, M187, M188, M194, M220, M721, and
M763 now retain only their evidenced temporal scopes and refuse unsupported
coordinates.

The prior high and medium findings are reconciled rather than erased. The
former representative-coordinate finding is closed by S13. The M038 June-2024
boundary, the M182 consumer and census corrections, the M187 Article-42
correction, and the M220 applicability correction have their corrective record
and follow-up review. The remaining temporal post-reviews report no unresolved
high or medium finding. The S13 review's low M353 emitted-byte expectation is
an export-owner concern and is not reclassified as temporal completion work.

### stale-checkpoint | resolved | The earlier S34 blocker audit remains historical evidence, not current state

The earlier S34 execution record and audit accurately captured the then-open
S13, S14, and S43--S50 rows. Current plan state and the later records now
contradict that historical snapshot, so this audit supersedes its conclusion
without rewriting its rolling findings. No stale unchecked predicate row,
missing predicate execution record, or unrecorded supersession remains.

### provenance | resolved | Mixed shared-worktree commits retain their original attribution

The reconciliation preserves rather than reattributes mixed implementation
history: S13 names `915a66a5bc` as its mixed-main implementation and its scope
correction record; S24 names `d7a4413227`; and the M763 S50 record names
`5ccbc15a693`, with separate tracking `d646c2d907` and independent review
`7140113c661`. The M763 record explicitly excludes the unrelated M182 census
change carried by its shared implementation commit. S44 corrective commits
`0745edd51c` and `f291e2af1f` remain their own M182 audit lineage. No new
commit is presented as the source-era implementation.

### single-temporal-authority | resolved | The canonical composer and denominator are not redeclared

`compose_temporal_coverage` remains the one temporal composer and the closure
report takes its temporal revision summaries as the canonical denominator.
Exact search found no second composer, selector, resolver, raw-fragment path,
or authority declaration. The S34 test correction derives the CLI's rendered
revision count from `load_registry_closure_report` and the bundled validated
authority; its two-coordinate renderer bite rejects a return to a literal
count. This changes no runtime behaviour.

### nonpredicate-temporal-backlog | resolved | Wider campaign work remains visibly open and is not claimed complete

`W02.P05.S11`, `S12`, and `S25`, together with the open W01 and W03 temporal
campaign rows, remain unchecked. They are advisory or production-consumption
hardening beyond the completed temporal coverage predicate and are not silently
included in this close claim. No phase summary is created because the temporal
phase itself is not complete.

## Recommendations

- Close only `W03.P06.S34` as the reconciled temporal predecessor limb.
- Keep `S11`, `S12`, `S25`, and the other temporal campaign backlog visibly
  open until their own scopes and reviews complete.
- Require the separately assigned independent S34 review before later
  release-honesty steps rely on this predecessor-close record.
