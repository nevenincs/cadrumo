# `iva-workflow` adr: `The rectificativa is the source of the signed Modelo 303 modification, and no method, cause, period or election is assumed` | (**status:** `accepted`)

Accepted 2026-11-22 by ALIGN under the operator's standing order to decide and record.

## Problem Statement

A factura rectificativa in the Libro de facturas blocks the Modelo 303 and declares nothing. `src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/invoice_iva_source.py:89` defines `invoice_rectificativa_modification_unmodelled` and raises it at `:434` for every rectificativa the period holds, issued or received, because the invoice screen declares no observation for one (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/modelo_bindings_invoice_iva.py:925-939`). The record cannot hold the modification: `Invoice` carries `invoice_class` (`src/cadrumo/domain/invoices/models.py:384`) and `rectifies_invoice_number` (`:477`), validates `base_total`, `iva_total` and `grand_total` non-negative (`:703-707`), and holds no rectification method, no cause and no reception date. Its lines are magnitudes with a rate (`:276-298`), so a rate row would add tax where the document reduces it.

The ledger side is already decided and implemented. A movement declared as a rectification deduction kind settles on the input side fact 0085 declares, `rectification_flow.<category>` (`src/cadrumo/domain/iva/deduction_facts.py:297-326`, `src/cadrumo/_data/registry/aeat/facts/0085-iva-deduction-applicability-catalogue.toml:53-59`), and an incoming refund projects negative base and cuota (`src/cadrumo/application/aggregation/iva_transaction.py:608` and `:617-638`) into the casilla 40/41 bindings, which are `ledger_iva_aggregation` providers keyed on the rectification deduction kind (`src/cadrumo/_data/registry/aeat/modelos/303/revisions/2022/bindings/0001-declarations.toml:598-601`). The issued side has no source at all: the 303 formula notes record that the modificación de bases y cuotas boxes, 15 for IVA and 26 for recargo, are operator input the total adds as printed (`src/cadrumo/_data/registry/aeat/modelos/303/revisions/2022/formulas/0001-declarations.toml:11-13`).

Verification refuses the whole family on a second ground. Fact 0085 requires the `rectification_evidence` authority for the rectification kind (`0085-iva-deduction-applicability-catalogue.toml:45`), no production writer records that authority, so `UNRECORDABLE_DEDUCTION_SCENARIOS` maps it to a terminal refusal (`src/cadrumo/application/modelo/verification_finding_contracts.py:86`) emitted as `iva_rectification_document_unrecordable` (`src/cadrumo/application/modelo/verification_iva_evidence.py:228-235`, producer at `:62-92`).

So a taxpayer who receives or issues a factura rectificativa cannot file: the issued modification has no source, the received one has a source but no admissible document, and the facts that decide the sign and the period are not in the record. The decision is which facts the document contributes and how each side's period is fixed, because every one of those answers changes a filed cuota.

## Considerations

- RD 1619/2012 art. 15.5 permits two methods and only two: the rectificativa states either "directamente el importe de la rectificación, con independencia de su signo" or the figures "tal y como queden tras la rectificación efectuada, señalando igualmente en este caso el importe de dicha rectificación" (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/normatives/html/rd-1619-2012.html.extracted.md`, art. 15.5). Substitution therefore also states the modification amount, which makes that amount a cross-check rather than a second source.
- LIVA 89.Cinco gives the issuer one genuine election and two non-elective routes. An increase founded on an art. 80 cause or on error fundado de derecho: "el sujeto pasivo podrá incluir la diferencia correspondiente en la declaración-liquidación del periodo en que se deba efectuar la rectificación"; an increase on any other ground requires a declaración-liquidación rectificativa with recargo e intereses; a minoración is an election between the art. 120.3 LGT procedure and regularising "en la declaración-liquidación correspondiente al periodo en que deba efectuarse la rectificación o en las posteriores hasta el plazo de un año", the second route obliging reintegro to the recipient (`ley-37-1992.html.extracted.md`, art. 89.Cuatro and Cinco).
- LIVA 114.Uno makes the recipient's minoración obligatory: "La rectificación de las deducciones será obligatoria cuando implique una minoración del importe inicialmente deducido." 114.Dos.1 gives the recipient a period election for an increase, the period of receipt of the document "o bien en las declaraciones-liquidaciones siguientes" within four years. 114.Dos.2 makes a minoración a declaración-liquidación rectificativa in general, except that for art. 80.Tres and for a concurso reintegración where the recipient is also in concurso it goes to "el periodo en que se ejerció el derecho a la deducción de las cuotas soportadas, sin que proceda la aplicación de recargos ni de intereses", and except that a cause in error fundado de derecho or the remaining art. 80 causes goes to the period "en que el sujeto pasivo reciba el documento justificativo del derecho a deducir".
- RIVA art. 24 conditions the recipient's increase and the issuer's reduction on issue and remittance of the rectificativa, adds a copy to the administración concursal in art. 80.Tres cases, and in art. 24.2.b fixes the recipient's declaration: the total rectified cuotas as a minoración of deducted cuotas in the period the rectificativas were received, and for art. 80.Tres in the periods in which the deduction was exercised (`rd-1624-1992.html.extracted.md`, art. 24.1 and 24.2.b.1 to 3).
- The Modelo 303 instructions state both boxes. Casillas 14 and 15: "se hará constar con el signo que corresponda la modificación de bases imponibles y cuotas de operaciones en las que concurra cualquiera de las causas previstas en el artículo 80 de la LIVA (Artículo 24, apartado 1 y 2 del RIVA), así como cualquier otra modificación de bases y cuotas", including the minoración for régimen de viajeros refunds. Casillas 40 and 41: "el importe de la base imponible y de las cuotas deducibles rectificadas, regularizadas en las autoliquidaciones del ejercicio. No se incluirán aquellas rectificaciones que hayan sido regularizadas en autoliquidaciones de periodos anteriores... Si el resultado de la rectificación implica una minoración de las deducciones, se consignará con signo negativo" (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/aeat_official/instructions/modelo_303/files/modelo-303-instrucciones-20261106.html`).
- Stored amounts are magnitudes and economic direction is a typed field, by `2026-06-10-ledger-amount-direction-adr`. `iva_transaction.py:617-638` already applies the rectification sign at projection and nowhere else, and records that an observation may carry signed negative IVA evidence for a rectification and for nothing else.
- `2026-11-08-iva-workflow-invoice-iva-source-adr` makes the invoice the IVA source and projects it through the one observation stream; a second rectification engine would contradict it and the aggregation rule.
- `2026-08-13-facturae-invoice-class-adr` already grounds the rectificativa class on the document's own declaration, so the class axis needs nothing here.
- `2026-08-07-stray-concept-sweep-iva-purchase-refund-representation-adr` left this representation question open and is still `proposed`; its movement half was answered by the rectification deduction kind now in `deduction_facts.py:297` and `iva_transaction.py:617`.

## Considered options

1. Leave the family blocked and let the operator type casillas 14/15 and 40/41. Rejected: it discards a document the taxpayer already recorded, puts tax arithmetic on a surface against `2026-11-03-backend-served-surface-adr`, and leaves the obligatory received-side minoración dependent on the person remembering it.
2. Derive the sign from the cash direction of a linked payment, as the movement path does. Rejected: a rectificativa commonly has no payment at all, art. 89 and art. 114 attach the modification to the document rather than to money, and the direction of a refund does not say which cause or which period applies.
3. Admit a default method and place every modification in the period of the document. Rejected: the method changes how the stated amounts are read, and 89.Cinco, 114.Dos.2 and art. 80.Tres place three different periods on three different causes. A default here is a silently wrong filed figure.
4. Record the document's own facts, derive the signed modification from the method, and fix the period from side plus cause plus an explicitly recorded election. Chosen.

## Constraints

- The rectificativa contributes through the one observation stream of `2026-11-08-iva-workflow-invoice-iva-source-adr`, as a signed modification observation carrying `invoice:<invoice_id>` provenance. No second rectification engine, no copy into a transaction, no frontend arithmetic.
- `Invoice` gains four facts, each operator supplied and none inferred: `rectification_method` (`diferencia` or `sustitucion`, RD 1619/2012 art. 15.5), `rectification_cause` (a closed registry vocabulary of the art. 80 apartados plus `error_fundado_de_derecho` and `otra_modificacion`, the instructions' "cualquier otra modificación"), `rectification_direction` (`minoracion` or `aumento`), and, on a received rectificativa, `document_received_on`. Totals and line amounts stay non-negative magnitudes; the direction fact carries the economic sense and the sign is applied only at projection, as `iva_transaction.py:617-638` does today.
- The signed modification is derived per rate and per category. Under `diferencia` the document's own lines are the modification and the sign is the recorded `rectification_direction`. Under `sustitucion` the lines are the post-rectification figures and the modification is new minus original, read per rate and per category from the catalogue invoice that `rectifies_invoice_number` resolves to; that original must be present and must resolve to exactly one catalogue invoice of the same kind and counterparty, or the rectificativa is blocked naming the missing or ambiguous original. Where the document also states the importe de la rectificación that art. 15.5 requires, it is compared against the derived difference and a divergence over one cent is a blocking finding, as the recargo checks already are. It is never a second source of the figure.
- An issued rectificativa's modification is declared with its sign in casillas 14/15, and in 25/26 where the operation carried recargo de equivalencia. Casillas 14/15 gain an invoice-sourced contribution through the observation stream. The operator-entered figure those boxes hold today survives as a distinct, separately labelled contribution for modifications the catalogue holds no document for, the régimen de viajeros refunds the instructions name among them. The two must never describe the same document: a typed value in a period that also holds in-period rectificativas, with no attested scope of its own, is a blocking finding rather than a silent addition.
- A received rectificativa's modification is declared in casillas 40/41 as a rectificación de deducciones, on the input side fact 0085's `rectification_flow.<category>` already declares, negative for a minoración as the instructions require. Its deduction family comes from the original invoice's recorded family, never from the rectificativa alone.
- The period is fixed per side and cause, never by proximity and never by the period being calculated. Issued side: an `aumento` whose cause is an art. 80 apartado or `error_fundado_de_derecho` is the 89.Cinco election, recorded as `issuer_increase_election` (`incluir_en_el_periodo` or `autoliquidacion_rectificativa`); an `aumento` on any other cause has no election and belongs to a declaración-liquidación rectificativa of the original period; a `minoracion` is the 89.Cinco election, recorded as `issuer_minoracion_election` (`procedimiento_120_3_lgt` or `regularizacion_en_autoliquidacion`), and that second route also records the period chosen inside the one-year limit. Received side: an `aumento` is the 114.Dos.1 election of the reception period or a later one within four years, recorded as `recipient_increase_period`; a `minoracion` is obligatory and its period is the cause's, with no election at all, namely the reception period for the art. 80 causes and `error_fundado_de_derecho`, the periods in which the deduction was exercised for art. 80.Tres and for a concurso reintegración where the recipient is also in concurso, and otherwise a declaración-liquidación rectificativa of the original period.
- An election the record does not hold is never assumed. The rectificativa is blocked with a finding naming the election, the side and the cause, and offering a step that opens the invoice. The same holds for a missing method, a missing cause, a missing direction, a missing `document_received_on` on a received rectificativa, and a missing exercised-deduction period where art. 80.Tres routes there and the catalogue cannot supply it from the original invoice's declared period.
- A modification whose decided period is not the period being calculated contributes nothing to it. That is a correct outcome, not a defect: it is disclosed as an excluded contribution naming its period and its legal route, with a step, and it does not block. This is also how the instructions' exclusion of rectifications already regularised in earlier autoliquidaciones is honoured, because one modification reaches exactly one period.
- Art. 80.Tres is admitted on both sides only against operator attestation, because the application cannot observe the RIVA art. 24 obligations. The issued side needs an attested communication and remittance under art. 24.1 and 24.2.a and a modification inside the two-month window art. 80.Tres fixes; without it the modification is blocked, never admitted on the document alone. The received side routes to the periods in which the deduction was exercised, per 114.Dos.2 and RIVA art. 24.2.b.3. The art. 24.2.b.3.b exception that routes part of the cuota to the pre-concurso declaration of RIVA art. 71.5 stays out of scope and is blocked with a finding naming it; no part of it is guessed.
- Stored rectificativas are not backfilled. They hold none of the new facts, so they stay blocked under the existing `invoice_rectificativa_modification_unmodelled`, whose finding gains the names of the facts the invoice is missing so the person can supply them. No method, cause, direction, reception date or election is inferred from the class, the issue date, the booking date, a linked movement or an amount.
- A rectificativa that satisfies this record is the production writer of the `rectification_evidence` authority fact 0085 requires, which retires `rectification_document_unrecordable` for the rows it grounds. It grounds that authority only when the record holds the document itself, by the test `2026-11-08-iva-workflow-invoice-iva-source-adr` already states for the art. 97 document, an attachment linked to the invoice or a paying movement carrying registered purchase-invoice evidence, and, on the received side, the `document_received_on` the period rule consumed. A rectification row with no such rectificativa in the catalogue keeps today's terminal refusal. The gap is graded as the existing deductible-evidence gap is: advisory at calculation, blocking at verification, export and local filing.
- Surfaces render served vocabulary only. The methods, causes, directions, elections, their words, and the set of facts a given side and cause requires, are served by the owning operations under `2026-11-03-backend-served-surface-adr`. No CLI, TUI or window holds a cause list, a period rule, a sign rule or a sentence with tax meaning.
- Filing years come only from the registry envelope of `2026-08-14-registry-temporal-coverage-adr`, and a projected year reaches calculation grade at most. No year literal is introduced. The one-year and four-year windows of 89.Cinco and 114.Dos are dated legal durations declared in the registry, not constants in Python.
- The new registry declarations are delta keyed under `2026-09-09-registry-edition-authoring-adr`: the cause, method and election vocabularies, the repercutido-side modification flow and the windows are declared once at the earliest edition where the official text holds, and a later edition states only a real official difference. Casilla keying follows `2026-06-13-m303-form-vs-semantic-casilla-dual-keying-adr`: the modification reaches a box through that box's semantic source, not through a new form-keyed path.

## Implementation

We will make the factura rectificativa the source of the signed Modelo 303 modification: record its method, cause, direction and reception date as explicit facts, derive the signed per-rate modification from the method, place it in exactly one period from side plus cause plus a recorded election, and block rather than assume any of those facts. Scope is Modelo 303 casillas 14/15 and 40/41, with 25/26 for recargo on an issued modification. Modelo 390 annual totals consume the same observations and need no separate rule.

Touch points:

- Invoice catalogue add and update contracts (`src/cadrumo/application/invoices/catalogue_add_contracts.py`, `catalogue_intake_contracts.py`, `catalogue_lifecycle.py`) carry the new facts and refuse an incoherent combination at the write boundary, as `models.py:940-990` already does for the class axis.
- The invoice creation wizard asks for method, cause, direction, reception date and the applicable election, driven by the served per-side fact set rather than by a wizard-local rule.
- CLI intake specs (`src/cadrumo/entrypoints/cli/app_ledger_invoice_intake_command_specs.py`, `app_ledger_invoice_common_command_parameters.py`) and the lifecycle specs gain the parameters, with stable untranslated tokens.
- Bulk import columns (`src/cadrumo/application/invoices/bulk_import_columns.py`) gain the same facts; a rectificativa row missing one imports and is blocked, never defaulted.
- Generated contracts (`native/desktop/frontend/src/shell/ledgerInvoiceContract.ts`) and the operation contract manifest regenerate in the same change as the schema, so the desktop does not see `operation_unavailable`.
- Locale keys for the served vocabulary and the new findings are authored through `dev.locales` in all four locales, as delta-keyed modelo text.
- The M303 invoice screen (`modelo_bindings_invoice_iva.py:925-939`, findings at `invoice_iva_source.py:89` and `:434`) replaces the blanket block with the derivation, the period decision, the disclosed out-of-period exclusion and the new specific findings. The verification gate (`verification_finding_contracts.py:86`, `verification_iva_evidence.py:62-92` and `:228-235`) stops treating `rectification_evidence` as unrecordable for grounded rows.
- The registry gains the vocabularies, the repercutido-side modification flow beside fact 0085's `rectification_flow`, the dated windows and the casilla 14/15 invoice-sourced binding; `just check-registry`, `just check-registry-gate` and `just check-bindings` prove them, and the authority is republished from stable final inputs.

Hypotheses that may change inside these constraints: whether the repercutido-side modification flow lives as new keys on fact 0085 or as its own fact; whether the elections are stored on the invoice or on the Modelo work unit; and whether the casilla 14/15 operator contribution keeps its present edit shape or becomes an attested named contribution under `2026-08-24-modelo-edit-contract-adr`.

Implementation choices recorded on 2026-11-22 (slice 1), inside the constraints above:
- The method, cause and direction vocabularies extend the invoice legal classification catalogue (fact 0141) at its single 2013-01-01 variant, as `invoice_class` does, because RD 1619/2012 art. 15.5 and the art. 80 grounds hold from that date.
- The closed cause vocabulary is the art. 80 apartados that are modification grounds (`art_80_uno_1`, `art_80_uno_2`, `art_80_dos`, `art_80_tres`, `art_80_cuatro`, `art_80_seis`) plus `error_fundado_de_derecho` and `otra_modificacion`. Cinco is excluded because it governs Tres and Cuatro rather than stating a cause; Siete is excluded because it states regulatory conditions.
- The tokens are lowercase snake case, as written in this record.
- The new coherence rules apply at write admission only, so a stored record still opens for correction.

Implementation choices recorded on 2026-11-23 (slices 3 and 4), inside the constraints above:
- The elections (89.Cinco increase, 89.Cinco minoración and its regularisation period, 114.Dos.1 recipient period, the art. 80.Tres attestation and the exercised-deduction period) are stored on the invoice, not on the Modelo work unit, because each belongs to one document and must hold for every return that reads it. The dated windows they are checked against are registry facts.
- The repercutido-side modification is its own ledger observation role, not new keys on fact 0085. Fact 0085 is the deduction-applicability catalogue: its resolver answers in deduction kinds and evidence authorities, and the observation validator refuses deduction authority on a row with no deductible cuota. An issued modification is devengado and carries no deduction kind. The casilla 14/15 and 25/26 bindings select that role, ordinary devengado bindings keep the settlement role, and the issued flow still derives from the category through the existing classification path.
- A received rectificativa that holds its document by the art. 97 test (an attachment linked to the invoice, or a paying movement with registered purchase-invoice evidence) and its reception date is the writer of the `rectification_evidence` authority. A received rectificativa without its document is refused as the recoverable `invoice_source_document_missing`, because the document can be attached, not as the terminal unrecordable finding.
- The casilla 14/15 and 25/26 operator contribution becomes an attested named contribution under `2026-08-24-modelo-edit-contract-adr`. The present edit shape cannot be kept: a casilla's input kind is exclusive, and a computed box takes no typed value. Each box is therefore computed as the bound invoice-sourced carrier plus its own manual contribution casilla. A typed contribution in a period that also holds in-period rectificativas, with no attested scope of its own, is a blocking finding.
- A saved BORRADOR revision whose operator value is keyed on box 14, 15, 25 or 26 is not migrated, because a migration would assert a scope the operator never attested. On recalculation or verification it raises a blocking finding naming the box, the stored value and the contribution casilla to re-enter it in. Local PRESENTADO and filed revisions are history and are not re-judged.

## Rationale

The knockout is that the document states what the return needs and the record cannot hold it. RD 1619/2012 art. 15.5 fixes the two readings of a rectificativa's figures, so the method is the only fact that says what the stated amounts mean. LIVA 89.Cinco and 114.Dos put three different periods on three different causes, so the side and the cause are the only facts that place the modification. And 89.Cinco and 114.Dos.1 hand the taxpayer a real choice, which an application may record but may never make. Deriving any of them, from the class, from a payment's direction or from the period being calculated, produces a filed figure the law does not support and the operator never stated.

Keeping the magnitude and the direction apart, and applying the sign only at the projection, is what lets the one observation stream carry a negative contribution without a second signed store. It also answers the question `2026-08-07-stray-concept-sweep-iva-purchase-refund-representation-adr` left open, in the place that record identified as the real decision, rather than by extracting a direction-only helper.

## Consequences

A taxpayer who records a rectificativa can file the period it belongs to, on both sides, with the sign the instructions require and the evidence gate satisfied by the document itself. The obligatory recipient minoración of 114.Uno becomes expressible rather than silently omitted, and the issued-side modification stops depending on a typed box.

Accepted costs: every rectificativa needs facts the person must supply, and stored ones stay blocked until they do; a modification whose period is not the one being calculated is visibly excluded rather than absorbed; art. 80.Tres on the issued side needs an attestation the application cannot check; and the RIVA art. 24.2.b.3.b pre-concurso split, the declaración-liquidación rectificativa of an earlier period, and a rectificativa under the régimen especial del criterio de caja remain refused with named findings. Those refusals are the conditions that would reopen this record, together with any official change to the two art. 15.5 methods or to the 89.Cinco and 114.Dos period rules.
