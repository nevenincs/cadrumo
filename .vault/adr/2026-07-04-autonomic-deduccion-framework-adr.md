# `autonomic-deduccion-framework` adr: `per-ccaa autonomic deduccion auto-trigger framework` | (**status:** `proposed`)

## Problem Statement

Issue #85 (systemic, generalizing #550): the Modelo 100 2025 revision declares 482 comunidad-specific `deduccion_autonomica_res` casillas across the 15 ordinary common-regime CCAA (`andalucia_res` 21, `aragon_res` 19, `asturias_res` 36, `i_baleares_res` 29, `canarias_res` 43, `cantabria_res` 32, `castilla_la_mancha_res` 29, `castilla_y_leon_res` 26, `catalunya_res` 16, `c_valenciana_res` 67, `extremadura_res` 19, `galicia_res` 32, `madrid_res` 33, `murcia_res` 41, `la_rioja_res` 39). Of these, exactly one -- Madrid casilla `1039` "Por nacimiento o adopcion de hijos" -- carries a `formula`/`target_casilla_id`; the remaining 481 are pure operator-typed manual boxes with no `binding` and no `formula`. `2026-07-01-autonomic-deduccion-auto-trigger-adr` built the Madrid slice as a worked first case and explicitly deferred generalization: its Python injector (`_inject_derived_autonomic_deduccion_facts`), its family-model helper (`RentaFamilyProfile.madrid_nacimiento_adopcion_eligible_count` / `_weighted_count`), and its verify-path advisory (`_madrid_nacimiento_adopcion_eligibility_advisory_finding`, landed on commit `aa51249a9a`, issue #596) are all Madrid-specific names and Madrid-specific gates, not parameterized by CCAA.

The generalization gap is concrete, not hypothetical: `semantic_role = "irpf_deduccion_andalucia_nacimiento_adopcion"` (casilla `0850`, "Por nacimiento o adopcion de hijos o acogimiento familiar de menores") is regulatorily the same shape as Madrid `1039` -- a per-child amount gated on residence, a birth/adoption date window, and (per the bundled manual) an income limit -- yet it remains a manual box today. At least 25 further nacimiento/adopcion-shaped semantic roles exist across Aragon, Asturias, Baleares, Canarias, Cantabria, Castilla-La Mancha, Castilla y Leon, Cataluna, C. Valenciana, Galicia, La Rioja, Murcia, and a second Madrid box (`irpf_deduccion_madrid_adopcion_internacional`, casilla `1040`, the +50% uplift explicitly deferred by the prior ADR). Beyond the nacimiento/adopcion family, the same 482-casilla set spans cross-cutting shapes the framework must eventually enroll: familia numerosa, discapacidad, gastos educativos, vivienda habitual, arrendamiento, guarderia, empleada de hogar -- each repeating across most of the 15 CCAA with a comunidad-specific amount/limit but a recognizably shared eligibility shape.

Re-running the Madrid slice's design once per comunidad -- a bespoke Python method name, a bespoke advisory function, a bespoke fail-closed predicate -- for potentially 100+ enrollments does not scale and directly risks the pattern `registry-resolver-family-extraction` and `calculation-source-canonical-mechanism` forbid: N one-off implementations of the same mechanism instead of one canonical mechanism with N declared enrollments. This ADR decides the reusable framework: the registry/data-model shape a NEW autonomic-deduccion enrollment declares, and the shared runtime mechanism (calculate-path injection, verify-path advisory) every enrollment rides, so that adding CCAA #2 (or the international-uplift sibling of an existing enrollment) is a bounded, mostly-declarative change rather than a new bespoke Python surface.

## Considerations

- The Madrid slice (`2026-07-01-autonomic-deduccion-auto-trigger-adr`, commits `8ea7ef3a78` + `aa51249a9a`) is the validated worked example, not a discarded prototype: its three-layer shape -- (1) a Python derived-fact injector for the terms no casilla can express (per-descendant date-window/convivencia count, unidad-familiar base aggregate), (2) a registry formula on the target casilla applying the income gate and CCAA-scoped amount parameter, (3) a verify-path advisory sharing the exact same eligibility primitive the calculate-path injector uses -- is the correct division of labor and this ADR keeps it. What must change is that the count/eligibility logic, the CCAA gate, and the advisory wiring become data-driven per enrollment instead of a new Python function per comunidad.
- `aeat-registry-authority-flow` and `aeat-schema-central-config`: every regulatory value (per-child amount, income limit, applicability window length) must be registry TOML (a `parameter`), never a Python literal; only the axis a casilla genuinely cannot express (the date-window count, the unidad-familiar aggregate) is Python-derived, per `calculation-source-canonical-mechanism`.
- `casilla-grounding-corrects-actividades-default-by-section`: every comunidad-specific casilla is already grounded to the LIRPF framework `ley-35-2006:art-77`, keyed by its renumbering-immune `section` tag (`madrid_res`, `andalucia_res`, ...); a new formula/parameter for that casilla must add the comunidad-specific disposicion (e.g. `madrid-dl-1-2010:art-4`) alongside art-77, not replace it.
- `registry-calculation-legal-grounding` and `legal-grounding-verifies-bundled-authoritative-corpus`: each per-child amount and income limit is a distinct regulatory figure per CCAA per year that must cite its own binding provision (its comunidad's ley/decreto, not a generic placeholder) and be cross-checked against the bundled manual/BOE before shipping -- grounding cost scales linearly with enrollment count and is not reduced by the framework; the framework only reduces mechanism-authoring cost.
- `no-silent-under-declaration` (symmetric for a deduccion, per the Madrid ADR's D4): a deduccion's failure mode is over-claim, not silent under-declaration, so the auto-trigger must remain fail-closed on any indeterminate eligibility axis (unidad-familiar aggregation depending on unpersisted spouse data, ambiguous marital/declaration-type unit) and surface a Notice rather than silently apply an unverified amount. This is a framework-level invariant, not a Madrid-only one: whichever new CCAA enrollment shares Madrid's need for the spouse's base imponible inherits the same fail-closed default automatically if the eligibility predicate is declared data rather than re-derived per enrollment.
- `no-dormant-source-resolvers` and `one-aggregation-path-pull-equals-calculate`: the mechanism must stay inside the enrolled `_profile_binding.py` mesh (both the calculate path and the Sheets-pull path already run the injector); a framework must not introduce a new binding source kind or a second resolution path.
- `retired-enum-members-need-consumer-reconciliation` / `aeat-architecture-boundaries`: CCAA is already the closed `cadrumo.domain.contribuyente.CCAA` StrEnum (15 ordinary common-regime members; foral/ciudades autonomas excluded, `2026-04-28-ccaa-in-profile-adr`); the framework's per-enrollment CCAA key must be values of this enum, never a re-declared string set.
- The eligibility shapes are not uniform across the 25+ nacimiento/adopcion-family casillas even though they share a family resemblance: some carry a flat amount (Baleares `irpf_deduccion_baleares_nacimiento`), some a per-child amount with a multi-year window (Madrid, Andalucia), some a discapacidad-conditioned uplift (Aragon `nacimiento_hijo_discapacidad`), some an anticipated-payment variant (Baleares), some a guarda/acogimiento variant (C. Valenciana, Andalucia). A framework that forces every enrollment through one rigid predicate shape (e.g. exactly Madrid's double income-limit gate) will not fit several of these; the eligibility predicate must be composable from a small typed vocabulary of conditions, not a single fixed template.
- `revision-resolution-is-law-determined`: every parameter, limit, and window is per filing year (the registry revision), never hardcoded across years; a comunidad that changes its amount year-to-year (as Madrid already does: 600 euros pre-2023, 721,70 euros from 2023) is expressed as multiple parameter value rows with `date_axis`/`valid_from`/`valid_to`, exactly as the Madrid parameter already does -- no new date-versioning mechanism is needed.

## Considered options

**Data model for declaring a new autonomic-deduccion enrollment (decision D1):**

- **A -- A typed AutonomicDeduccionEnrollment registry construct declaring: CCAA key (the CCAA enum), target casilla id, per-child/flat amount parameter ref(s), an income-limit predicate expression (reusing existing formula ops: greater_than/min/max over casilla refs and CCAA-scoped parameters), an applicability-window length (years), and a small closed eligibility_axis tag naming which Python-derived count/aggregate primitive it needs (CHOSEN).** Pro: each enrollment is registry TOML (`aeat-registry-authority-flow`), the amount/limit/window are typed parameters (grounded per-CCAA per `registry-calculation-legal-grounding`), and the framework only needs to implement each distinct eligibility_axis primitive once (e.g. descendant_entry_window_count, unidad_familiar_income_aggregate, familia_numerosa_flag, discapacidad_flag) and dispatch by tag -- new enrollments that reuse an existing axis (the 25+ nacimiento/adopcion casillas mostly reuse descendant_entry_window_count) require zero new Python. Con: an enrollment whose eligibility shape needs a genuinely new axis (e.g. a residence-duration-in-municipio test) still requires one new Python primitive -- but that cost is paid once per axis, not once per CCAA.
- **B -- One bespoke Python injector plus one bespoke advisory function per CCAA-deduccion pair (the Madrid slice's shape, left un-generalized).** Rejected: this is the status quo the problem statement documents does not scale to 100+ enrollments; it also directly risks `registry-resolver-family-extraction`'s "N one-off implementations" anti-pattern and makes every new comunidad's deduccion a full mechanism-design exercise instead of a declarative enrollment.
- **C -- A fully generic rule-expression DSL letting registry TOML express arbitrary Python-equivalent logic (no closed eligibility_axis vocabulary; free-form predicate composition over any profile fact).** Rejected: the existing formula-expression runtime is deliberately a closed, typed op vocabulary (`aeat-schema-central-config`'s "closed value sets are enums" applies equally to a runtime op set); an open DSL re-derives an entire second expression language outside that runtime and duplicates `no-dormant-source-resolvers`'s "no parallel mechanism" concern at the sub-formula level. The bounded per-axis primitive set (Option A) stays inside the existing formula/binding channel contract; new eligibility shapes are added as new closed axis tags with a reviewed Python primitive, not as free-form rules.
- **D -- Do not build a framework; enroll each comunidad's deduccion ad hoc as its own small ADR/plan, repeating the Madrid pattern each time.** Rejected: explicitly the option this ADR exists to replace; it was viable for exactly one worked case (per the prior ADR's own framing -- Madrid nacimiento/adopcion as the worked first case) but the 482-casilla / 25+-enrollment scale documented above makes per-CCAA re-design a standing tax on every future comunidad rollout.

**Shared runtime mechanism across calculate and verify (decision D2):**

- **A -- Generalize the existing three shared primitives (`is_madrid_resident` to `is_ccaa_resident(ccaa)`, `is_indeterminate_unidad_familiar`, the candidate weighted-count helper to an axis-keyed dispatch) in `_profile_binding.py`/`_autonomic_deduccion_advisory.py` so one injector function and one advisory function are parameterized by the enrollment's declared CCAA/axis/casilla rather than one function per CCAA (CHOSEN).** Pro: preserves the exact calculate-verify parity guarantee the Madrid slice's own test (`test_advisory_weighted_count_matches_calculate_path_candidate_count`) proves -- the shared primitive stays the single source of truth for what the calculate path would have computed, now dispatched by enrollment rather than hardcoded to Madrid. Con: the dispatch table becomes a piece of framework surface that itself needs test coverage (mitigated: it is a thin registry-driven dispatch over already-tested per-axis primitives, not new eligibility logic).
- **B -- Duplicate `_inject_derived_autonomic_deduccion_facts` and `_madrid_nacimiento_adopcion_eligibility_advisory_finding` once per new CCAA (copy-paste-rename).** Rejected: this is Option B under D1 restated at the runtime layer; it is the literal anti-pattern `registry-resolver-family-extraction` names, and a fix to the shared fail-closed predicate (e.g. a future broadening of what counts as an indeterminate unidad familiar) would have to be hand-propagated across every copy.
- **C -- Move the eligibility/advisory logic fully into the registry formula layer, dropping the Python injector.** Rejected for the same reason Option C was rejected in the Madrid ADR (decision D1 there): the per-descendant date-window count and the unidad-familiar base aggregate are not expressible as a pure casilla-referencing formula; the registry cannot see profile facts directly, only through the binding/injector channel.

## Constraints

- Grounding cost does not shrink with the framework: each of the ~100+ candidate enrollments still needs its own per-CCAA legal citation (comunidad ley/decreto establishing the amount), its own bundled-manual cross-check (`legal-grounding-verifies-bundled-authoritative-corpus`), and its own `reviewed_by` provenance chain before shipping as filing-grade. The framework bounds mechanism cost, not grounding cost; a plan built on this ADR must budget per-enrollment legal research as its own line item, not assume it is amortized away.
- The unidad_familiar_income_aggregate axis inherits the Madrid slice's open F9 finding: whether the app persists a spouse's base imponible anywhere retrievable. Every enrollment declaring this axis is fail-closed (advisory-only) until that data-availability question is resolved; this ADR does not resolve F9, it generalizes the fail-closed default so a future resolution of F9 benefits every enrollment simultaneously rather than requiring a re-fix per CCAA.
- Not every one of the 25+ nacimiento/adopcion-shaped casillas shares Madrid's exact predicate (double income-limit, 3-year window, split-in-two prorrateo). Enrolling a new CCAA against this framework requires per-casilla verification of its actual regulatory shape against its own comunidad's law and the bundled manual -- the framework's axis vocabulary must stay small and composable (Considerations) rather than assuming uniformity; forcing a mismatched casilla onto the wrong axis is a correctness risk, not a mechanical one.
- The registry formula/parameter per-enrollment additions are per-filing-year (`revision-resolution-is-law-determined`); a comunidad enrollment authored against the 2025 revision does not automatically apply to 2024/2026 -- each year's revision needs its own parameter values (and, where the law changed, its own eligibility predicate), consistent with how the Madrid 600/721,70-euro split is already modelled.
- Parent stability: this ADR depends on the Madrid slice's runtime surfaces (`_profile_binding.py`, `_autonomic_deduccion_advisory.py`, `_semantic_role_resolution.py`) remaining the enrolled mesh entry points -- all already merged and test-covered at HEAD (`aa51249a9a`, `8ea7ef3a78`). No frontier dependency; this is a generalization of shipped, tested code.
- CCAA scope is bounded to the 15 ordinary common-regime comunidades already in the CCAA enum; the foral regimes (Pais Vasco, Navarra) and the autonomous cities (Ceuta, Melilla) are explicitly out of scope for this framework, consistent with `2026-04-28-ccaa-in-profile-adr`'s existing exclusion (they raise a ForalRegimeError / are not modelled by this profile axis at all).

## Implementation

Four layers, generalizing the Madrid slice's three-layer shape with one new declarative layer in front.

1. Enrollment declaration (new, registry TOML). A new revision-scoped autonomic-deduccion-enrollment construct (or, if narrower reuse of the existing construct shape suffices without a new top-level array, a typed extension of the existing construct/formulas grouping -- a plan-phase decision) declares, per casilla: ccaa (a CCAA enum value), target_casilla_id, eligibility_axis (one of a closed, small set: descendant_entry_window_count, unidad_familiar_income_aggregate, familia_numerosa_flag, discapacidad_flag, flat_amount for the simplest Baleares-style case with no per-child window), the amount/limit parameter refs it consumes, and its legal_refs/source_refs (the comunidad-specific disposicion plus `ley-35-2006:art-77`, per `casilla-grounding-corrects-actividades-default-by-section`). This is the only per-enrollment authoring surface for a casilla whose eligibility shape matches an already-implemented axis; no Python change is required to enroll it.

2. Shared Python derived-fact dispatch (generalizes `_profile_binding.py`). `_inject_derived_autonomic_deduccion_facts` is generalized to iterate the snapshot's declared enrollments (rather than being hardcoded to Madrid) and, per enrollment, dispatch to the one Python primitive implementing its eligibility_axis (e.g. a descendant-entry-window-count helper parameterized by CCAA and window length, rather than a Madrid-only method). The synthetic fact keys become enrollment-scoped (e.g. keyed by ccaa and casilla id) rather than the Madrid-only key name in use today. `RentaFamilyProfile.madrid_nacimiento_adopcion_eligible_count` is renamed/generalized to accept a CCAA plus window-years parameterization (or superseded by a shared helper) so a second CCAA enrollment does not require a bespoke sibling method -- this move follows `aeat-architecture-boundaries`'s one-atomic-commit relocation discipline, not an incremental bridge.

3. Registry formula per enrollment (already-existing mechanism, per-enrollment authoring). Each enrolled casilla gains a formula expressed with the same existing op vocabulary the Madrid formula already uses (if_then_else, greater_than, sum, multiply, min/max, CCAA-scoped parameters) -- no new formula op is required for the axes already identified; a genuinely new eligibility shape (e.g. a residence-duration test) may require one new op, added once and reused by every future enrollment needing it, never a per-CCAA formula-op fork.

4. Shared verify-path advisory dispatch (generalizes `_autonomic_deduccion_advisory.py`). The Madrid-specific advisory finding builder is generalized to one enrollment-driven advisory builder that iterates the snapshot's enrollments, and -- for any enrollment whose declared eligibility_axis resolved fail-closed (candidate count positive but the calculate-path injector could not resolve determinacy) and whose target casilla is zero -- emits the same shape of non-blocking Notice the Madrid case emits today, parameterized by the enrollment's own casilla/CCAA/amount. The shared residency/indeterminacy/weighted-count primitives remain the single source of truth calculate and verify both read, preserving the calculate-verify parity property the Madrid slice's own regression test locks (`one-aggregation-path-pull-equals-calculate`).

A follow-on plan enrolls the second worked case (Andalucia `0850`, the closest one-axis-reuse candidate to Madrid `1039`) as the framework's own proof: if `0850` enrolls with zero new Python (only a new TOML enrollment plus formula plus parameters), the framework's core claim is validated; if it needs Python beyond the shared descendant_entry_window_count primitive, that need is itself the signal that the axis vocabulary must be revisited before further CCAA rollout.

## Rationale

Option A (D1) is the only option that turns the Madrid slice's proven three-layer mechanism into a mechanism whose per-enrollment marginal cost is registry TOML, while keeping the closed, typed, reviewable op/axis vocabulary `aeat-schema-central-config` and `no-dormant-source-resolvers` require -- Option B (status quo) does not scale past the single Madrid case already documented as unable to generalize on its own terms, and Option C (a free-form rule DSL) trades a bounded, auditable axis set for an open one that duplicates the formula runtime outside its own contract. The knockout evidence is empirical, not projected: casilla `0850` (Andalucia nacimiento/adopcion) already exists, already shares Madrid's regulatory shape, and already sits unenrolled -- the framework is validated the moment a second CCAA enrolls against the same axis primitive with no new bespoke function, which Option A's design makes possible and Option B's does not. Decision D2 follows directly: generalizing the shared calculate/verify primitives (rather than duplicating them) is what preserves the exact parity guarantee (`one-aggregation-path-pull-equals-calculate`) the Madrid slice already tested, at the framework's proven single-primitive-per-axis granularity rather than at the Madrid ADR's single-primitive-per-CCAA granularity.

The eligibility-axis vocabulary is deliberately small and closed rather than attempting full coverage of all 482 casillas' regulatory nuance up front: `casilla-grounding-corrects-actividades-default-by-section`'s lesson (the same section tag can hide heterogeneous regulatory content across a CCAA's casillas) applies equally here -- forcing every deduccion through one rigid template would misclassify the anticipated-payment / guarda / discapacidad-uplift variants the Considerations section documents. A composable, closed axis set that grows by one reviewed Python primitive per genuinely new eligibility shape (never per CCAA) is the durable middle ground between a single hardcoded case and an unbounded free-form rule engine.

## Consequences

- Establishes the reusable per-CCAA autonomic-deduccion enrollment framework: a declarative registry construct, a small closed eligibility_axis primitive vocabulary, and one generalized calculate-path injector plus one generalized verify-path advisory builder -- replacing the Madrid slice's CCAA-specific function names with an enrollment-driven dispatch that the Madrid casilla (`1039`) itself is refactored to ride, proving the generalization preserves its existing test coverage.
- Unlocks the roughly 25 nacimiento/adopcion-family casillas as the framework's first wave of near-zero-Python enrollments (reusing the descendant_entry_window_count axis), and leaves the remaining roughly 450 comunidad-specific casillas (familia numerosa, discapacidad, vivienda, gastos educativos, etc.) as a mapped-but-not-yet-enrolled backlog requiring additional axis primitives -- this ADR does not claim those axes are already covered.
- Per-enrollment legal grounding remains a linear cost the framework does not amortize; a plan built on this ADR must sequence CCAA/casilla enrollments with their own grounding research, not assume framework existence substitutes for it.
- The fail-closed unidad_familiar_income_aggregate axis inherits the Madrid slice's open data-availability question (F9); until resolved, every enrollment declaring that axis ships advisory-only for indeterminate units, which is an honest limitation shared framework-wide rather than a per-enrollment surprise.
- Refactoring RentaFamilyProfile's Madrid-named method and the Madrid-named advisory/injector functions into their generalized, enrollment-parameterized replacements is a relocation subject to `aeat-architecture-boundaries`'s one-atomic-commit discipline (canonical-site move, consumer sweep, and test updates in one commit) -- the existing Madrid tests must continue to pass unchanged in behavior, only in call shape.
