# `aeat-export-fragment-generator-authority` adr: `official-binary coordinates, reviewed render profiles, and semantic maps generate export fragments` | (**status:** `accepted`)

## Problem Statement

The accepted official-binary and semantic-map split leaves some wire facts unauthorised when the exact workbook field anchor carries no usable content, and it does not classify variable composition wrappers. The generator needs one exhaustive reviewed authority for those absent wire facts and an explicit boundary between fixed-width records and variable envelopes before real-target generation can resume.

Modelo 390 adds a distinct unresolved composition boundary. Its source-scoped page zero is a complete thirteen-anchor `<T...<AUX>...</AUX>` header, but it is neither a source-total-bearing fixed record nor either variable-envelope form already decided below. Its four in-window designs also lack explicit epoch identities and have no reviewed semantic maps or render profiles. Tree generation therefore cannot start by treating page zero as an ordinary record, dropping it, or borrowing the removed manual tree.

## Considerations

- The original coordinate-versus-meaning authority split is grounded by `2026-08-10-aeat-export-fragment-generator-authority-source-authority-research`.
- The unresolved fixed-width totals, absent wire facts, and variable-wrapper boundary are grounded by `2026-08-10-aeat-export-fragment-generator-authority-s08-authority-gap-research`.
- The real-target completeness and refusal requirements are recorded by `2026-08-10-aeat-export-fragment-generator-authority-s08-independent-review-audit`.
- Export fragments are filing-correctness inputs, so partial coverage, implicit defaults, conflicting authorities, and guessed output are unacceptable.
- Revision selection and applicability remain authored registry decisions governed by the parent relayout ADR; this decision does not infer revision boundaries.
- The Modelo 390 missing-authoring prerequisite is recorded by `2026-08-13-aeat-design-relayout-boundary-blocked-waves-carry-forward-audit`.
- The existing typed auxiliary-header roles and product/software identity split are grounded by `2026-08-08-m200-export-envelope-tag-adr`; Modelo 390 reuses those roles without treating a removed tree as an oracle.

## Considered options

- **Keep refusing every design with absent field-level wire facts. Rejected as the permanent model.** It is safe but prevents reviewed source-scoped conventions from completing an otherwise authoritative design.
- **Put wire formatting in the semantic map. Rejected.** It mixes registry meaning with source wire representation and creates a second coordinate-adjacent authority.
- **Infer formats from AEAT type and width or from a neighbouring tree. Rejected.** Defaults and legacy-tree oracles turn incomplete evidence into silent filing behavior.
- **Use an exhaustive source-SHA-pinned per-design render profile. Chosen.** It provides one reviewed home for wire facts absent at exact workbook field anchors while leaving official coordinates and registry meaning in their existing authorities.
- **Treat variable wrappers as fixed records with inferred totals. Rejected.** It truncates composition semantics and falsely converts a variable envelope into a fixed-width record.
- **Treat Modelo 390 page zero as a fixed record by assigning its terminal extent as a declared total. Rejected.** The source does not declare that total; synthesising one would contradict the fixed-record refusal boundary.
- **Omit Modelo 390 page zero and emit only numbered pages. Rejected.** It removes the source-declared filing and product header and cannot produce the official composition.
- **Model Modelo 390 page zero as a typed fixed auxiliary envelope header. Chosen.** Render its thirteen exact source anchors as one source-hash-bound 328-byte header, prove contiguous anchored geometry and the terminal `</AUX>` literal, then append the ordered numbered fixed records. The 328-byte header extent is an emitted-byte invariant over exact anchors, not a parser `declared_total` and not permission to infer totals for fixed records.

## Constraints

- The exact bundled official binary, selected through the source catalogue and verified by SHA-256, is the sole coordinate input.
- The shipped typed record-design parser output is consumed directly; derived extraction files are never inputs.
- The parser may recover official integer totals expressed by the source's `Total:` label and must prove them against terminal extent. It never invents a total, content value, coordinate, or wire interpretation.
- A separate per-modelo, per-design semantic map supplies only registry meaning and is keyed by exact source anchors. The map and every fragment set carry one required exact `source_ref` and `source_sha256`; design epoch alone is not an admissible identity. Renderer formatting and transport interpretation never enter semantic-map entries. The join proves map-to-parser source identity, exact anchor and record bijection, selected-revision source membership, and exact projection-declaration admission, and refuses the entire design on missing, duplicate, fuzzy, ambiguous, implicit-source, or source-drifted matches.
- One exhaustive per-design render profile, bound to the exact source SHA-256, is the sole reviewed authority for wire facts absent at their exact workbook field anchors. It may not override or conflict with wire facts present in the official source.
- Every profile rule resolves to exact source anchors. Coverage must be complete for every otherwise-unrenderable field, including all 126 smaller-width fields; group conventions require an explicit reviewed membership set rather than type-and-width inference.
- Profiles distinguish unsigned `Num` handling from signed `N` handling and define the sign representation explicitly. No numeric, decimal, date, flag, identifier, digit-string, or literal default is implicit.
- Missing anchors, uncovered fields, duplicate or overlapping rules, conflicts with official content, inapplicable design identity, or source-hash drift refuse the whole design before output.
- `DP200000` is a typed variable envelope and composition wrapper outside fixed-width record generation. It is never truncated to its fixed prefix, assigned an inferred fixed total, or emitted as a fixed record; its envelope and composition behavior must be modeled separately and proven before any generation for that design.
- `DP30300` is likewise a typed variable envelope, not a fixed record and not part of the fixed-record semantic-map entry set. Its thirteen prefix anchors, Variable body, relative eighteen-byte closer, and derived total form one source-hash-bound composition contract shared across the five explicit Modelo 303 epochs.
- The four in-window Modelo 390 sources carry explicit, exact epoch identities `2022`, `2023`, `2024`, and `2025`, respectively. Each selected Modelo 390 revision admits exactly one matching source identity and hash; an absent epoch, cross-epoch source, or multi-source selection refuses before parsing.
- Modelo 390 page zero is a typed fixed auxiliary envelope header outside the fixed-record semantic-map entry set. Its thirteen exact anchors occupy positions 1 through 328 contiguously and terminate in the source literal `</AUX>`. Generation emits those anchors once, proves exactly 328 bytes, and then appends numbered fixed records in source order. It never writes `328` into the parser's absent `declared_total`, generalises terminal extent into a fixed-record rule, or accepts a gap, overlap, missing anchor, altered literal, or additional anchor.
- The Modelo 390 page-zero roles are exact: modelo, discriminant, annual period, fixed markers, auxiliary tags, and reserved spans come from source literals or source-declared blank policy; filing year comes from the draft; program version and software-developer tax identifier come from the canonical product/software identity authority. Presenter, taxpayer, or generic legacy header paths may not substitute for the software-developer identity.
- Each Modelo 390 epoch owns one reviewed semantic map and one exhaustive source-hash-bound render profile. Every numbered-page parser anchor classifies exactly once as a literal, reserve, canonical `CasillaId`, canonical typed producer, draft field, or revision-admitted typed projection. Printed box number, description text, offset, neighbouring fields, and the removed manual tree are diagnostics only, never semantic-home authorities.
- A Modelo 390 semantic map may reuse a canonical owner already present in the loaded revision authority, including the revision-independent continuity identity, only after exact source-anchor adjudication. A missing owner is a prerequisite to add a typed canonical owner and value-arrival path before map authoring; the map may not create an open string key, generic value bag, modelo-specific producer duplicate, or compatibility reader.
- Semantic-home review is per epoch. Unchanged source anchors may reuse an earlier adjudication only when source identity, anchor identity, source content, and canonical owner are unchanged; every added, removed, or meaning-changed anchor is reviewed independently even when its byte offset is stable.
- The four-byte AEAT program identifier and nine-byte software-developer tax identifier are one typed product/software identity authority. They are not filing producers, presenter or taxpayer identities, generic headers, guessed literals, or defaults. Generation refuses until explicit product authority supplies both values.
- The envelope definition declares exact prefix-anchor semantics, source-ordered body-record families, closer reuse of modelo, discriminant, year, period, and record-type semantics, and emitted-byte total derivation. Static provenance carries the envelope schema and digest, source hash, generated-layout identity, product-authority requirement, closer derivation, and total derivation. Filing-period values, product-identity values, emitted occurrences, member digests, payload digest, and total belong only to the filing-instance render result and its acceptance evidence, never to generated registry provenance.
- Neighbouring fragment trees are neither inputs nor correctness oracles. Legacy trees are explicitly unverified bootstrap evidence and may not supply profile rules or defaults.
- Generated replacements are a hard cutover: superseded manual fragment trees, single-file/direct-revision compatibility loaders, derivative record-design fallbacks, and print-only unmeasured paths are deleted. No legacy fallback, migration support, or silent green result remains.
- Typed, hash-pinned exceptions may describe parser or source anomalies but may never supply coordinates, bypass mapping bijection, override official content, or substitute for exhaustive render-profile coverage.
- Generated TOML and provenance are CLI-owned and never hand-edited.
- One invocation targets one authored revision/design pair; revision splitting, renames, and migration remain explicit outside the generator.
- User-profile `export_headers` metadata and export-only `filing_export.*` paths are legacy producer redeclarations and are deleted, not migrated or aliased. Registry export meaning reaches the renderer only through canonical typed owners and the filing producer snapshot.
- Modelo 100 filing modality is a semantic carve-out rather than an export producer: its sole persisted home is `renta_filing.declaration_type`, typed by the public core `RentaDeclaracionType`. Bindings, questions, setup, wizard, calculation, comparator, and XML projection consume that one axis directly; `FilingProducerKey` does not duplicate it.
- The rental reduction tier is not export metadata. Its canonical profile home is `renta_rental.reduccion_art_23_2_tier_2024`, retaining its existing enum and legal grounding.
- Old profile paths, old enum import paths, fallback reads, aliases, silent migration, and re-entry through generic profile facts are hard failures.

## Implementation

Build the development-only generator under `dev/registry/`. It parses the selected official binary into an intermediate representation retaining source reference and hash, workbook format, sheet, source row or cell anchor, ordinal, record identity, offset, length, AEAT type, normalized description, validation/content metadata, declared total, and typed record kind.

Normalize official `Total:` integers without synthesizing absent values. Represent variable envelopes separately from fixed-width records, and block target generation until their composition contract has passed dedicated structural and byte-level proof.

For Modelo 303, generate the typed DP30300 envelope declaration and fixed-record layout as static registry authority without accepting or rendering filing-instance values. The application filing path then loads that generated authority and, from one approved `ModeloDraft`, immutable `FilingProducerSnapshot`, exact selected `RegistrySnapshot`, explicit product/software identity, and snapshot-owned projection plan, resolves every admitted record occurrence through the exhaustive production field resolver. Compose the exact prefix, ordered rendered occurrences, and relative closer only after that resolution; derive and prove the Variable total from the complete emitted bytes. Refuse malformed, incomplete, reordered, duplicate-addressed, source-drifted, applicability-inconsistent, occurrence-collapsed, or product-authority-incomplete envelopes before output.

For Modelo 390, first bind the four catalogue sources to their exact epoch identities and selected revisions. Extend the parser-owned intermediate representation with the typed fixed auxiliary envelope-header classification rather than assigning page zero a total. Render and prove page zero independently from the fixed-record renderer, then append every numbered fixed record in official order. Author and review the 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025 semantic maps and render profiles as four independent source-scoped artefacts before generating any Modelo 390 revision tree.

Join fixed-width source fields to the reviewed semantic map, then resolve only absent wire facts through the exhaustive render profile for the exact design and source hash. Validate source applicability, complete semantic bijection, exact-anchor profile coverage, rule consistency, explicit `Num` and signed-`N` behavior, and agreement with every present official content value before rendering.

Generate the entire target revision's `export/` tree plus an adjacent non-loader provenance manifest. The manifest records source, semantic-map, and render-profile digests; parser, profile, and generator schema versions; target revision; normalized loader-semantic digest; and file digests.

Generate into a temporary target, load and validate it completely, compare normalized semantics and provenance, and swap atomically. `--check` regenerates independently and refuses semantic, profile, provenance, or exact generated drift.

The profile-side cutover deletes the `export_headers` selector taxonomy and export-only `filing_export.*` fields. It atomically retargets every Renta modality consumer to `renta_filing.declaration_type`, relocates `RentaDeclaracionType` to the core public facade without an old-path re-export, and retargets the rental reduction tier to `renta_rental.reduccion_art_23_2_tier_2024`. Tests reject every removed path and import surface.

The static generator gates cover parser completeness, official-total recovery, fixed-record geometry, envelope-schema validation, canonical-reference validity, mapping bijection, exhaustive profile coverage, `Num` versus signed-`N` encoding, all 126 smaller-field rules, deterministic double generation, mutation failures, extent and overlap, complete registry load, provenance, and repository drift without filing-instance inputs. Application filing gates separately cover approved-draft and immutable-snapshot matching, exhaustive all-kind resolution, applicability, zero, one, or many record occurrences, repeated DP30302 ordering, prefix and closer derivation, total length, payload digest, and real emitted bytes.

## Rationale

The chosen profile is the narrowest extension that preserves the decisive split in `2026-08-10-aeat-export-fragment-generator-authority-source-authority-research`: the official design owns coordinates and present wire facts, the semantic map owns registry meaning, and a reviewed source-bound profile owns only wire facts the exact anchors omit. The authority gaps and variable-envelope classification in `2026-08-10-aeat-export-fragment-generator-authority-s08-authority-gap-research` rule out parser invention, type-and-width defaults, semantic-map expansion, and fixed-record truncation. Modelo 390's fixed auxiliary header preserves the same boundary: exact source anchors authorize its bytes, while its typed composition keeps the absent fixed-record total honest and prevents either omission or terminal-extent inference from becoming a general rule.

## Consequences

- `2026-08-08-aeat-design-relayout-boundary-export-fragment-generator-adr` remains superseded; this accepted record remains the single governing generator-authority decision.
- The generator remains a prerequisite to relayout steps that promise parsed, non-transcribed export trees; existing manual trees remain unverified until regenerated and proven.
- Render-profile changes become independently reviewable wire-authority changes and their digest becomes part of provenance and drift detection.
- Semantic maps remain reviewable meaning-only artifacts and are the sole reviewed source-epoch authority mapping parser coordinates to typed owners; projection declarations remain coordinate-free revision admission evidence.
- A design containing a variable envelope may publish static fixed-record fragments only after its separate typed envelope declaration passes source, semantic, geometry, composition-rule, and provenance validation. That static publication does not establish filing readiness: production emission remains refused until the application path proves complete typed value arrival, applicability, occurrence order, prefix, closer, and derived total against the committed generated authority.
- Modelo 390 generation remains blocked until its epoch identities, fixed auxiliary header, four semantic maps, four render profiles, canonical-owner prerequisites, and emitted composition proof have landed. The existing generation row is not map-authoring authority.
- The fixed auxiliary envelope-header rule is deliberately narrow to the source-proved thirteen-anchor Modelo 390 shape. Another total-less page or modelo receives no inference or reuse without a separate adjudication.
- Source, parser, or profile corrections can produce large generated diffs, but exhaustive validation and deterministic checks make that drift explicit.
- Tests fail if generation reads legacy layout membership, applies a default to an uncovered anchor, conflates `Num` with signed `N`, leaves a smaller field ungrounded, accepts profile conflict or hash drift, truncates a variable envelope, or permits deleted compatibility surfaces to reappear.
- Renta calculation and question bindings retain their semantic modality axis independently of export layout support; deleting export producer redeclarations cannot delete or default personal-income-tax inputs.

## Amendment (2026-08-14): DP30300 body instances reuse the canonical filing value resolver

The DP30300 structural contract decided above does not authorize a second, generator-local value channel. The body is a sequence of filing-instance record occurrences, not one schema record definition rendered once. One new application-owned, facade-exported `M303FilingEnvelopeRenderRequest` is the closed request boundary: it carries the law-selected `RegistrySnapshot` and its owned generated layout, one approved typed `ModeloDraft`, one immutable `FilingProducerSnapshot`, the explicit prior-domiciliation election required by record applicability, and the separate `AeatProductSoftwareIdentity` required by the envelope prefix. It does not accept a projection plan or rendered members. Modelo, revision, schema, filing year, period, producer snapshot, election, product authority, and layout ownership must agree before resolution.

The application derives `FilingProjectionPlan` internally from the request's snapshot-owned layout and producer snapshot; callers cannot inject it. The canonical filing renderer then resolves applicability, suppression, record occurrence, and every admitted fixed-field kind. Casilla and binding observations arrive through the draft, scalar producer values through the filing snapshot, draft and computed values through their closed renderer axes, and repeated projection values through typed projection references plus positive `FilingRecordRenderContext` occurrence. One facade-exported `M303FilingEnvelopeRenderResult` contains the ordered rendered occurrences and composed envelope evidence minted only by this path. The static development generator never calls the filing-instance path, reimplements its dispatch, or feeds the casilla-only fixed-record codec directly. An arbitrary byte payload, an open field-id or semantic-value mapping, a per-kind generic bag, a casilla-only carrier, a caller-authored projection plan, or a caller-authored list of opaque members is not authority.

Static fragment generation and filing-instance byte proof are separate operations. Static generation joins the official design, reviewed semantic map, source-bound render profile, and selected revision to emit the complete layout plus structural provenance. It accepts no filing period, `ModeloDraft`, `FilingProducerSnapshot`, product/software identity value, casilla-value collection, rendered member, or other filing-instance payload. Its provenance records the envelope schema and digest, source identity, ordered body record definitions, product-authority requirement, prefix derivations, closer derivation, and total-derivation algorithm; it does not fabricate filing values or persist filing-instance member, payload, or total digests. Filing-instance proof takes the closed request above, resolves the exact applicable record occurrences through the production path, composes prefix, members, and closer in memory, derives the emitted total, and may attest transient instance digests without writing taxpayer bytes or instance-derived digests into the generated registry tree.

Repeated and conditional records remain instance-shaped. `projection_rows` may emit zero, one, or multiple positive occurrences only as its typed applicability and projection authority permits; a required applicable family with no occurrence refuses. A fixed record definition is never treated as exactly one body instance merely because it appears once in the generated layout. Record order is the reviewed envelope order, while occurrence order within a record is the canonical filing projection order. Missing, duplicate, extraneous, reordered, cross-snapshot, cross-period, or value-incomplete instances refuse before envelope bytes and before target mutation.

This amendment closes the value-arrival and proof boundary left explicit by `2026-08-11-aeat-export-fragment-generator-authority-s61-dp30300-envelope-authority-research`. It does not move semantic-map authoring, wire formatting, product identity, projection declarations, calculation, applicability, or persistence into the generator. A new serialized prerequisite owns the public application request/result boundary, internal projection-plan derivation, exhaustive occurrence renderer, static-generator input deletion, and focused real-byte proof; every Modelo 303 map and render-profile row remains blocked until it lands. Complete-tree publication writes only static generated authority. The later real-source determinism step verifies both static regeneration/check mode and, through the public application boundary with authoritative filing inputs, exact occurrence order, exhaustive field-kind value arrival, prefix, closer, total, payload digest, and emitted bytes; it does not design either path. Defaults, blank pre-seeding, fixtures standing in for filing authority, compatibility readers, legacy-layout consultation, parallel resolvers, and duplicated business logic remain forbidden.

## Amendment (2026-08-16): the variable-envelope contract is modelo-agnostic

Constraints 60, 61 and 69 above decided the DP30300 and DP200000 composition contract per modelo. That framing is amended: the contract is ONE contract for every modelo whose official design declares a `Total: Variable` envelope, and Modelo 303 is its first enrolment rather than its subject.

The evidence is the bundled corpus itself. Thirty-six variable envelopes across roughly twenty modelos share one grammar — a `<T…>` record identifier, an `<AUX>` block carrying the four-byte program identifier and nine-byte software-developer tax identifier between reserved spans, a `Variable` body, and an eighteen-byte relative closing identifier — and thirty-five of the thirty-six declare a 328-byte prefix. What differs between designs is only which of the shared roles a design PRINTS and how wide each one is, and both of those are facts the design declares. Modelling them as per-modelo code made one modelo's envelope composable by accident of naming rather than by contract, and left every other modelo carrying an identical grammar refused at generation.

The typed declaration is therefore `FilingEnvelopeDefinition`, carried by `ExportLayoutDefinition.filing_envelope`, with `FilingEnvelopePrefixRole` as the one closed role vocabulary. Every fact that was a Modelo 303 constant in code becomes declared data: the record identity, the printed prefix roles, and the prefix extent. Roles must be unique and appear as a subsequence of the canonical source order. `COMPOSED_OPENING_TAG` is the one genuine alternative rather than an omission — Modelos 200, 100 and 714 print the whole seventeen-byte identifier as one source row where Modelo 303 prints six — so a declaration carries either it or all six of its component roles, never both and never a mixture. `closer_derivation` and `total_derivation` become the closed `relative-closer-v1` and `emitted-byte-total-v1`, one algorithm each rather than a per-modelo token.

Genericity does not weaken the source proof; it moves it from naming to assertion. The generator proves, per design and against the pinned binary, that the modelo row names the reviewed modelo, that each opening-tag spelling carries the official identifier for that modelo, that the discriminant is the shared-grammar `0`, that reserved spans carry `BLANCOS` or an empty content cell and nothing else, and that the closer is the eighteen-byte relative identifier for that modelo. Both official closer spellings are admitted — the `AAAAPP` placeholder form most designs print, and the concrete-exercise form Modelos 151 and 200 print — because the filing instance supplies year and period in either case, and pinning a design's own exercise would refuse its next edition. Everything the grammar does NOT fix stays refused: Modelo 220's conditional `(*)[A|E|I|0]` discriminant, and Modelo 309's bundled closer content cell, which reads `"</3090AAAAPP0000>"` where its own row description reads `</T3090+Ejercicio+periodo+0000>` — an AEAT omission of the `T` that a declared, sourced correction may open and a widened pattern may not.

Modelo-specific tax law does not become generic and is not absorbed. Which records apply to a taxpayer, and which operator elections a modelo's records make mandatory, stay per modelo and arrive at the renderer through a registered policy row rather than a branch inside it. A modelo with no registered row carries no constraint beyond the generic ones the renderer already proves, because declaring no conditional record family is a legitimate state rather than an unfinished one. The application boundary is correspondingly `FilingEnvelopeRenderRequest` / `FilingEnvelopeRenderResult` / `render_filing_envelope`, and the request carries no modelo field: the modelo is read from the selected snapshot, which the request's own validators prove agrees with the draft, the producer snapshot, and the layout's owning revision. Admission of the product/software identity is keyed on whether the SELECTED LAYOUT declares an envelope, not on the modelo id, because that identity is required by the envelope prefix itself.

This amendment closes the deferred defect the generic-schema naming gate tracked as its sole allowlist entry (`ExportLayoutDefinition.m303_filing_envelope`); the allowlist is now empty. Six modelos — 151, 200, 202, 308, 322 and 353 — reach the compiler with no per-modelo code, and a seventh is a registry row rather than a branch. What this amendment does NOT do is author their semantic maps or render profiles: structural admission is not grounding, and every modelo still needs its own reviewed map and source-bound profile before any layout is generated for it. Nor does it move product identity, applicability, projections, or persistence into the generator.

Carry-forward, recorded rather than closed: the five committed Modelo 303 generated layouts were re-keyed to the amended shape as a bootstrap, because the loader and the committed tree deadlock — the tree cannot load under the amended model, and the generator cannot read the authorities it needs to regenerate a tree it cannot load. Republication through `publish_validated_generated_export_tree` is blocked independently: all five Modelo 303 revisions are `pending_review`, and the publication path requires a filing-grade snapshot. Their `_generation.provenance.json` therefore still attests the pre-amendment envelope digest, and stays stale until those revisions clear review and the trees are republished through the generator's own authority. This is a known open item, not a silent one.
