# `export-parity` adr: `Typed filing kind on captured observations proves complementaria-only casillas zero for originals` | (**status:** `accepted`)

Accepted 2026-11-01 on the operator's rulings recorded below.
## Problem Statement

Modelo 100 casilla 0604 credits, per quarter of the declarant's Modelo 130, casilla 18 plus the positive part of casilla 19 (Modelo 131: casilla 14 plus the positive part of 15), read from the quarter's effective observation (`src/cadrumo/domain/calculations/registry/observation_fold.py:169-200`). The fold refuses a matched filing that lacks a declared source casilla, so the relation stays unresolved rather than reading zero (`observation_fold.py:56-64`); the live relation capture and the clean-state gate refuse the same gap (`src/cadrumo/adapters/outbound/aeat/sede/declarations_capture.py:405-411`, `src/cadrumo/application/calculations/cross_period_dependency_evaluation.py:311-319`).

Casilla 18 is filled only on a complementaria: "En el supuesto de autoliquidación complementaria, consignaremos en esta casilla el resultado a ingresar de las autoliquidaciones, modelo 130, presentadas anteriormente por el mismo ejercicio y período" (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/aeat_official/instructions/modelo_130/files/modelo-130-instrucciones.html:186`); the record design labels it "[18] A deducir (exclus. complementaria)" (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/aeat_official/disenos_registro/modelo_130/files/01-130-orden-hap-258-2015-ejercicios-2019-y-siguientes-actualizado-marzo-2019-176-kb-xls.xls.extracted.md:64`). Modelo 131 says the same of casilla 14 (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/aeat_official/instructions/modelo_131/files/modelo-131-instrucciones.html:221`). When AEAT's submitted file is unavailable, capture falls back to the declaration PDF (`declarations_capture.py:177-184`), which prints nothing in casilla 18 of an original, and the extraction profile treats an unfilled box as absent (`src/cadrumo/_data/registry/aeat/modelos/130/revisions/2019-y-siguientes/extraction_profiles/0001-declarations.toml:9-13`). Nothing on the observation says original or complementaria (`src/cadrumo/domain/calculations/registry/bindings.py:212-231`), so a correct original quarter leaves 0604 unresolved.

The operator ruled on 2026-11-01: captured observations carry a typed original or complementaria (and sustitutiva where applicable) filing kind; an original proves casilla 18 zero; an unknown kind stays unresolved; no backward-compatibility constraint except that stored revisions stay replayable under their pinned generation. This record decides how.

## Considerations

- Instruction (7) defines a complementaria as one "complementaria de otra u otras autoliquidaciones presentadas anteriormente por el mismo concepto y correspondientes al mismo ejercicio y período" (`modelo-130-instrucciones.html:224` and following; `modelo-131-instrucciones.html:260`). An original has no earlier autoliquidación of the period, so casilla 18 has nothing to hold, and casilla 19 equals casilla 17 (`modelo-130-instrucciones.html:187`).
- The Modelo 130 design of the `2019-y-siguientes` revision declares position 432 "Declaración complementaria." with the closed value domain `blanco o "X"`, followed by the prior justificante at 433 (`01-130-...extracted.md:66-67`). It is the only bundled Modelo 130 design from that revision onward, so the single registry revision `2019-y-siguientes` covers every later year as well. Modelo 131 declares the same marker at 613 (the `2019-2023` revision) and at 692 from the `2024` revision (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/aeat_official/disenos_registro/modelo_131/files/05-131-...extracted.md:89-90`, `06-131-...extracted.md:108-109`, `07-131-...extracted.md:108-109`, and for the newest designs `01-131-ejercicios-2026-...extracted.md:108-109` and `09-131-ejercicio-2026-actualizado-28-09-26.xlsx.extracted.md:108-109`). On the principal page, position 12 ("Indicador de página complementaria", always blank) and position 13 ("Tipo de declaración", I/U/G/N/B) are different fields (`01-130-...extracted.md:40-41`, `:73`; Modelo 131 `:46`).
- From the `2024` revision the Modelo 131 DPA page carries its own position-12 "Indicador de página complementaria" with content "Blanco (No complementaria) o "C" (Complementaria)" (`07-131-...extracted.md:140`; the same line in `06-131-...`, `01-131-...` and `09-131-...`). The text can be read as a second complementaria-declaration marker or as an additional-page indicator. The registry renders that position through `continuation_page_marker`, whose docstring states it is "never of the declaration's amendment kind" (`src/cadrumo/application/filing/record_field_renderer.py:469-486`; field `modelo-131-2025-dpa-complementaria-indicator` in `src/cadrumo/_data/registry/aeat/modelos/131/revisions/2025/export/0002-record-modelo-131-2025-dpa.toml:82-87`, and likewise for the other revisions that carry the DPA page). Which reading is right is not settled by the bundled design.
- Modelo 131 revisions in the registry are `2019-2023`, `2024`, `2025`, `2026` and `2026-3t-4t` (the last valid 2026-07-01 to 2026-12-31 for periods 3T and 4T, `src/cadrumo/_data/registry/aeat/modelos/131/revisions/2026-3t-4t/revision.toml:1-9`). Casilla 14 is authored at the `2019-2023` storage baseline and inherited forward (`131/revisions/2019-2023/casillas/0001-declarations.toml:110-114`).
- The registry represents that marker inconsistently: a header with producer key `amendment_evidence.is_complementaria` on Modelo 130 (`src/cadrumo/_data/registry/aeat/modelos/130/revisions/2019-y-siguientes/export/0001-record-modelo-130-page-01.toml:616-620`), a `manual_input` binding on Modelo 131 from the `2024` revision (`src/cadrumo/_data/registry/aeat/modelos/131/revisions/2024/bindings/0001-declarations.toml:102-104`).
- The submitted-file header projection drops blank values on purpose and returns no facts when the parse fails (`src/cadrumo/adapters/outbound/aeat/sede/declarations_observations.py:304-313`, `:315-336`), so an absent header cannot today tell "blank, so original" from "not read".
- The register `tipo_solicitud` is stored raw (`src/cadrumo/application/live/filed_observation_persistence.py:681-683`); `declaration_kind_for_tipo_solicitud` reads only correction words and never returns ORIGINAL (`src/cadrumo/domain/modelos/filing_record.py:380-399`). Every register value in the tree is from a synthetic fixture ("Presentación", "Original", "complementaria"; for example `src/cadrumo/adapters/outbound/aeat/sede/tests/test_declarations_register_period_multiplicity.py:1-27`). No AEAT register specimen or bundled AEAT page documents that column.
- The declaration-PDF extraction profile is `provisional_pending_specimen` and reads no complementaria box (`extraction_profiles/0001-declarations.toml:6`, `:14-34`).
- The filing chain already carries `declaration_kind` with ORIGINAL, COMPLEMENTARIA, SUSTITUTIVA and RECTIFICATIVA (`filing_record.py:165-171`; `2026-09-17-filing-chain-reconciliation-adr`). But for an AEAT entry with no stated kind, reconciliation infers ORIGINAL merely because the local catalogue holds nothing for the period (`src/cadrumo/application/modelo/filing_chain_reconciliation.py:679-682`); that is chain position, not evidence that AEAT holds no earlier filing.
- The local `CalculationRevisionAmendmentKind` lists only correction kinds (`src/cadrumo/domain/modelos/calculation_revision_amendment.py:22-38`). A revision without `amendment_identity` is filed as ORIGINAL (`src/cadrumo/application/modelo/filing_revision_persistence.py:317`) and its export leaves position 432 blank (`src/cadrumo/application/filing/record_field_renderer.py:501`). The amendment path casts one kind into the other by string value (`src/cadrumo/application/modelo/amendment_actions.py:656`).
- Envelope precedent: a filed declaration's non-casilla facts are typed envelope projections carrying how they were established, so `RegistryModeloObservation` "remains a calculation-only record" (`src/cadrumo/application/calculations/observations_repository.py:137-149`, `:277-302`).
- Stored calculation revisions replay from their persisted binding payloads, not from the observation store (`src/cadrumo/application/modelo/calculation_resolution.py:104-110`, `:327-376`).
- Storage refuses a row below the reader's schema version and has no upgrade chain (`src/cadrumo/adapters/persistence/storage/schema_lineage.py:53-71`). The observation namespace `CALCULATION_OBSERVATIONS_NAMESPACE` is V2 (`src/cadrumo/adapters/persistence/storage/secure_object_namespaces.py:424-430`) and is shared by every modelo's observations, so a bump would force every stored observation of every modelo to be re-captured. The filed-declaration capture namespace is V1 (`:996-1001`).
- Additive optional envelope fields already have precedent in V2 without a bump: `result_disposition`, `prior_domiciliation_election` and `m303_compensation_basis` default to `None` (`src/cadrumo/application/calculations/observations_repository.py:290-321`). The capture payload `FiledDeclaracionObservation` likewise carries defaulted fields such as `headers` (`src/cadrumo/adapters/outbound/aeat/sede/schema.py:318-350`).
- Modelo 130 and 131 rectify by anything other than an increase through a solicitud de rectificación, not a declaration (`modelo-130-instrucciones.html`, "(7) Complementaria", Importante paragraph; `2026-09-17-filing-chain-reconciliation-reference`, Legal mechanics). No rectificativa or sustitutiva for these modelos is evidenced.

## Considered options

- **Kind on `RegistryModeloObservation`.** Rejected. It turns the calculation-only domain record into an evidence carrier against the envelope precedent, and every registry fixture and resolver would carry a field only the fold reads.
- **Kind only on the filing chain (`ModeloRecord.declaration_kind`), joined at fold time.** Rejected as the source. The chain kind of an AEAT entry can be a positional inference (`filing_chain_reconciliation.py:679-682`), and the chain is keyed per filing record while the fold reads the effective observation of a coordinate; a join would make the fold depend on catalogue-observation agreement about which filing is current. The chain remains a consumer and consistency check.
- **Synthesise casilla 18 = 0 at capture for an original.** Rejected. It writes a value AEAT did not print as if observed, breaking the observed-versus-derived distinction and the clean-state value comparison (`cross_period_dependency_evaluation.py:490-505`).
- **Treat a sole register row for the period as proof of original.** Not admitted. Sound under instruction (7), but whether the register lists every presentation of a period (paper filings, bajas, pagination) is not established beyond synthetic fixtures. Kept as a reconsideration path.
- **Chosen: a typed filing-kind projection on the observation envelope, using the one existing `FilingDeclarationKind` vocabulary with a closed evidence basis; a registry declaration marking complementaria-only source casillas; one shared domain predicate that turns "absent + admitted ORIGINAL + declared complementaria-only" into a proven zero.**

## Constraints

- One vocabulary. `FilingDeclarationKind` (`filing_record.py:165-171`) is the only type naming a declaration's legal kind. No new kind enum is introduced. Unknown is the absence of a kind with an explicit `NONE` evidence basis, never a fifth member and never a default ORIGINAL.
- Additive storage. The projection is optional on the envelope and on the capture payload, and neither namespace changes schema version. A stored row without the projection reads as evidence basis `NONE` (unknown), which is the fail-closed reading, so no stored observation of any modelo has to be re-captured and nothing written earlier changes meaning.
- Admitted evidence bases (obligations):
  - `SUBMITTED_FILE_MARKER`: AEAT's own submitted file parsed successfully against the pinned layout, and the record-design "Declaración complementaria" position read `X` gives COMPLEMENTARIA, blank gives ORIGINAL, because the design's value domain for that byte is `blanco o "X"` (`01-130-...extracted.md:66`; Modelo 131 at 613 or 692 as cited, for every revision through `2026-3t-4t`). A failed or partial parse, or a revision whose layout declares no typed marker at that position, yields `NONE`.
  - (Amended 2026-11-02, see the amendment section.) From the `2024` revision, a Modelo 131 file also carries the DPA position-12 indicator on each DPA occurrence. It is read and recorded as an unadmitted reading, not as a basis, because its meaning is unsettled (Considerations). A `C` on any DPA occurrence beside a blank page-1 marker, or a blank beside an `X`, is a disagreement under the rule below. Agreement adds nothing to the admitted basis.
  - `LOCAL_REVISION`: the pending-local envelope written by a local filing takes ORIGINAL when the filed revision carries no `amendment_identity`, otherwise the same-named member of its amendment kind. This is a local assertion; it never becomes filing grade by itself, because the pending layer stays non-official and the clean-state gate keeps blocking it (`2026-09-17-filing-chain-reconciliation-adr`).
- UNVERIFIED, therefore not admitted (fail-closed; recorded as evidence, never as proof): the register `tipo_solicitud` (what AEAT shows for an original is unknown; only synthetic fixtures exist), the declaration PDF's printed complementaria box (no specimen; profile provisional), a sole register row for the period, and the chain's positional ORIGINAL inference. A `tipo_solicitud` naming exactly one correction kind may be recorded as a COMPLEMENTARIA or SUSTITUTIVA reading but is not admitted as proof of ORIGINAL; a value naming none yields `NONE`. Admitting any of these needs a real AEAT specimen or official text and an amendment of this record.
- Disagreement between bases, or an ORIGINAL kind beside a nonzero observed complementaria-only casilla, yields `NONE` and a structured blocking finding naming the coordinate, each basis and the casilla; no side silently wins.
- For Modelo 130 and 131 only ORIGINAL and COMPLEMENTARIA are admissible; any other kind read for them is a contradiction under the rule above.
- The complementaria-only property is a registry declaration on the source casilla, grounded with source citations to the instruction text above: Modelo 130 casilla 18 and Modelo 131 casilla 14 in every revision that carries them. The fold carries no modelo-name or casilla-id branch.
- Proven-zero rule: a declared source casilla absent from the effective observation folds as zero only when the casilla is declared complementaria-only and the envelope's kind is ORIGINAL on an admitted basis. A COMPLEMENTARIA with the casilla absent, or `NONE`, stays unresolved and never reads as zero. The fold, the live relation capture and the clean-state presence check apply the same predicate, and the resolved value records that it was proven zero by filing kind.
- Replay: stored calculation revisions are not recomputed or rewritten; they replay from their persisted payloads under their pinned generation. The new rule applies only to new calculations, and a new calculation over an observation stored before this change sees basis `NONE`.
- (Amended 2026-11-02: settled, the rendering is correct.) Export rendering of the DPA position-12 indicator is outside this record. If the operator or the owning export session settles that the indicator denotes a complementaria declaration, the `continuation_page_marker` rendering for Modelo 131 from the `2024` revision is an export defect for that session, and this record's unadmitted reading may then be amended into an admitted basis.
- Affected prior rulings: `2026-06-10-modelo-130-100-continuity-adr` stays (0604 on the relation channel). `2026-06-05-cross-period-filing-clean-state-adr` stays; a proven zero satisfies source presence only, not any other clean-state requirement, and a pending-local envelope stays blocking. `2026-09-17-filing-chain-reconciliation-adr` needs no text change: its chain `declaration_kind` is unchanged and its positional ORIGINAL inference stays valid for chain linking; this record only bars that inference from serving as observation evidence. `2026-09-27-export-parity-renta-withholding-sources-adr` Step 7 is realised by commit 10dcf5c8fc together with this record.

## Implementation

We will add a typed filing-kind projection to the calculation observation envelope, derive it at capture and at local filing from the admitted bases, declare the complementaria-only source casillas in the registry, and let the shared fold predicate prove them zero for an admitted ORIGINAL. Scope is plan Step `P02.S16` of `2026-11-01-export-parity-plan` (`src/cadrumo/application/live/` and `src/cadrumo/domain/calculations/registry/observation_fold.py`) plus the minimum adjacent edits named here.

- Envelope: an optional `filing_kind` projection on `ObservationEnvelopePayload` (`observations_repository.py:240-330`), defaulting to `None` like `result_disposition` and modelled on `ResultDispositionProjection`. It carries the kind or none, a closed basis enum (`SUBMITTED_FILE_MARKER`, `LOCAL_REVISION`, `NONE`, plus recorded-but-unadmitted readings, including the Modelo 131 DPA indicator) and the source locator, with validators enforcing "no kind if and only if `NONE`". Every reader maps an absent projection to basis `NONE` through one accessor, so no consumer distinguishes "absent" from "unknown". Exact type name and the shape of the unadmitted readings are hypotheses.
- Schema: no version change. `CALCULATION_OBSERVATIONS_NAMESPACE` stays V2 and `AEAT_FILED_DECLARATION_OBSERVATIONS_NAMESPACE` stays V1. The capture payload gains an optional parsed-marker field with the same absent-means-not-read rule. Rows written earlier keep loading and read as `NONE`.
- Capture: the submitted-file reader records the complementaria marker as parsed, blank included, at its record-design locator and distinctly from "not read", through one typed role that both the Modelo 130 header field and the Modelo 131 binding field resolve to. For Modelo 131 from the `2024` revision it also records each DPA occurrence's position-12 indicator as an unadmitted reading. The generic header projection keeps omitting other blank flags. The persistence path (`src/cadrumo/application/live/filed_observation_persistence.py`) derives the projection; PDF-fallback and register-only captures yield `NONE` unless the submitted-file marker is present.
- Local filing: the pending-local writer derives `LOCAL_REVISION` from `amendment_identity`, and the string-value cast at `amendment_actions.py:656` becomes one explicit total mapping from `CalculationRevisionAmendmentKind` to `FilingDeclarationKind`. Retiring `CalculationRevisionAmendmentKind` into `FilingDeclarationKind` (45 files) is plan Step P07.S32, not part of S16. Pending-local envelopes written before this change read as `NONE` and are not rewritten.
- Fold: `fold_observed_requirement_values` and `gather_observed_requirement_values` receive, per matched observation, the kind and basis and the requirement's complementaria-only casilla set through a domain-level input that does not import application types; placing `FilingDeclarationKind` where the registry fold can import it without a cycle (it lives in `domain/modelos` today) is part of the Step. The relation prefill (`src/cadrumo/application/calculations/relation_prefill.py:767-791`), the live relation capture check and the clean-state presence check call the shared predicate.
- Registry: declare complementaria-only on Modelo 130 casilla 18 at the `2019-y-siguientes` revision and on Modelo 131 casilla 14 at the `2019-2023` storage baseline, with source citations, projected onto the relation requirement. A test confirms the declaration is inherited by Modelo 131 `2024`, `2025`, `2026` and `2026-3t-4t`. Source edit, publication and runtime adoption are reported separately.
- Tests through the real capture, persistence, fold and calculation paths with hand-derived oracles: original quarter from a submitted file with a blank marker and from a local revision credits 0604 with casilla 18 as zero; a PDF-only quarter with an unknown kind stays unresolved; a complementaria without casilla 18 stays unresolved; marker and casilla 18 contradiction and kind disagreement between bases block; a Modelo 131 file from the `2024` revision onward whose DPA indicator disagrees with its page-1 marker blocks (amended 2026-11-02: replaced by the amendment's tests); a failed submitted-file parse yields `NONE`; an observation and a capture row stored before this change load and read as `NONE`; a stored revision replays to identical values and identity; a detector test proves removing the registry declaration returns the original-quarter case to unresolved.
- Hypotheses to confirm in the Step: whether the fixed-width codec already reads a zero-filled casilla 18 from a submitted file as zero (`modelo-130-page-01.toml:575-586`, `left_zero`), and the per-revision marker coverage of every Modelo 131 layout, every later revision included.
- Cross-domain dependency, coordination authorized (Operator rulings 2026-11-01). The new blocking findings and the extra fold outcomes reach the shared relation prefill and clean-state check, which other modelos' sessions own, and can change their goldens. Plan Step P01.S28 coordinates that before S16 lands, and the operator has authorized that coordination. This adds no product scope to S16.

## Rationale

The envelope is where Cadrumo already keeps a filed declaration's non-casilla facts with the provenance that established them, and the kind is evidence from the same artefact as the casilla values the fold reads, so it travels with them in one row and needs no join against a catalogue that may disagree about which filing is current. Reusing `FilingDeclarationKind` keeps one legal vocabulary across chain, observation and export. A closed evidence basis lets the system record what it saw without promoting it, so readings backed only by synthetic fixtures stay visible but fail closed. Declaring "complementaria-only" on the casilla puts the regulated fact in the registry where its grounding lives and keeps the fold generic. The submitted-file marker is the one basis whose meaning the official design fixes byte for byte; the local revision is the one basis Cadrumo itself produced and whose export renders from the same identity.

## Consequences

- Gains: an original Modelo 130 or 131 quarter captured from AEAT's submitted file, or filed locally, credits 0604 without operator input; complementarias and unknown kinds stay honestly unresolved; contradictions between kind evidence and casilla 18 become visible.
- Costs: a new optional envelope and capture field, a registry declaration and a shared predicate. No namespace bump, so no modelo's stored observations need re-capture. A quarter observed before this change keeps 0604 unresolved until it is re-captured from a submitted file or re-filed locally, because its absent projection reads as unknown.
- Remaining gap: the motivating case, a quarter whose submitted file is unavailable and is captured only from the declaration PDF, still leaves 0604 unresolved. No admitted basis exists for it until a real register `tipo_solicitud` specimen for a known original, or a declaration-PDF specimen showing the printed complementaria box, is observed in an attended live session.
- Reconsider if AEAT documents the register request-type vocabulary, if register completeness per period is established (admitting the sole-row basis), if the meaning of the Modelo 131 DPA position-12 indicator is settled, or if AEAT adopts the rectificativa for Modelo 130 or 131.

## Amendment 2026-11-02: the Modelo 131 DPA position-12 indicator is a page marker

Authorized by the operator on 2026-11-02 ("record b is a go"). The official record designs settle the meaning that Considerations left open. Every Modelo 131 design from the `2024` revision labels DPA position 12 "Indicador de página complementaria" (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/aeat_official/disenos_registro/modelo_131/files/06-131-ejercicios-2024-actualizado-13-12-24-180-kb-xlsx.xlsx.extracted.md:140`, and line 140 of the later designs). The page-01 position-12 indicator is fixed "En blanco" even on a complementaria declaration (`06-131-...:46`), whose kind is carried only by page-01 position 692, "Complementaria (7) - Declaración complementaria", `blanco o "X"` (`06-131-...:108`). The Modelo 303 design states the shared convention in its Nota 1: the page indicator is filled "cuando en el fichero van más de una página del mismo tipo" (`src/cadrumo/_data/corpus/aeat_official/disenos_registro/modelo_303/files/01-303-ejercicio-2026-y-siguientes-...xlsx.extracted.md:360-361`). Position 12 therefore marks a continuation page.

- The DPA position-12 indicator is neither an admitted basis nor an unadmitted reading of the filing kind. It never creates a disagreement. A blank or `C` there says nothing about whether the declaration is original or complementaria.
- For Modelo 131 from the `2024` revision the submitted-file kind comes from page-01 position 692 alone; the `2019-2023` revision keeps position 613.
- The `continuation_page_marker` rendering (blank on the first DPA occurrence, `C` on later ones) matches the design and is not an export defect.
- The misleading field id `modelo-131-2024-dpa-complementaria-indicator` and its later siblings are a registry naming matter for the registry session; renaming is a contract change and is not part of this amendment.
- Tests replacing the struck DPA-disagreement test: a Modelo 131 original from the `2024` revision onward with several activities (position 12 `C` on continuation occurrences, position 692 blank) reads ORIGINAL; the same file with 692 `X` reads COMPLEMENTARIA; neither raises a disagreement.
