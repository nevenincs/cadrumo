## Goal and initial scope

Prove that an autonomo can record an asset and its acquisition evidence through either frontend, determine its correct expense/capitalization treatment, inspect a legally grounded depreciation schedule, preserve its history across periods and years, and use the resulting deductions in Modelo 130 and annual Modelo 100. Where IVA applies, the same acquisition must feed the existing deduction and capital-goods regularization contracts without confusing them with income-tax depreciation.

This is backend-first work with CLI/TUI projections, not two independent frontend implementations of tax logic. Start with common-regime direct estimation, explicitly selecting normal or simplified for each scenario. Cover ordinary equipment and a purchased home with a separately usable business-office portion. Do not silently apply these rules to objective estimation, foral regimes, vehicles, inherited property, rental-income property, or special incentives without their applicable authority. Unsupported cases remain visible; expanding them requires assigned scope.

Resolve the latest completed tax year programmatically, then resolve actual authority support and capability for that year. On the research date the latest completed calendar year is 2025, not 2026. A missing filing-grade path is a reported gap, not permission to silently choose an older year. Allow an explicit year override, pin generation/revisions and source applicability, and derive scenario dates from the selection. Parameterized dates alone do not make tax rules year-independent.

## Official domain grounding

The following are researched rules for scoping and independent oracles, not a claim that every condition is implemented. Preserve provision, source year, effective date and taxpayer applicability when enrolling authority.

1. **Three different objects:** statutory depreciation tables, a generated schedule for one asset, and the recorded/claimed depreciation history are not interchangeable. AEAT requires relevant professionals/non-commercial businesses to record each asset's annual depreciation charge in the investment-goods book. An IVA regularization register does not by itself satisfy that IRPF requirement. [AEAT 2025 depreciation requirements](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/eu_es/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-1-determinacion-rendimiento-neto/amortizaciones-dotaciones-ejercicio-fiscalmente-deducibles/requisitos-generales.html).
2. **Tables are regime-sensitive, not automatically different for every region and year.** Common-regime direct simplified uses the specific linear table under RIRPF article 30 and the Order of 27 March 1998: buildings have a 3% maximum coefficient and 68-year maximum period. The normal table distinguishes commercial/administrative/service buildings and dwellings at 2%/100 years. These are legal limits, not an instruction to apply both maxima together. Territorial regimes, special conditions and genuine temporal changes need explicit selection; do not manufacture annual or regional copies of unchanged authority. [AEAT simplified table](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/eu_es/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-1-determinacion-rendimiento-neto/amortizaciones-dotaciones-ejercicio-fiscalmente-deducibles/especialidades-fiscales-amortizaciones-modalidad-simplificada.html), [AEAT normal linear table](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/eu_es/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-1-determinacion-rendimiento-neto/amortizaciones-dotaciones-ejercicio-fiscalmente-deducibles/requisitos-generales/coeficientes-amortizacion-lineal.html).
3. **Deductibility is not a binary expense-versus-asset flag.** Ordinary depreciation requires the appropriate depreciable base and effective charge. Separately, qualifying new tangible assets with unit value no greater than EUR 300 can use free depreciation subject to the EUR 25,000 tax-period limit and its conditions. Immediate lawful depreciation does not erase the asset or authorize another deduction of the same cost. Do not apply this threshold to every regime or confuse it with IVA's threshold. [AEAT IRPF free depreciation, LIS article 12.3](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/eu_es/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-1-determinacion-rendimiento-neto/amortizaciones-dotaciones-ejercicio-fiscalmente-deducibles/supuestos-libertad-amortizacion/libertad-amortizacion-supuestos-contemplados-articulo-12_3.html).
4. **Acquisition is not necessarily the depreciation start.** The base includes applicable acquisition/production costs and qualifying readiness costs, excludes residual value where applicable, and depreciation starts when the asset is ready for operation. Used assets have distinct conditions. Preserve acquisition, business-affectation and in-service dates separately when the rules require them. [AEAT depreciation rules](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/eu_es/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c07-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-estimacion-directa/fase-1-determinacion-rendimiento-neto/amortizaciones-dotaciones-ejercicio-fiscalmente-deducibles/reglas-amortizacion.html).
5. **A home office requires more than a generic percentage.** IRPF partial business affectation concerns a separately and independently usable part of a divisible asset. Home ownership expenses, including depreciation, use the relevant business portion and ownership treatment; common spousal property has special rules. Do not blindly halve such property or apply the household-utilities percentage to depreciation. [AEAT affectation criteria, LIRPF article 29/RIRPF article 22](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/eu_es/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c06-rendimientos-actividades-economicas-cuestiones-generales/elementos-patrimoniales-afectos-actividad-economica/criterios-afectacion-bienes-derechos-ejercicio.html).
6. **Land and construction differ.** Building depreciation excludes the land component. RIS article 3 provides a cadastral allocation when the land value is unknown, with an evidence-based market-value alternative. A residential rental-income calculation is a separate tax treatment, not the office-asset formula simply because both mention 3%. [BOE RD 634/2015, article 3](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-7771), [AEAT rental-income depreciation](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c04-rendimientos-capital-inmobiliario/gastos-deducibles/cantidades-destinadas-amortizacion.html).
7. **IVA is a separate dimension.** IVA investment-goods classification normally concerns durable productive assets and excludes goods below EUR 3,005.06, among other exclusions. That threshold is not a universal IRPF immediate-expense allowance. IVA deductibility requires its own eligibility/use evidence; a home-office VAT allocation is not automatically the same as every IRPF allocation or an activity's prorrata. A property purchase may not bear deductible IVA at all. [AEAT IVA capital-goods definition](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/eu_es/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/manual-iva-2025/capitulo-05-deducciones-devoluciones/deducciones/regularizacion-deducciones-bienes-inversion/concepto-bienes-inversion.html), [AEAT IVA business-use requirements](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/eu_es/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/manual-iva-2025/capitulo-05-deducciones-devoluciones/deducciones/requisitos-deduccion/iva-soportado-adq-bienes-servic-profes.html).
8. **IVA regularization is not amortization.** AEAT describes four subsequent calendar years, or nine for land/buildings, measured from acquisition or later first use/operation, and a greater-than-ten-point deduction-percentage condition for the described annual regularization. Audit use changes and disposals under their own provisions rather than generalizing one threshold. Existing IVA code must be reused and tested against these conditions, including delayed first use. [AEAT IVA regularization](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/eu_es/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/manual-iva-2025/capitulo-05-deducciones-devoluciones/deducciones/regularizacion-deducciones-bienes-inversion.html).
9. **Cost and deductible VAT must reconcile.** PGC NRV 12 includes non-deductible input VAT in acquisition cost, but later prorrata/capital-goods regularizations do not change the initial valuation under that rule. Keep recoverable VAT out of depreciable acquisition cost; do not retrospectively rewrite the schedule's cost each time IVA regularizes. Establish the applicable cost treatment for each scenario. [BOE PGC, NRV 12](https://www.boe.es/buscar/pdf/2007/BOE-A-2007-19884-consolidado.pdf).
10. **Modelo 130 uses cumulative depreciation.** Its expense casilla includes deductible depreciation from the start of the year through the quarter end. Annual M100 needs the annual economic-activity result, not the sum of four cumulative M130 expense totals. The independent oracle must distinguish quarterly increments, year-to-date charges and annual totals. [AEAT Modelo 130 instructions, section I/casilla 02](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/impuestos-tasas/impuesto-sobre-renta-personas-fisicas/modelo-130-irpf______esionales-estimacion-directa-fraccionado_/instrucciones.html).

Reproduce whether an ordinary full-cost purchase is accepted as a complete deductible expense without rate/history validation. `full_deductible` by itself is not proof of erroneous full-cost expensing.

- Audit empty-register zero handling against positive ledger evidence of an investment acquisition. No assets declared, no assets owned, missing register, failed register load and fully depreciated assets are distinct states.

Resolve building/land and non-IVA asset needs without treating the IVA record as universal or merging tax-specific registers by fiat.
