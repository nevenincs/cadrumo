## Goal and scope

Prove that a synthetic autonomo's declared circumstances derive the correct withholding obligations, that actual paid financial activity and invoice/payroll/rental evidence are recorded through supported CLI and TUI flows, and that persisted records drive correct periodic returns and complete annual recipient-detail exports. Prove fresh-session persistence, frontend parity, and continuation both ways.

Primary chains are professional/staff payments 111 -> 190 and applicable business rent 115 -> 180. Include the conditional capital-income chain 123 -> 193 because the existing obligation and aggregation paths cover it; report that branch separately rather than claiming all possible capital-income treatments. Initial scenarios target resident recipients and a common-regime autonomo. Non-resident 216/296 and other withholding families are routing/applicability boundaries: identify unsupported cases and never force them into a resident scheme. A new family implementation requires separately assigned scope.

Withholding suffered on the autonomo's issued sales invoices is a different direction: it is a credit in the income-tax flow, not automatically an obligation to pay that same withholding through the autonomo's own 111. Reuse the INCOME-01 direction/provenance boundary. Invoice VAT remains governed by IVA-01; do not net withholding into taxable bases, VAT, or business income/expense without the established treatment.

Use the latest complete year with explicit supported-year diagnostics, pinned authority/revisions, and a year override. Annual key/subkey catalogues and export layouts are year-specific. The runner must not hide an unsupported latest-year annual export by silently selecting an older year or reuse one year's oracle across changed rules.

## Domain grounding before implementation

1. **Obligation follows relevant payments and circumstances.** AEAT identifies business/professional individuals paying income in their activity as withholding agents when that income is subject to withholding. Being autonomo alone does not activate every return. Profile declarations, recipient status, applicable exceptions, and financial evidence must agree. [Who must withhold](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/irpf/retenciones-ingresos-cuenta-pagos-fraccionados/retenciones-ingresos-cuenta/personas-entidades-obligadas-retener-efectuar-cuenta.html).
2. **Time matters.** AEAT describes IRPF withholding as arising when income is paid; its IS explanation uses a different exigibility/payment rule. An invoice date is therefore not a universal withholding-period oracle. Fix recipient tax status and the applicable rule for each scenario, and test invoice/payment dates crossing periods/years. [Timing and withholding duties](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/folleto-actividades-economicas/7-otras-obligaciones-fiscales-retenciones.html).
3. **Periodic and annual duties differ.** The core pairings are 111/190 and 115/180. AEAT also requires withholding certificates and communication to recipients. Report certificate/data availability as part of obligation coverage; do not claim full withholding-agent compliance from an exported tax file alone. [Withholding-agent obligations](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/irpf/retenciones-ingresos-cuenta-pagos-fraccionados/retenciones-ingresos-cuenta/obligaciones-retenedor.html).
4. **Zero is not absence.** Modelo 111 distinguishes relevant income paid with no effective withholding due from a period in which no relevant income was paid. Missing evidence is neither of those states. Exercise each separately and ground applicability/negative-return behavior in the selected year. [Modelo 111 instructions](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/pagos-cuenta/modelo-111-reten_____moniales-imputaciones-renta-autoliquidacion_/instrucciones.html).
5. **Rent has exceptions and property detail.** Do not activate 115 from any rent-labelled payment indiscriminately. Annual 180 needs property identity and can require multiple records for the same landlord across properties/contracts; a simple distinct-NIF count is not automatically the exported-record count. [Rent withholding](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/folleto-actividades-economicas/7-otras-obligaciones-fiscales-retenciones/7_3-retenciones-arrendamiento-bienes-inmuebles-180.html), [Modelo 180 property/record guidance](https://www3.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/impuestos-tasas/declaraciones-informativas/modelo-180-decla_____arrendamiento-inmuebles-urbanos-anual_/preguntas-frecuentes.html).
6. **Annual detail is substantive.** Modelo 190 uses recipient records with identity and clave/subclave plus applicable monetary/personal fields. Validate actual record grouping and control totals, not just quarterly sums. The 2025 layout is research evidence, not a permanently fixed runner year. [Official 2025 Modelo 190 record design](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/static_files/Sede/Disenyo_registro/DR_100_199/archivos_25/DISENOS_LOGICOS_190_2025.pdf).
7. **Conditional families stay distinct.** Applicable capital-income payments use 123/193; non-resident withholding has separate 216/296 surfaces and treatment. Do not select a family solely because an invoice has a withholding percentage. [Capital-income guidance](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/folleto-actividades-economicas/7-otras-obligaciones-fiscales-retenciones/7_4-retenciones-capital-mobiliario-modelos-193.html), [Modelo 216](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/no-residentes/irnr-sin-establecimiento-permanente/retenciones-irnr-sin-establecimiento-permanente/modelo-216.html), [Modelo 296](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/procedimientoini/GI22.shtml).

These sources establish the domain questions. Before computing a year/regime-specific oracle, ground actual rates, bases, exceptions, record keys and temporal rules in the corresponding official revision. Do not derive expected values by calling the production calculation or invent a payroll-rate engine for this acceptance campaign. Payroll fixtures may supply independently grounded payroll facts, labelled as such; they must not be disguised as supplier invoices.

## Required source-to-return mapping

For every observation show: payer/recipient role -> obligation/profile evidence -> payment and source document -> canonical transaction/invoice or payroll/rental record -> persisted withholding observation/detail -> periodic source binding/casilla -> annual grouped record -> exported bytes and validation. Retain source identity and demonstrate corrections invalidate downstream results appropriately.

One income/payment must not become two withholding liabilities because it appears in both a ledger transaction and an invoice/manual observation. Conversely, a scalar invoice withholding amount must not invent recipient identity, legal scheme, clave/subclave, property data, or payroll attributes. If separate stores are required by existing contracts, prove their coherence and explain exactly how a supported user flow produces each.

## Current code map and first risks

Paths below are relative to src/cadrumo/ unless otherwise marked. Static observations are not runtime proof.

- Profile: domain/user_profile/setup_answers.py contains employee/professional/rent/capital flags and explicit no-retencion periods. Applicability payer facts and registry conditions derive 111/190, 115/180 and 123/193; inspect current applicability_payer_facts.py and tests/test_applicability*.py. Incomplete facts must not be conflated with false.
- Periodic/annual totals: application/aggregation/retenciones.py defines RetencionObservation (source, recipient NIF/name, scheme, base, withheld amount, accrued_on); modelo_bindings_retenciones.py dispatches 111/115/123/180/190/193.
- Persistence: application/aggregation/retencion_observations_repository.py defines the encrypted-adapter contract for exact modelo/year/period observation windows. adapters/persistence/profile/retencion_observations.py implements FINANCIAL-classified encrypted envelopes and atomic window replacement. Whole-window replacement still makes safe incremental input and replay a load-bearing acceptance requirement; atomicity of one store does not establish coherence across both stores.
- Invoice liability: application/aggregation/invoice_retencion.py projects received invoices into that observation set with an explicit caller-declared scheme. Issued invoice withholding is excluded from payer liability. Parent source check found accrued_on assigned from invoice.issued_at; investigate invoice-versus-payment timing with a grounded boundary test before assuming this producer proves period correctness.
- Public producer: entrypoints/cli/_modelo_aggregate_cli.py and _modelo_nonwork_calculations_command_specs.py expose --retencion-observation, --received-invoice-retencion, and Modelo 190 --withholding-observation. The command merges invoice/manual totals before one replacement write; richer annual details are persisted separately. Check omitted arguments and partial-failure behavior, not only successful first creation.
- Annual Modelo 190 detail: application/aggregation/withholding_source.py reads a separate recipient/clave/subclave substrate defined in domain/calculations/registry/withholding_bindings.py and application/aggregation/percepciones_observations_repository.py. Invoice-derived totals do not automatically populate it. Empty detail can yield zero with an advisory; establish whether verification/export then correctly prevent an incomplete declared success.
- Annual carry-up: annual aggregators accept an annual period and supplied observations. Exact-key repository reads alone do not demonstrate automatic collection of quarterly windows into annual detail. Require a real supported user workflow without retyping totals or bypassing source lineage.
- TUI: the inspected ledger injection exposes scalar invoice retention fields but no dedicated withholding-observation/recipient-detail producer. Reuse the Modelo action gap inventory from the other briefs; do not reduce acceptance to read-only parity. Existing contract coverage versus a new architecture need must be determined by the principal/adviser before changes.
- 216/296: types/registry references are not operational support. The inspected retenciones dispatcher excludes these modelos, and producer_snapshot.py documents an unconnected M296 row source. Keep non-resident refusal/routing evidence separate from supported resident cases.

Focused candidates, not a batch command: application/aggregation/tests/test_invoice_retencion_routing.py, test_retenciones_aggregation_resolver.py, test_withholding_source_resolver.py, test_retenciones_empty_store_advisory_guard.py; CLI tests/test_withholding_producer.py, test_invoice_retencion_aggregate_cli.py, test_modelo_190_clave_breakdown.py; adapters/persistence/profile/tests/test_retencion_observations_repository_roundtrip.py, test_modelo_190_percepciones_e2e.py, test_modelo_190_111_reconciliation_continuity.py, test_modelo_180_115_reconciliation_continuity.py, test_invoice_retencion_routing_store.py.
