## Scope and legal distinctions

The selected entity, taxpayer kind and applicable period determine obligations; a user-facing filter only changes the view. It must not silently change legal applicability. Start with the supported autonomo profile and a small contrast set of other supported entity/attribution and monthly/quarterly profiles. Report unsupported classes or incomplete facts explicitly instead of returning a reassuring empty calendar. Do not implement new tax families merely to populate the calendar.

Use an explicit evaluation date and the governing timezone, with a bounded date range and pinned authority. Calendar year, return tax year/period, source-revision year, profile effective dates, actual filing time and evidence capture time are different coordinates. A previous year's annual return may be due in the current calendar year. Preserve that identity throughout CLI, TUI and reconciliation.

Official grounding:

1. **Frequency depends on the taxpayer and tax.** AEAT distinguishes annual, quarterly and monthly obligations. Entity kind alone does not settle all regimes/exemptions. [AEAT 2026 obligation frequency](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/calendario-contribuyente/calendario-contribuyente-2026/obligatoriedad-presentar-autoliquidaciones.html).
2. **There is no universal date-shift rule.** Filing and direct-debit cutoffs are distinct; holiday treatment has specific exceptions, including Modelo 369. Some corporate deadlines are relative to the end of the entity's tax period. Pin the relevant published dates/rules, their jurisdiction and effective scope; do not blindly shift a date already adjusted by authority. [AEAT holiday rules](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/calendario-contribuyente/calendario-contribuyente-2026/recuerde/vencimientos-dias-inhabiles-sabados-festivos.html), [direct-debit deadlines](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/contenidos-lectura-facil/calendario-contribuyente-2026-simplificado-personas-fisicas/plazos-presentacion-autoliquidaciones-domiciliacion-bancaria.html), [period-relative corporate deadlines](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/calendario-contribuyente/calendario-contribuyente-2026/informacion-sobre-presentacion-modelos-no-periodicos/impuesto-sobre-sociedades.html).
3. **Cessation is not a blanket erasure of obligations.** AEAT distinguishes individual cessation, partial activity cessation and legally unextinguished inactive entities; applicable corporate filing obligations can continue. Annual IVA exemption rules also depend on whether the final-period filing obligation exists. Test residual obligations due after cessation using the relevant period's law. [AEAT cessation guidance](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/censos-nif-domicilio-fiscal/tramites-censales-relacionados-empresarios-profesionales-retenedores/preguntas-frecuentes-modelos-036-037/modificaciones-censales/dejar-ejercer-todas-actividades-empresariales-profesionales.html), [official 2025 Modelo 390 instructions](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/static_files/Sede/Procedimiento_ayuda/G412/Instrucciones_modelo_390-2025.pdf).
4. **Payment proof and presentation proof differ.** An NRC identifies a tax payment; obtaining it does not itself complete the declaration's presentation. AEAT's filed-declaration consultation separately provides declaration copies/files and presentation justificantes. Match the actual evidence kind and coordinates rather than treating any PDF or reference code as a filed return. [AEAT NRC definition](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/consultas-informaticas/pago-impuestos-deudas-tasas-ayuda-tecnica/que-nrc.html), [payment followed by presentation](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/consultas-informaticas/pago-impuestos-deudas-tasas-ayuda-tecnica/pago-autoliquidaciones-tarjeta.html), [filed-declaration consultation](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/consultas-informaticas/otros-servicios-ayuda-tecnica/consulta-declaraciones-presentadas.html).
5. **An overdue flag is not a penalty assessment.** Article 27 addresses qualifying voluntary late presentation without prior administrative requirement: its rate increases with completed months, and after the specified twelve-month boundary different surcharge/interest treatment applies. Eligibility, exceptions and reductions matter. Sanctions, collection-period charges and interest are separate categories; a zero/refund/informative declaration does not automatically follow the same monetary rule. Do not equate display weeks with statutory completed months. [AEAT Article 27 explanation](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c18-cuota-liquida-resultante-autoliquidacion/regularizacion-situaciones-tributarias/recargos-aplicables.html), [LGT articles 26–28, 191 and 198](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2003-23186).
6. **Notification access can be legally consequential.** AEAT distinguishes communications/notifications, their issue/service/read states and represented-person access. Access to notification contents can establish service; elapsed availability also has legal consequences under the applicable rules. Listing metadata, opening content, acknowledging and responding are different permissions. Do not derive a response deadline from issue date alone or interpret a notification template's “modelo” as a tax-return Modelo. [AEAT consultation metadata](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/consultas-informaticas/notificaciones-electronicas-ayuda-tecnica/consulta-notificaciones-comunicaciones.html), [AEAT effects of electronic notification](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/otros-servicios/notificaciones/notificaciones/preguntas-frecuentes.html).

- **Unadjusted status versus adjusted display:** the engine classifies using `closes_on`; calendar stores `adjusted_closes_on` but retains the original status/recovery; agenda buckets use the adjusted date. - **Jurisdiction and missing authority:** overview passes `ccaa_code=None` to the holiday shift. Determine the correct jurisdiction for each deadline kind; do not indiscriminately reuse residence for every cutoff. - **Historical evaluation:** overview passes an evaluation date to the engine but re-derives applicability without it; some special-regime resolution may use today's Madrid date. ## Current CLI/TUI usability and required outcome

Existing CLI surfaces:

- `aeat app overview calendar --from DATE --to DATE`, with `--allow-incomplete`, `--show-suppressed`, `--all-profiles` and language options.
- `aeat app overview agenda --date DATE --horizon DAYS` and `aeat app overview backlog --from DATE --to DATE`.
- `aeat app overview explain MODELO --year YEAR` for applicability/legal/profile explanation.

These are existing command shapes, not commands run by this preflight. Specs: `entrypoints/cli/_overview_command_specs.py:107`; rendering: `_overview_rendering.py:620`, `:652`; typed payloads: `_overview_payloads.py:364`. Agenda exposes next/due-today/due-soon; backlog reports late count and oldest close first. Neither inspected calendar presentation supplies a ready-to-read overdue age. CLI explanation currently addresses applicability, not a direct filing-evidence/reconciliation drilldown.

TUI already has `declarations.calendar`, reached from Home agenda with modelo/year/period focus. It supports search and `all`, `past`, `upcoming`, `overdue`, `filed`, `evidence_unknown` scopes. Columns show close date, declaration, legal status, local state and AEAT state. Details show opening/payment/closing dates, justificante state and source availability/observation time. Sources: `entrypoints/tui/declarations/calendar.py:90`, `:122`, `:215`; `controller.py:430`; `models.py:30`.

The inspected TUI lacks explicit entity/regime selectors within the calendar, evaluation-date/overdue-age display, and direct calendar-to-reconciliation/notifications drilldown. Do not confuse source `observed_at` with the date used to decide overdue status. Filing History and AEAT Sync already have related views; link/reuse them rather than recreate their content. Home intentionally shows at most three chronological agenda rows: keep it compact but make the full list and omitted backlog discoverable.

Acceptance is task-based, not “a table rendered.” With one selected entity and a visible evaluation date, users must be able to answer:

| Question | Required information/action |
| --- | --- |
| What must I file next, and why? | Modelo and tax period; profile/applicability reason; next effective deadline; incomplete coverage warning; link to explanation/preparation. |
| When exactly, and can I still use direct debit? | Filing close and distinct applicable payment/direct-debit cutoff, date-shift reason and authority. A missed debit cutoff is not automatically a missed filing deadline. |
| What is overdue according to our records? | Effective due date, exact days overdue plus readable duration, evidence status and freshness, and whether absence is local-only or a scoped completed AEAT lookup. Never say “confirmed not filed” from an empty local store. |
| What was actually filed? | Local preparation separate from observed/accepted AEAT submission, matched justificante grade and actual submission timestamp. Filing confirmation does not automatically prove payment. |
| How do I resolve a gap or conflict? | Open existing work/history, inspect/import appropriate evidence, reconcile through the existing owner, or see why live refresh is blocked. Do not silently overwrite local or official history. |
| Is this message about this return? | Evidence-backed tax identity/period/expediente association or explicit unlinked/ambiguous status; accessible metadata; no unsolicited content opening/acknowledgement. |

CLI JSON must retain typed status, dates, evaluation context, provenance and actionable notices; human output must not require interpreting internal enums or colors alone. TUI keyboard search/scope/table navigation, focus restoration, locale coverage and narrow-terminal behavior have existing tests. Extend them at the owning boundary; do not infer rendered usability from their presence.
