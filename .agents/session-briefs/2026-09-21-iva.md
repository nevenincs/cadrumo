## Goal

Prove that issued and received invoices representing distinct business operations can be entered through CLI and TUI, persist with sufficient tax meaning, feed the local IVA ledger and appropriate periodic/annual calculations, and produce valid official-format exports. Explain and verify separately how existing AEAT-held IVA information is retrieved, stored, interpreted, and reconciled with local evidence.

Working modelo scope is periodic Modelo 303 and annual Modelo 390 where applicable, including the alternative annual-information obligations when 390 is not applicable. Discover supported periods and actual applicability from canonical authority/profile contracts. Other IVA returns are inventoried only where relevant dependencies arise; do not silently expand into implementing every VAT regime/modelo.

Carry forward the user's latest-year and programmatic-workflow preference from INCOME-01. Resolve the latest complete year with explicit support diagnostics, pin authority/revisions, and allow a year override. Use independent year-specific expectations and small resolver/parameterization checks; do not multiply full end-to-end runs across historical years.

User clarification: "IVA ledger" means the running settlement position derived from filed IVA returns: payable outcomes, credit generated and applied, amounts remaining for compensation, and refund outcomes. It does not mean SII invoice books. SII discovery is a completed ancillary finding, not the target of the balance workflow. No real taxpayer access or AEAT write/submission is authorized by this brief.

## Filed IVA balance and AEAT reconciliation

Use "IVA settlement position" as the brief's descriptive umbrella, not a claim that AEAT exposes one unified account. AEAT names its carry-forward-credit service "Cartera de cuotas a compensar". This is narrower than all payable debts, actual payments, and refunds. Official terminology reference: https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/pre-303/preguntas-frecuentes/cuestiones-especificas-sobre-servicio-pre303_.html . Use the selected year's Modelo 303 instructions for settlement rules; the current explanatory reference is https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/todas-gestiones/impuestos-tasas/iva/modelo-303-iva-autoliquidacion_/instrucciones-2026.html .

Trace each period's calculated output/input VAT into the filed settlement outcome, then into subsequent-period compensation and annual/cross-year continuity. Display and verify separately: payable declared; payment evidenced or unknown; credit generated; prior credit applied; credit remaining; refund requested; and any independently evidenced refund approval/payment. Do not infer payment from a filed positive return, infer refund receipt from a negative return, or combine requested refunds with credit still available for compensation.

The existing local compensation model and AEAT wallet are a concrete starting point, not proof that the entire settlement position is represented. The wallet observation schema exposes available amounts by generation period; it documents that original generated/applied amounts are unavailable from this captured surface. Reconstructing movement history needs filed observations and their provenance, not an invented breakdown of a wallet snapshot.

Required directed balance scenarios: opening credit from an earlier period/year; new credit; partial/full credit use against a later payable outcome; remaining payable; refund election where applicable; amendment/supersession; missing intermediate filing; stale/conflicting AEAT balance; and repeated retrieval without duplicate credit. Derive expected movements independently under the selected year's rules. Treat missing payment/refund status as unknown and report unsupported visibility explicitly. New payment/refund integrations require a separate scoped decision.

## Required data-to-calculation map

For each scenario document: user-entered field -> canonical persisted field -> ledger observation/classification -> aggregation binding -> modelo/revision/casilla -> exported field. Show which facts are user-entered, inferred with evidence, imported, or calculated, and preserve that provenance.

Keep these dimensions distinct: issued/received invoice; goods/services or other supported operation; party country/territory versus actual tax treatment/place-of-supply evidence; domestic/intra-community/third-country or other admitted classification; taxpayer regime versus transaction regime; invoice-line VAT base/rate/amount; recargo de equivalencia base/rate/amount; exemption/non-subjection/reverse-charge reason where applicable; gross input VAT versus deductible VAT and supporting eligibility; invoice/accrual/booking/payment dates; correction and original-document linkage. Do not confuse recargo de equivalencia with late-filing surcharges.

For supported cases, preserve different rates and treatments within one invoice. Missing tax treatment is not the same as zero-rated, exempt, or outside scope. Foreign currency, territory, and non-IVA taxes must not silently become domestic IVA. Determine which cases are supported, rejected, or inaccessible through the UI before deciding implementation scope. No blanket assumption that every listed dimension applies to every taxpayer or operation.

## Initial source map and evidence limits

- application/aggregation/modelo_bindings.py explicitly describes domestic Modelo 303 IVA as ledger-owned and invoice checks as supporting evidence. This differs from assuming that every persisted invoice automatically supplies tax amounts. Inspect the actual ingestion/classification/linking bridge.
- application/aggregation/iva_ledger.py contains IVA observations and annual deductible-total/prorrata handling; modelo_bindings.py maps these to revision bindings and reports unconsumed quantities.
- application/modelo/tests/test_verification_m390_reconciliation.py contains annual devengada/deducible versus periodic reconciliation predicates; presence of tests is not end-to-end proof.
- application/filing/tests/test_modelo_303_exonerado_390_refusal.py and test_m303_exonerado_390_evidence_projection.py identify an explicit annual-applicability boundary to exercise when relevant.
- Previously mapped TUI Modelo workspace is predominantly read-only. Reuse the existing action/composition gap inventory instead of repeating discovery; establish which contracts permit the required IVA actions.
- Canonical invoices: domain/invoices/models.py supports lines, category, operation type/date, recargo and rectification metadata. Transactions in domain/transactions/models.py hold the IVA accounting classification; supporting links include invoice_id and purchase_invoice_evidence_id. Map each business flow to its actual link type instead of assuming one universal invoice link.
- TUI entry gap: entrypoints/tui/ledger/invoice_entry.py and ledger_doors.py accept one base/rate pair and omit the explicit category, operation type/date, recargo and rectification-target arguments exposed by the richer CLI invoice path. The canonical model's multi-line capacity does not prove mixed-rate user entry. Validate imports separately before declaring the entire frontend incapable.
- CLI: entrypoints/cli/_ledger_business_invoice_cli.py exposes category, operation type/date and recargo options. Inspect structured readback as well as text output; representation gaps can hide correctly persisted fields from programmatic clients.
- AEAT lifecycle: application/live/iva_remote_state.py separates filed-303 history from wallet reads. adapters/outbound/aeat/sede/iva_compensation_wallet.py is a read-only authenticated Pre303 wallet reader, not a full remote invoice ledger.
- AEAT storage: adapters/outbound/aeat/sede/observation_store.py stores financial observations and artefacts through the active bucket's encrypted secure-object boundary. Source/capture identities and integrity checks are distinct from locally calculated values.
- Local compensation: domain/iva_compensation/carry_forward.py, balance.py and reconciliation.py reconstruct local period balances and compare them with wallet evidence, including identity, freshness, disagreement, and casilla-110 decision handling. Test the selection policy rather than assuming AEAT values always override local state.
- SII capability result: adapters/inbound/einvoice/shape.py recognizes SII envelope/record families, including SOAP-wrapped batches. core/document_shape.py excludes XML_AEAT_SII from single-invoice structured shapes; adapters/inbound/einvoice/parsers.py refuses it. entrypoints/ledger_evidence_extraction_composition.py routes through that single-invoice parser. tests/test_aeat_batch_shape_refusal.py explicitly covers detection plus rejection. The bounded adapter/caller inspection found no SII record decoder/importer or live SII books/API synchronization. Format recognition and enrolment flags are not supported SII ingestion. Report this as an existing capability boundary; a new batch importer or network integration needs a separate user-directed scope decision.
